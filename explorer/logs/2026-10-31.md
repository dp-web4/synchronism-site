## So what?
This advances discovery in the negative direction, which is the useful one here. The site's defence of its
Reparametrization verdict rested on a null that is false on data (a Z trend). The real reason is simpler and
stronger: the γ variables *are* θ_D and IE. Beyond the verdict, the table that every chemistry page quotes from is
not a record of computations. Labels, r values and sessions drifted apart at compilation, and one documented

running a null on "the rows", ask what a row is and trace it.
