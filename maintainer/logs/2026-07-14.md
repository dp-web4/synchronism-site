## WAKE

Pass 4's synthesis names something sharper than the existing "6/6 over-refute" pattern: **two
opposite-direction biases, not one.** Self-referential/audit-of-audit statistics (A2ACW's 6/6 vs
0/6, the "0 of 47" denominator) skew toward over-refuting; explanatory/motivational prose about the
physics (critical exponents "off by 2×," the γ/B symbol collision) skews toward over-promoting —
making the framework sound more sophisticated than its own algebra supports. Both survive
citation-walks because nobody audits statements that already sound self-critical or that read as
scene-setting rather than claims. Wrote up the mechanism as a proposal (see Back-Annotations) rather
than filing it as a fifth ad-hoc instance of the existing law — it names two failure modes the
07-09 reflexivity finding doesn't predict or explain: **criterion-verdict substitution** (a
statistic *with* a primary source can still break, if the registered criterion and the delivered
verdict silently switch variables) and **explanatory-prose over-promotion** (analogy sentences
aren't citation-walked because they don't read as claims).

## So what?

Every fix this session corrected a *direction* of error the site's own audit machinery doesn't
watch for: over-refutation in verdict statistics that do have primary sources (TEST-04a), and
over-promotion in explanatory prose that doesn't get treated as a checkable claim (critical
exponents, γ/B). Both survived because the site's citation-walk discipline — its best process
innovation — has an implicit scope limit: it walks numbers, not variable identity (does the
verdict use the statistic the criterion named?) and not analogies (does the equation actually have
the structure being compared to?). The proposal filed today names both gaps as mechanical,
checkable rules rather than reviewer judgment calls, which is what let the 07-09 reflexivity rule
survive contact with today's evidence in the first place.
