================================================================================
(2) WHY IT ESCAPES THE SIZE IDENTITY
================================================================================
  For a virialised system rho/g = 3/(4 pi G r) exactly -- mass cancels and
  the only lever is size.  A disk column at fixed R is NOT such a system:
  it is supported in z by pressure against a field set mostly at another
  scale (the radial one).  So rho and |g| are genuinely decoupled here,
  and the decoupling is not bought by making an object physically bigger.

  system                                  rho/|g| at z=0    at z=2kpc   ratio
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  solar column (this calc)                     1.267e-11    6.314e-14  0.0050
  equivalent virial radius at z=0        :   9148.5 pc
  equivalent virial radius at z=2 kpc    : 1836105.0 pc
  -> the column 'looks like' an object 201x larger at z=2 kpc without
     anything getting larger.  That is the loophole, and it is free.

================================================================================
(3) PREDICTED K_z SPLIT, as a function of beta
================================================================================
  Hybrid: x = (|g|/a0)*(rho/rho_ref)^beta.  Working observable is the
  vertical force K_z(z) = |g_z| including the coherence boost:
     K_z^pred(z) = K_z^bar(z) / C(x(z))
  Both keyings agree at z=0 by construction (a0/rho_ref absorb it); the
  SHAPE in z is the discriminant.

================================================================================
(4) WHAT THE DATA CAN ACTUALLY SEE
================================================================================
  Bovy & Rix (2013): Sigma(|z|<1.1 kpc) measured to ~6% per mono-abundance
  population, ~3% combined.  Gaia DR3 vertical Jeans analyses reach 2-5%
  on K_z out to |z| ~ 2 kpc.  Take 3% as the achievable systematic floor.

  beta detectable at 3% (Gaia DR3 systematic floor)        : beta > 0.0104
  beta detectable at 6% (single mono-abundance population) : beta > 0.0203
  beta detectable at 10% (dSph dispersion, for comparison) : beta > 0.0327

================================================================================
(5) THE HONEST CAVEAT
================================================================================
  This is a Newtonian sech^2 column used to compute a LEVER, not a
  self-consistent solution of the modified vertical Jeans equation.  A
  real test must re-solve hydrostatic equilibrium with the boost included,
  because changing K_z changes the scale height that sets rho(z).  The
  back-reaction is second order in the boost and will REDUCE the split
  somewhat.  Quoted numbers are therefore upper bounds on the signal --
  which is the direction that matters, since the conclusion is a ceiling.
