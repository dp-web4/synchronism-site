# Topic: EFE Divergence Computation

## Question

Can MOND+EFE's σ_int(environment) prediction be computed and compared to the Synchronism coherence ansatz numerically? The "EFE gap" has been invoked as an unquantified blocker on every environment-dependence test (TEST-01, TEST-02, TEST-05) for months. With the RAR shape test now closed (compander collapses to MOND at fitted γ), this computation is the **only remaining path** to a discriminating test — or to formally closing the question.

## Context

MOND's External Field Effect (Bekenstein-Milgrom 1984; AQUAL/QUMOND) gives a specific, computable prediction for how galaxy dynamics vary with local environmental density. Synchronism's coherence ansatz also predicts environment dependence via σ_int(ρ_env). For TEST-01/05 to discriminate the two frameworks, the Synchronism prediction must differ numerically from the MOND+EFE prediction at some measurable parameter value.

Two pieces of context from the explorer (2026-05-13):
- The MOND+EFE prediction for TEST-01/05 was estimated to be ~120× below SPARC reach at current data quality
- Both frameworks may be degenerate in *direction* (both predict density-dependent scatter)

Now that the compander's transition function is confirmed MOND-equivalent at fitted γ, the question sharpens: does the compander (with γ free) diverge from MOND+EFE in the *amplitude* of the σ_int(ρ_env) signal? Or is it completely degenerate?

## Why It Matters

- If computable divergence exists at some parameter: TEST-01/05 become live discriminators
- If no divergence: formally close TEST-01/05 as MOND-degenerate and clean up the Tier 1 count
- Either outcome is more useful than the current "possibly degenerate" status
