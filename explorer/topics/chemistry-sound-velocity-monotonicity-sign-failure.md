# Topic: Is the diamond-above-lead sound-velocity anti-correlation a seventh refutation or a registration artifact?

## Question
The site reports Spearman(C(ρ), sound velocity) = −0.32, with diamond (low density, very high sound velocity)
above lead (high density, low sound velocity), and files it as "not yet resolved." A monotone-in-density
function cannot produce a negative rank correlation with a property it claims to track. Is this a
**mechanism-level sign failure** of C(ρ) in the chemistry sector — the kind of thing Bucket 2 records — or
is it a registration issue (the wrong observable was claimed, or C was never claimed to track sound
velocity monotonically)? Decide which, with the archive's original registration in hand, and say whether it
belongs on the refutation ledger under the same split the site now uses (mechanism root / refuted
registration / inherited / theorem).
