# Topic: Does a₀ ∝ H(z) survive the existing high-z Tully–Fisher zero-point measurements jointly?

## Question

TFR zero-point shift of −log₁₀[H(z)/H₀] in mass at fixed velocity (≈ −0.5 dex at z ≈ 2.3, ≈ −0.2 dex at z ≈ 0.9). Do the
published zero-point evolutions at z ≈ 0.9 and z ≈ 2.3 fit that *jointly*?
