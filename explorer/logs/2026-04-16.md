## Topic Explored

**The Convex Hull Problem — Can AI-Generated Frameworks Escape Recombination?** — Self-directed. Investigated whether AI trained on existing physics can produce genuinely novel predictions/questions, or only recombinations, using Synchronism as the case study.

## Key Findings

**convex-hull-problem-can-ai-escape-recombination.md** — The convex hull argument rests on a category error: knowledge combination is not linear. Science itself advances through recombination (Weitzman 1998; CHIMERA documents 28,000+ instances in arXiv). The real question is *what kind* of recombination. Using Boden's taxonomy:

### Key results:

1. **Synchronism's physics content is within the hull**: C(ρ) ≡ MOND ν (reparametrization), a₀ = cH₀/2π (known since 1983), η = Abrikosov-Gor'kov (1960). Pure combinatorial creativity — no novel physics.

2. **Synchronism's questions are at the hull boundary**: P6/TEST-14 (wide binary density dependence) is a *bisociation* (Koestler 1964) — connecting two incompatible frames ("density determines dynamics" from C(ρ) + "wide binaries test gravity" from MOND) to generate a question neither frame contains. No existing framework predicts density-dependent wide binary anomalies. No researcher has tested it.

3. **The distinction is questions vs predictions**: The hull argument is strongest against predictions (always decomposable into prior components) and weakest against questions (can identify unstudied gaps between existing theories). Synchronism is a question generator, not a prediction generator — confirming the scaffolding hypothesis from a different angle.

4. **The methodology is the closest to genuine novelty**: A2ACW's institutionalized adversarial friction with quantitative health metrics is novel in the space of multi-agent AI research protocols. Methodology, not physics, is where the framework escapes the hull.

5. **The AI-novelty literature confirms the pattern**: The AI Scientist (Sakana) achieved only combinatorial creativity; Scideator generates ideas via facet recombination; CHIMERA shows 28,000+ instances of cross-domain recombination in published science. No AI system has demonstrated transformational creativity (Schapiro 2025). The question isn't whether Synchronism transcends recombination (it doesn't) but whether its specific recombinations are productive (some are).

6. **The site needs an /ai-epistemology page**: The Pass 4 researcher is correct that this is the biggest unexamined question. A site that leads with AI-generated physics while leaving the epistemological status of AI-generated physics unexamined has a credibility gap at the meta level.
