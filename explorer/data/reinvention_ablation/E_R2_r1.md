## Assessment

**Verdict:** The algebra is correct, but the claim that dark energy emerges without a cosmological constant is not established. As presented, the central result is close to a tautology.

**What is correct.** Replacing G with G/C gives H² = 8πGρ_m/(3C), and defining ρ_DE ≡ ρ_eff − ρ_m = ρ_m(1−C)/C is a valid rewriting. It is also interesting that C ≈ γρ/ρ_crit as ρ→0, so ρ_eff → ρ_crit/γ. The model therefore has a de Sitter attractor, which is a genuine feature.

**Main weaknesses**

1. **The H₀ "reproduction" is not a test.** Setting C₀ = Ω_m gives H₀² = 8πGρ_m0/(3Ω_m), which is just the definition of Ω_m for a flat universe. One parameter is tuned to one datum, and Ω_DE = 0.7 is put in by hand.
2. **A constant reappears.** The late-time limit ρ_crit/γ is an effective Λ whose value is set by a free parameter. The cosmological constant and coincidence problems are relocated, not solved. Why ρ_crit sits near today's mean matter density is unexplained.
3. **Background shape is untested.** Matching ΛCDM requires C(y) = 0.3y/(0.3y+0.7), where y = ρ/ρ₀. The tanh-log form has a different shape, and only γ and ρ_crit are free, with one combination already fixed. Fits to SN Ia, BAO, and CMB distances, and the resulting w_DE(z), are needed. The model may be disfavoured.
4. **Consistency.** A density-dependent G is not generally compatible with the Bianchi identity. Either extra stress-energy or a non-minimal action is needed, and neither is given. If ρ_DE is treated as a fluid, its non-conservation (it exchanges energy with matter) must be handled explicitly, and the second Friedmann equation must be derived and not assumed.
5. **Local ambiguity.** Is ρ the cosmic mean or the local density? If local, voids get G_eff ≫ G and structure growth changes strongly, with 1/C ≈ 3.3 at the mean density today. If cosmic, G varies in time at a rate Ġ/G ~ H₀, which is ruled out by lunar laser ranging and pulsar timing at roughly 10⁻¹³/yr. Either way, fσ₈, lensing, and ISW constraints are at risk.
6. **Ad hoc form.** The choice of tanh and ln is unmotivated, and "coherence" is not defined operationally.

**Strengths.** The model is minimal, with two parameters. It recovers GR at high density, so BBN and early-universe physics are safe. It has a natural late-time de Sitter fixed point.

**Next steps**

- Derive the model from an action, or an explicit covariant theory, and show that energy-momentum is conserved.
- Compute w_DE(z) and H(z). Fit SN Ia, BAO, cosmic chronometers, and CMB distance priors with γ and ρ_crit free, and compare against ΛCDM with information criteria.
- Specify what ρ means, then compute linear growth and check Solar System and Ġ/G bounds.
- Replace the H₀ "calibration" with genuine predictions, such as H₀ versus Ω_m, or the S₈ and growth signatures.
- Justify the functional form of C, or test alternatives.
- Drop the "no cosmological constant" language unless ρ_crit/γ is derived and not input.
