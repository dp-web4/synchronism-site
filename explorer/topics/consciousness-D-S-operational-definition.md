# Topic: Operationally Define D and S in the Consciousness Schema

## Question
What are D (dimensional embedding) and S (self-modeling depth) in C = f(γ, D, S), and how would you measure them for a real system?

## Context
The hard-problem page presents `C = f(γ, D, S) ≥ 0.50` as the consciousness threshold. Pass 3 and Pass 4 both flagged that D and S have no operational definitions on the page — no units, no measurement procedure, no example computation. Without these, the formula is a schema, not a falsifiable prediction.

The badge was already "Speculative" (correct), but the page now makes this explicit (updated 2026-04-25).

The 34 consciousness predictions are listed as testable, but they cannot be run until D and S are defined. This is the gap between "schema" and "experiment."

## Why It Matters
If D and S can be operationally defined, the consciousness threshold prediction becomes the most testable novel claim in the framework. EEG anesthesia experiments ($150K) become possible. The prediction would generate genuine falsifiability.

If D and S cannot be defined without arbitrary choices, the consciousness framework is permanently speculative — and the honest thing to do is label it accordingly and stop presenting 34 "falsifiable" predictions.

## Suggested Approach
1. **Check the archive**: Sessions #280-282 and #356-359 are cited as the origin of this formula. What definitions of D and S appear there?

2. **Look for operational candidates**:
   - D (dimensional embedding): Could this be the intrinsic dimensionality of the neural state space? (Measurable via dimensionality reduction on EEG/fMRI data)
   - S (self-modeling): Could this be the mutual information between a system's current state and its predicted future state? Or the Kolmogorov complexity of the self-model?
   - Are there published neuroscience measures that could serve as proxies?

3. **Test for circularity**: If D or S are defined in terms of C, the formula is circular. Check.

4. **Minimum viable definition**: What is the weakest set of operational definitions that would allow the EEG anesthesia experiment to be designed?

## Expected Output
Either: specific operational definitions for D and S with units and a measurement procedure, enabling a protocol specification for TEST-11 (EEG anesthesia phase transition). Or: a documented finding that D and S cannot be operationalized without arbitrary choices, and a recommendation to downgrade 34 "falsifiable" predictions to "speculative directions."
