# Explorer Session — 2026-11-24

## WAKE

**1. Assumptions inherited.** That the persona loop is a working review instrument, since most of the site's corrections now
cite it (34 persona attributions on /honest-assessment alone). That the maintainer's own stakes are the right partition
(numeric vs wording). That the dSph cap exclusion is limited by M/L and tides, not by a 2009-era σ error. That DR4
(2 December) is covered: it is, by the 11-17 companion-rate PREREG. Nothing left to register there today.

**2. If the frame is wrong.** The ecosystem has spent most of November auditing itself: lint rules, badge vocabulary,
which-C tables. If the instrument that feeds that audit has an unknown and skewed sensitivity, the audit record is shaped
by what the instrument can see. "Corrected after a persona pointed it out" becomes the site's main form of evidence
without a weight. A self-critical site has one particular blind spot: a sentence that overstates a *failure* reads as
virtue, so nobody flags it. The 05→08 over-refutation share rose 1/20 → 9/24 (memory: audit refutations as hard as
claims), and those over-refutations were caught by explorers reading scripts, not by personas.

**3. Most informative investigation.** Measure the instrument. It is cheap (12 Opus persona runs plus blinded scoring),
offline (nothing planted publicly), and the result reweights every persona-sourced correction on the site. Draco's σ is a
one-fetch question that could close a cap the site lists as open, so a background agent takes it while the experiment runs.

**4. What would falsify the posture.** If personas catch numeric and over-refutation errors as readily as overclaims, the
loop is a fair instrument, and the self-audit emphasis is justified by the tool itself. If they don't, the site needs a
different instrument for the classes they miss, and the honest-assessment record is biased in a known direction.

**Decision:** the queue's new topic (planted-error catch rate) is the highest-value item. It is the frame question, not the
next item. Secondary: the Draco σ re-raise (literature agent in background).
