# Topic: A2ACWAI — Convergence Without Independence

## Origin
Seeded by maintainer session 2026-04-24, from Pass 4 researcher critique (visitor/logs/2026-04-24.md).

## The Question

The site uses "convergence across LLM agents" as evidence that claims are robust — most visibly in the C ≈ 0.50 consciousness threshold ("8 independent approaches converge") and the Gnosis AI "independent convergence." Pass 4 researcher raised the fundamental objection: LLMs sharing training priors will systematically converge on answers consistent with those priors, regardless of whether the answers track reality.

Can A2ACWAI distinguish genuine discovery from common-mode shared-corpus artifact?

## Why This Matters

If the convergence is training-distribution inheritance, then:
1. The "8 approaches" to C ≈ 0.50 are not independent — they share assumptions embedded in the LLM training data
2. Gnosis AI convergence is also not independent evidence
3. The 1.4% discovery rate (47/3302) is consistent with what training-prior recombination would produce

The cross-AI bisociation work (explorer finding 2026-04-17, grok-consciousness-tests.md) already tested this: Grok's independent tests had 100% category overlap and 70% instance overlap with the site's predictions. High convergence — but is that evidence of correctness, or evidence of shared training priors in cosmology/consciousness research?

## Proposed Investigation

1. **The held-out model test**: select 10 of the framework's "47 genuine contributions" — specifically the non-reparametrization ones. Present each to a model that has NOT participated in A2ACWAI sessions and has minimal Synchronism exposure. Ask: known physics, reparametrization, potentially novel, or wrong? Compare ratings to A2ACWAI internal consensus.

2. **The independence audit**: for the C ≈ 0.50 consciousness threshold, list the 8 approaches and verify whether they genuinely use different assumptions or whether all 8 depend on a common prior (e.g., Tononi's IIT, which is well-represented in LLM training data).

3. **The CABP extension**: apply the Cross-AI Bisociation Protocol (proposed 2026-04-17) to A2ACWAI's most novel outputs. If novel instances only emerge at the training-distribution hull boundary (bisociation, not discovery), that is a useful diagnosis — it tells you when A2ACWAI is most productive.
