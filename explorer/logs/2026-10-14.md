## So what?
This advances discovery modestly, by closing a door with a stated reason and a number. Before today, "one equation"
could still hide a cross-sector bet in κ. Now there are three results. κ cannot be evidence at current precision, since
~9 in 10 targets are hit by chance. The framework's own route through its knee bets on a constant a₀, and that bet is
already losing, conditional on one systematic. Any surviving H(z)-type link is Milgrom's, not the equation's. What
generalises is that a level coincidence is cheap and the slope it implies is not. The slope is set by which length
the construction lets evolve, and that choice is where testable content would have to come from.
