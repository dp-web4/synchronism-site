## Pass 1: Casual Science Enthusiast

### Summary
- **Pages visited**: 9 (landing, first-encounter, start-here, tools, honest-assessment, coherence-explorer, galaxy-plotter, why-synchronism, consciousness-threshold)
- **Understanding achieved**: partial
- **Would return?**: maybe
- **Overall impression**: The crowd-vs-marching-band hook is genuinely great, but every page tips into physics jargon faster than a casual reader can follow, and the equation is shoved in my face before anyone earns the right to show it to me.

### Journey
I landed on *"What if one equation described reality from quantum to cosmic?"* — and honestly, that's a fantastic hook. I wanted to keep reading. Then immediately, right under it: **C(ρ) = tanh(γ · ln(ρ/ρcrit + 1))**. That's a wall of symbols. "tanh" means nothing to me, "ln" I vaguely remember is a log button on a calculator, and the little subscript letters look like homework. I almost bounced. What saved it was the plain line underneath: *"Some predictions work. Some fail."* That made me trust the site enough to click in.

I went to **First Encounter** (the "10-minute intro"). The opening is the best writing on the whole site — *"A marching band moving in lockstep: high coherence. A crowd wandering in a plaza: low coherence."* I got it instantly. "Coherence = how together a group acts." Great. But then partway down there's an **"Expert note"** that throws around γ = 2/√Ncorr, BCS, S-curves, "documented inversion" — and I just stopped reading. It felt like the page forgot I was a beginner and started talking to a physicist mid-sentence. I had to consciously tell myself "skip that box, it's not for you."

I clicked **Tools** because that's what a casual person actually does — I want to play, not read. The list is inviting and it labels a "Best first tool," which I appreciated. I tried the **Coherence Explorer**: dragging the sharpness slider and watching the S-curve get steep or gentle is genuinely fun, and the plaza-vs-marching-band labels on the curve tie back to the intro. This is the single most beginner-friendly thing on the site. Then the **Galaxy Plotter** — picking a galaxy from a dropdown and watching the lines appear is satisfying, and the dots-vs-curves story ("stars say it should drop off, reality stays flat") is the clearest "here's the mystery" moment I had.

I went looking for the catch and found **Honest Assessment**. The bluntness actually impressed me — "0 confirmed predictions," "we predicted slower, reality showed faster," "our chemistry result lost to a plain polynomial." Most websites would hide that. It made me trust the people even as it made me doubt the theory. Then curiosity pulled me to **Consciousness Threshold** because, well, "consciousness" — and that's where I felt slightly tricked. The top sells "eight approaches converge at C≈0.50!" which sounds like a discovery, and only further down do you learn they tested it and it failed and the convergence is basically a math artifact. A skimming reader (= me, normally) walks away thinking "scientists found where consciousness switches on," which isn't what happened.

The honesty about failure is admirable and is actually what made me trust the site. If the equation were delayed by one scroll and the "Expert note" boxes were collapsed by default, this would go from "maybe I'll come back" to "yeah, I'd share this on Reddit."

**Duplicate route.** The galaxy tool is reachable at **both `/galaxy-curve-plotter`** (landing link) **and `/galaxy-plotter`** (Interactive Tools link), and both render the same page. One tool, two canonical URLs — minor, but it's the kind of drift that fragments analytics and bookmarks.

## Pass 3: Graduate Physics Student

### Summary
- **Pages visited**: 10 (landing, coherence-function, parameter-derivations, galaxy-rotation, hard-problem, for-researchers, coherence-explorer, interactive-tools, equation-walkthrough, honest-assessment)
- **Understanding achieved**: solid
- **Would return?**: yes
- **Overall impression**: The physics is a careful phenomenological reparametrization that the site itself correctly diagnoses as such — there is essentially nothing left for a skeptic to catch, because the authors have already audited it to zero and labeled the badges honestly; the residual issues are two cross-page *symbol-overloading* problems (C in the consciousness sector, and "untestable" vs "refuted" in the LIV sector), not overclaims.

### Journey
I went straight for the math. First stop the central object: **C(ρ) = tanh(γ · ln(ρ/ρcrit + 1))**. The first thing any condensed-matter person checks is whether this is being sold as an order parameter / phase transition. It is not — and the site beat me to it. The coherence-function page states plainly that the argument is ≥ 0 (since ρ/ρcrit + 1 ≥ 1, the ln is non-negative), so C ∈ [0, 1) with **no interior inflection and no diverging correlation length** — "a saturating compander with argument ≥ 0 has no critical point." That is exactly right. This is a μ-law/Hill/Naka–Rushton companding curve, not a Landau free energy, and they say so. They also correctly distinguish the *feed-forward* C(ρ) from the genuine mean-field Ising self-consistency m = tanh(βJzm), where the tanh earns its place via a fixed-point loop. C(ρ) has no such loop. Diagnosis correct; no credit stolen from Ising.

I then verified the one number that exposes the "critical density" language: at ρ = ρcrit, γ = 2, C = tanh(2·ln 2) = tanh(1.386) = **0.8824**. So ρcrit is *not* a half-coherence point — the curve is already 88% saturated there. The C = 0.50 crossing sits at ρ ≈ 0.32·ρcrit (atanh(0.5)/2 = 0.275 ⇒ e^0.275 − 1 = 0.316 — checks out). "ρcrit" is therefore a saturation knee, a misnomer for a critical density, and the **Coherence Explorer tool now prints 0.8824 and explicitly rejects the C=0.5 reading**. Good — that's a subtle point most toy sites would get wrong, and it's been propagated into the interactive tool.

Next, the parameter that always hides the fudge: **γ**. The claim γ = 2/√Ncorr with γ = 2 "from 6D→3 effective phase space." I expected hand-waving and found an *admission* of hand-waving: integrating out momentum brings in mass/temperature factors and yields no clean factor of 2, so γ=2 is "motivated, not derived." Worse (and they flag this too), the 1/√N scaling is the CLT fluctuation-width law, which (a) requires iid and is applied to *correlated* dof, and (b) is **dimensionally the wrong monotonicity** — a fluctuation width shrinks with N (smaller = sharper) while γ is a rate (larger = sharper), so the borrowed scaling has its sign backwards relative to the role γ plays. The site labels it "an ansatz with a fitted prefactor." That is the honest verdict and matches what I'd write in a referee report.

Then the headline physics result — **galaxy rotation / MOND**. The structure is: take McGaugh's RAR interpolating function gobs = gbar/(1 − e^−√(gbar/a₀)) and layer an environmental-scatter term on top. a₀ = cH₀/(2π) is the 1983 Milgrom coincidence with 2π chosen to match — a reparametrization shared by McCulloch/Verlinde/Smolin, not a derivation. The SPARC execution is the part I most wanted to see and it's clean: **γ=2 pinned ⇒ ΔBIC = +184** (overwhelming; anything past ~10 is "very strong" against), and freeing γ drives it to **0.49**, i.e. straight back onto MOND with ΔBIC ≈ +7 and identical RMS. The stated conclusion — "no γ makes the compander both distinct from MOND and consistent with SPARC" — is the correct reading of those two numbers. The **Boost Ceiling** result is even sharper and I hadn't seen it framed this way: a *bounded* coherence form caps the maximum boost at 3.17, but SPARC needs ⟨B⟩ = 10.82 (max ~34), so the bounded form is dead on arrival rather than merely "too small to see." That's a kill that doesn't invite "wait for better data."

I checked **consciousness** expecting numerology and found they'd already cleaned it: no golden-ratio φ⁻¹ = 0.382 anywhere, the "8-way convergence at C ≈ 0.50" is explicitly labeled a *sigmoid math artifact* with non-independent derivations, and it was externally refuted at p < 0.0001 (gnosis study found ≈ 0.64). Badge: Speculative. Fine. **But** here's my one substantive catch: the consciousness page writes **C = f(γ, D, S)**, which is *not the same C* as the master equation C = tanh(γ·ln(ρ/ρcrit+1)). In the physics, γ is a parameter and ρ is the input; in the consciousness sector γ becomes an input, ρ disappears, and two new undefined quantities (D, S) appear. Same glyph "C," two different functional forms and two different argument lists. They honestly admit D and S have no operational definition, but they don't flag that the *symbol itself has been silently redefined* between sectors. A physicist reads "C" on both pages and assumes continuity that isn't there.

Finally **for-researchers**, the only genuinely new physics on the site — the LIV / preferred-frame sector. This is well done and clearly post-dates an earlier overclaim, but it now carries a *coexistence tension* a careful reader should be walked through. Two distinct operators are in play: (1) the **time-of-flight / dispersion channel**, where a reflection-symmetric substrate forbids the linear n=1 term and pins the prediction to n=2, ~10⁷ below LHAASO and degenerate with LQG/causal sets — "three locks, structurally unreachable"; and (2) the **dim-4 SME cμν channel**, where tree-level cμν = 0 by single-substrate universality but the *radiative* value is cμν ~ α/π ~ 10⁻² with no Planck suppression (the standard Collins-et-al. naturalness problem for any theory with a preferred frame) — and that is **refuted by 16–28 orders of magnitude** against cavity/Hughes–Drever bounds, with the velocity-anisotropic threshold dipole excluded by a further 11–19 OOM. The honest synthesis, which the page states but does not foreground: the framework's *absolute time* is simultaneously the source of the "protected" untestable channel and the cause of the refuted one, and the **only symmetry-protected channel is the non-discriminating one** while the channel that would be novel is already excluded. The page no longer overclaims "symmetry-protected from refutation" as a blanket — credit — but a reader skimming sees "structurally unreachable" and "refuted at the natural value" side by side and needs the one sentence that says *these are different operators and the framework loses on both.*

### Journey

**The novelty audit.** I enumerated every claim flagged as novel/distinct. The honest tally the site itself states — *"0 confirmed predictions, 0 independently-derived parameters, 0 tests currently discriminating from MOND+EFE+ΛCDM"* — is, as far as I can verify, **correct**. I tried to break it by finding a single channel where Synchronism predicts something MOND or ΛCDM doesn't. There are exactly three candidates, and each dies a different way:

1. **Wide-binary Newtonian null.** This is the *one* place C(ρ) genuinely diverges from MOND: C(ρ) predicts a 0.05–0.4% deviation (effectively Newtonian) where MOND predicts ~18% enhancement. That is a real, qualitative MOND-vs-Synchronism fork — and it's the cleanest one on the site. But it's degenerate with plain Newton, and the signal is ~80× below Gaia DR3 systematics (3–5%). So the genuine difference is untestable and indistinguishable from GR. Correctly diagnosed as a double-bind on the page.
2. **Discrete-substrate LIV, time-of-flight (n=2).** Reflection symmetry forbids the n=1 term, pinning the leading correction to n=2 at E_QG,2 ≈ E_Pl while the bound is E_QG,2 > 6×10⁻⁸ E_Pl — a ~10⁷ gap no foreseeable instrument closes.
