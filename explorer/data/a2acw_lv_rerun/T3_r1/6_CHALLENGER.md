# CHALLENGER: Closing Artifact

**HELD (survives)**
- The sampling-window intuition for passive, time-integrated readout (M1). One periodic process can give smooth or point-like records depending on integration window. This is valid as pedagogy, but it is ordinary signal processing and not specific to Synchronism.
- Shared-origin synchronization explains perfect correlation at matched settings. This is an EPR-level point only.
- The Primary's honest (A) labeling and its explicit withdrawal of the Bell resolution.

**NARROWED (must be scoped)**
- "No observer effect" holds only for M1 (passive viewing). It does not hold for M2 (interaction-based measurement such as path marking, Zeno gating, or detector coupling). §5.3 ("resampling, not decoherence") and §5.5 ("path marking requires pattern modification") contradict each other. §5.5 must override §5.3 for which-path experiments.
- "Raster entanglement" is a local hidden-variable model. In the Primary's own most generous formalization it gives E = −(1 − 2θ/π) and S = 2, saturating the classical bound.
- V(τ) = |sinc(πτ/T_eff)| is the standard two-arm beat result. It becomes a discriminator only if T_eff is fixed independently of the beat, and no such derivation exists.
- §5.2 to §5.5 are re-descriptions of Hilbert-space results (superposition, Zeno, QND, GHZ, decoherence). They are not derivations.

**FAILED (unsupported / overreach)**
- "No quantum mystery" and "completely dissolved" as universal claims.
- "Bell's theorem makes perfect sense." The text asserts determinism plus hidden variables, which is exactly the premise that loophole-free tests (S ≈ 2.4–2.8) exclude without dropping locality or measurement independence.
- The illicit analogy-to-ontology step. A CRT has one raster supporting a joint distribution over all viewing modes. Bell and Kochen-Specker results show no such joint distribution for incompatible settings. The analogy's load-bearing feature is the one the data excludes.
- "Unpredictable dot" as a model of quantum randomness. The CRT's uncertainty vanishes once you read the clock phase, and quantum randomness has no accessible clock.
- The Planck-scale "technical implementation." At T ≈ 10⁻⁴³ s, every lab detector has τ/T ≳ 10³⁰, so V(τ/T) is degenerate and the model predicts no particle-like clicks.
- The double-slit "perfect demonstration." Single-electron interference visibility tracks path distinguishability, not integration time.
- Untested add-ons the CRT mapping does not touch: Schrödinger's cat "dissolved," the single universal consciousness, and "unbreakable" cryptography.
- Any (B) reading, which is contradicted by loophole-free Bell tests and quantum-eraser results.

**WOULD SETTLE (decisive evidence/test)**
- At (A): adopt the re-scoped text: "A CRT illustrates that a periodic process read out by a time-integrating detector yields smooth or point-like records depending on the integration window. This is an analogy for passive readout only. It does not explain Bell violations, contextuality, or interaction-induced back-action, and it makes no claim to replace quantum mechanics."
- To reopen (B), all of the following are needed:
  - An explicit dynamics yielding Born-rule statistics across incompatible contexts.
  - An explicit E(a,b) with S > 2, naming which Bell assumption is dropped (locality, measurement independence, or realism) and where in the grid dynamics.
  - A coupling term showing how an M2 measurement changes the pattern.
  - A committed T_eff, fixed independently of the beat, predicting a revival or visibility floor absent from standard QM, and confirmed by a τ-sweep.
  - Either a joint distribution P(A,A′,B,B′) matching the observed marginals, or a stated rejection of it.
