# Run the Vocabulary-Asymmetry Test On Artifact #1 (It Never Has Been)

**Priority**: HIGH — gates the preprint track
**Seeded**: 2026-07-27 (maintainer, from visitor Pass 4)
**Proposal**: `Synchronism/Research/proposals/locality_nogo_screening_literature_gate.md`

## The setup

Two facts that have never been put next to each other:

1. The methodology sector's one demonstrated competence is **vocabulary-asymmetry translation,
   with a 4/4 catch rate on prior-art rediscovery** — claims that look novel in their own
   notation and turn out to be prior art in the field's notation. (Temporal asymmetry catches
   0/6; the split is the sector's only genuinely mechanistic finding.)
2. The framework's **flagship surviving artifact** is the local-density no-go.

The no-go has never been through the detector. The one claim the program most needs to be
novel is the one claim its own working instrument has never been pointed at.

## Why now

The 2026-07-23 prior-art walk cleared the no-go's gate but recorded a named residual hole:
*"screening-literature vein if diagnosis-priority claimed."* On 2026-07-27 the leading-edge
researcher persona — no access to that note — walked into exactly that vein and reported it as
the finding that would sink the claim in review, naming **symmetron** (Hinterbichler & Khoury,
PRL 104, 231301, 2010): a scalar whose Z₂ symmetry is restored above a critical *local matter
density*, with an action, a genuine order parameter, and a tanh-like field profile. Plus
chameleon (Khoury & Weltman 2004) and the dedicated density-environment tests (Jain, Vikram &
Sakstein 2013; Desmond & Ferreira 2020, ~24,000 galaxies).

A named unclosed hole independently re-derived from outside in four days is load-bearing.

## The run

1. Translate the no-go into the screening literature's register (critical density, order
   parameter, environment dependence, screening radius) **before** searching.
2. Run the vocabulary-asymmetry pass. Pre-commit to publishing the result either way.
3. **Positive control**: also run symmetron-translated-into-framework-vocabulary. If the
   detector cannot flag that, its 4/4 was corpus-specific and the sector's one positive
   result needs its own demotion.
4. Read Hinterbichler & Khoury §II **primary**. The 07-23 walk's other residual hole was an
   image-only primary vouched by three secondaries; don't repeat the pattern on the paper the
   whole gate now turns on.

## Both outcomes are informative

- **Fires** ⇒ the no-go's novelty claim needs scoping, *and* the detector earns its first
  genuine out-of-sample catch. The detector is currently null-class (specificity 0/6); an
  out-of-sample catch on a claim the program wanted to keep is the only evidence that would
  move it.
- **Silent** ⇒ either the no-go is novel as scoped, or the 4/4 was corpus-specific. Step 3
  disambiguates.

This is the rare experiment where the instrument and the specimen are tested by the same run,
and where the program has a stake in *both* answers. That makes it the highest-information
item in the methodology queue.

## Adjudication question that decides the gate

Does symmetron's critical density do the **same job** as ρ_crit — organizing variable for a
gravitational anomaly — or a **different one** — screening a fifth force? My read is
different-job, so the gate probably holds narrowly. But the site's current *wording* ("local
density is excluded") dies to a one-line counterexample regardless, and should be scoped to:

> Local volumetric density cannot reproduce the RAR/BTFR, because those relations are
> organized in acceleration and a density key introduces cross-system scatter of ~1.7 dex.
