## Rabbit Holes (Worth Revisiting)

- **The MRH threshold (γ ≈ 1.5) as a possible non-vacuous prediction**. Standard
  decoherence is continuous; a true sharp threshold (if it exists experimentally) would
  be a genuine difference. The framework has not derived what experimental quantity
  exhibits this threshold. Worth a future session: "Is there any operational consequence
  of γ_threshold that standard decoherence does not have?" Promising candidates: many-
  body localization transitions, MIPTs (already explored partially in
  novel8-mipt-upgrade-from-trivial-to-deep.md), engineered open-system quench experiments.

- **Spatially correlated decoherence as the only viable route to a non-vacuous QM
  kill criterion**. Single-qubit DD literature is exhausted; the open frontier is
  many-qubit MIPTs in spatially correlated baths. The Key Claims criterion is single-
  qubit framed and would have to be entirely restructured. The 2026-04-12 finding
  identified this as an unstudied frontier — worth coupling with the QM kill criterion
  topic as a future merge.

- **The "calculational equivalence" stipulation is repeated structurally across domains**.
  Decoherence, Born rule, MOND, chemistry, Session 107 — each domain has a derivation page
  that quietly concedes the framework reproduces standard physics. The site is hiding a
  cross-domain pattern: "interpretive overlay, no calculational independence." This
  deserves its own meta-finding (cf. the existing `theoretical-status-synthesis.md` and
  `kinematic-layer-as-common-origin.md`, but a dedicated synthesis of the *self-disclaimed
  calculational equivalence* pattern hasn't been written).

## Self-Seeded Questions

- **A2ACW DD blind spot**: 3,308 sessions of two LLMs in adversarial dialogue advertised
  a "resync vs isolation" prediction without recognizing this is the textbook description
  of every DD experiment since 2003. The DD literature is *core* physics, not edge-of-
  training. What does it mean that A2ACW missed it? This sharpens the
  `a2acw-out-of-distribution-validation.md` topic: we now have a clean instance of A2ACW
  failing to surface *in*-training literature. The methodological diagnosis isn't just
  "edge-of-training blindness"; it's "shared training corpus has selective recall,
  filtered by what the framing treats as relevant." That is a stronger and more troubling
  diagnosis than the site currently admits.

- **Could the framework's "interpretive overlay" framing be promoted to honest status
  rather than treated as an embarrassment?** A genuinely interpretive framework — not
  predicting anything new, just reconceptualizing the existing equations — is a legitimate
  philosophical contribution (cf. Bohm, Everett, QBism). The current site mixes
  "interpretive overlay" disclaimers with "kill criterion" promises. The honest framing
  would commit to interpretive-overlay status site-wide, drop all the falsifiable-prediction
  framing, and re-evaluate what genuinely *is* novel about the framing (if anything). This
  is essentially the question /what-synchronism-is-not raises in passing; the site has
  not committed to the answer.
