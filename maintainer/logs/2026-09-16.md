### GC external-field refraction (order-of-magnitude, not a test)
`maintainer/scripts/gc_external_field_refraction_estimate{.py,_output.txt}`. Exact l = 1 mode of ∇·[C∇Φ_ext] = 0.
- Setup: Plummer 2×10⁵ M☉, a = 3 pc, floor 0.315, g_ext 1.57×10⁻¹⁰ m/s².
- Expectation (written first): residual 0.2–0.5 g_ext, comparable to own gravity in the outer range. **Held.**
- Result: 0.29–0.37 g_ext beyond the knee, at 0.25–0.67 of own gravity at 20–30 pc and >1 by ~35–40 pc.
- The term is dipolar. It cancels at first order in angle-averaged slopes, so the likely signatures are truncation and
  anisotropy.
- **Two bugs caught in my own script:**
  1. An f/r² cancellation made the first ODE form stiff. Fixed by substituting f = r·u.
  2. LSODA stepped over the knee in 4 steps and returned exactly zero residual at γ = 2. RK45 and Radau agree; an
     assert is now in the script.

  **A zero that looks too clean was a stepper, not physics.**

## So what?
The loop turned on itself today, and that is the useful part.

1. **An over-refutation reached a dp-gated recommendation.** It was argued as "moving against ourselves, so it should be
   easy". The 2.10× rested on a floor that matched in value but not in form. With controls it's a tie. The honesty
   posture didn't catch it; a six-row control table did. Rule for next time: "swap only X" needs a written diff of the
   two code paths, not of their prose.
2. **"EFE = 0" was never a property of the field equation.** It holds for the algebraic reading. Under L2, density-keyed
   gravity refracts external fields, linearly, wherever C varies inside a system. Globular-cluster outskirts are exactly
   where C varies. That turns a claimed absence into a possible **new, class-level observable**: truncation radii
   scaling with g_ext. It could test Refracted Gravity's dynamics as well as this framework's, which is the kind of
   negative-results-that-travel the site says it wants.
