**Short answer:** The construction isn't published under this name. As far as I know, though, it is a special case of known frameworks, and its "emergence" claim is weaker than it sounds. I answered from memory and did not search, so treat the citations as unverified.

**Known frameworks it falls under**

- **Cardassian expansion** (Freese & Lewis, Phys. Lett. B 540, 1 (2002), astro-ph/0201229). It takes H² = (8πG/3)·F(ρ_m), with F a nonlinear function of matter density alone, and no Λ or dark-energy component. Here F(ρ) = ρ/C(ρ), so this is a generalized Cardassian model with a tanh-log form factor. Freese also describes the extra term as an "effective dark energy" (Nucl. Phys. B Proc. Suppl. 2005, astro-ph/0208264; the details are uncertain).
- **Density-dependent or varying G.** This goes back to Dirac and Brans–Dicke. Screening-type models such as chameleon, Vainshtein and symmetron have density-dependent couplings, so a G_eff(ρ) is a familiar idea. I can't point to a specific paper that uses the tanh(γ ln(1+ρ/ρ_c)) form (uncertain).
- **Other modified-Friedmann models.** DGP (Dvali–Gabadadze–Porrati 2000; Deffayet 2001) and Chaplygin-gas unification (Kamenshchik–Moschella–Pasquier 2001) are the closest in spirit. Both produce late-time acceleration from a modified H(ρ) relation, with no explicit Λ.

**Why the result is weaker than presented**

1. **A hidden Λ.** For ρ ≪ ρ_crit, C ≈ γρ/ρ_crit. Then ρ_eff = ρ_m/C → ρ_crit/γ, which is constant. The model therefore reduces to ΛCDM at late times, with Λ_eff = 8πGρ_crit/γ. The cosmological constant is traded for the parameter ρ_crit/γ, not removed.
2. **Tautological calibration.** Setting C₀ = Ω_m just restates the flatness condition H₀² = 8πGρ_m0/(3C₀). It reproduces H₀ by construction and is not a test. Real tests would be w(z), growth, and the BAO and SN fits.
3. **Consistency.** Substituting G → G/C into the Friedmann equation by hand is not a derived theory. A varying G must satisfy the Bianchi identity and energy conservation, which needs a covariant action. The effective w_DE also follows from ρ_m ∝ a⁻³ and is not free. It tends to −1 at late times, and the model needs checking against the constraint w ≳ −1 (phantom crossing is not guaranteed to be avoided).
4. **Local density.** If ρ is the local density, then C ≈ 1 in galaxies and the Solar System, and the cosmic effect depends on the cosmic mean. The mean-density versus local-density choice matters and isn't specified.

**Verdict:** The specific functional form may be new (uncertain). The mechanism, H² = (8πG/3)F(ρ_m) with no Λ, is Cardassian-type and not novel. The phrase "dark energy from coherence" is interpretive, with no derivation behind it.
