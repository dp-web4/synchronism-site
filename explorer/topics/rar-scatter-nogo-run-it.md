# Topic: Run the RAR intrinsic-scatter no-go (parameter-free, one afternoon)

## Question
What is the intrinsic scatter of local density ρ at fixed g_bar across the 153-galaxy SPARC
sample? Does it exceed the RAR's own intrinsic scatter (~0.03–0.13 dex, Lelli+2017, Li+2018)?

## Context
Today's visitor Pass 4 (Leading-Edge Researcher persona, 2026-08-02) identified this as the
sharpest available no-go and noted it has never been run: "any theory whose boost keys on a
variable X other than g_bar must inject into the RAR at least the scatter of the X↔g_bar
relation at fixed g_bar." If ρ has even a few tenths of a dex of intrinsic spread at fixed
g_bar, algebraic C(ρ) is excluded parameter-free — no fitting, no model comparison, just a
scatter-propagation bound. This is a stronger and cleaner form of the existing ~1.7 dex
*offset* argument already cited on `/for-researchers` (which is an offset, absorbable by
recalibrating ρ_crit — a spread is not).

It also matters for the site's one cited counterexample: Burrage, Copeland & Millington (PRD
95, 064050, 2017) — the symmetron construction with a published escape from the algebraic
locality no-go — reproduced the RAR's *mean* relation with universal (μ, M, λ), not
necessarily its scatter. If the scatter bound holds and BCM's construction doesn't survive it,
BCM stops being "the end of the no-go" and becomes its first test case.

## Why It Matters
This is citable, zero-cost (public SPARC data already used elsewhere on the site), and
parameter-free — exactly the kind of result `/for-researchers` says it wants more of. It would
either sharpen the flagship no-go considerably or reveal a gap in it. Either outcome is
publishable.
