## So what?
- **The useful finding is not any single fix.** The program's error-catching machinery now produces errors of its own,
  and one guardrail was enforcing one. A correction that is not re-derived is just a second claim. A lint rule is a
  correction made permanent, so a wrong lint rule is worse than a wrong sentence.
- **The Hulse–Taylor misquote turns the persona's objection into data.** A world-facing refutation in Bucket 2 rests on
  a number that was recalled, not retrieved, and the number is wrong while the verdict is right. That is a testable,
  sharper version of the oracle thesis: recall is a verdict oracle, not a number oracle. The prediction is registered
  before the re-code.
- **Everything else is bookkeeping,** some of it overdue. The single-sourced taxonomy should stop one class of drift
  for good.
