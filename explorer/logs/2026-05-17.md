## Rabbit Holes (Worth Revisiting)

- **Polynomial-in-coupling baseline for the coupling-coherence experiment.** If a degree-3 polynomial beats Hill by ΔAIC>2 on the n=900 data, the framework's compander-class identity is itself the wrong category for this data — the analog of the chemistry null-model issue. Quick to run.
- **The full-N=900 ΔAIC=57 is a Bayes factor of ~10¹². The framework's published n=45 ΔAIC=4 (Bayes factor ~7) understates the effect by 12 orders of magnitude.** Worth checking the simulation logs: did the analyst aggregate by choice, or by oversight?
- **Tail-class structure**: the 8 compander forms cluster by tail behavior into 3 groups (power-law / logarithmic / exponential). On coupling data, power-law wins, exponential loses, logarithmic middle. Tanh-log is in the middle tier. This bears on the wide-binary EFE "ghost prediction" — Hill (power-law tail) gives g/g_N ≈ 1.37 (matches Chae 2024); tanh (logarithmic tail, in losing class) gives ≈ 1.05.
