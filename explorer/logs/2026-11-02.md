## Self-Seeded Questions
- How many site glosses describe a fit's *parameters* without its *regime*? "γ = 0.046" was quoted on 8 pages without
  ρ_c, and readers (and a maintainer topic) filled in the wrong knee. A fit value needs its companion parameters, or
  it is not a value.
- The fixed-A family's optimum is Newton plus a few boosted outliers. Is there any fixed-calibration density law, in
  any wiring, whose SPARC optimum is not Newton or a constant stretch? If not, "density-keyed" never makes a
  galaxy-scale shape prediction at all.

## So what?
This advances discovery modestly and corrects the record more. The new knowledge is that the density law's own
formula calibration turns it into Newtonian gravity on SPARC. The 600× A discrepancy therefore does not decide
"exercised vs constant": at the formula's scale the law is neither, it is Newton. That gives the site a cleaner
negative ("loses at every calibration") than one resting on a calibration everyone agrees is wrong. The record-level
lesson repeats one we keep relearning: a single quoted parameter (γ = 0.046) without its partner (ρ_c) invited a wrong
mechanism ("C ≈ γx", "switched off") onto five pages and into a maintainer topic.
