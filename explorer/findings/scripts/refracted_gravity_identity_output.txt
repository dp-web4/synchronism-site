(1) C_Omega IS RG's PERMITTIVITY, ALGEBRAICALLY -- the logistic written twice
================================================================================
  0.5*(tanh(u)+1) = 1/(1+exp(-2u))                       [logistic]
  y/(1+y) with y = z^p = exp(p ln z) = 1/(1+exp(-p ln z)) [logistic]
  => identical with  u = (p/2) ln z,  i.e.  q*log10(z) = (p/2) ln z
     =>  p = 2q/ln(10) = 0.8686 q      (RG uses log base 10)

  q = 0.5    ->  p = 0.4343   max |eps_RG - C_Omega| over 8 decades = 2.220e-16
  q = 0.75   ->  p = 0.6514   max |eps_RG - C_Omega| over 8 decades = 2.220e-16
  q = 2.0    ->  p = 1.7372   max |eps_RG - C_Omega| over 8 decades = 2.220e-16

  ** EXACT.  C_Omega's functional form is RG's permittivity, with the
     'vacuum permittivity' eps0 renamed Omega_m and 1/phi in place of p. **
  The site calls the resulting bounded boost B <= 1/Omega_m 'the framework's
  only feature distinguishing it from MOND'.  In RG that is B <= 1/eps0, a
  published property of the 2016 construction.

(2) C_rho IS RG's PERMITTIVITY AT eps0 = 0, up to the regulator
================================================================================
  RG at eps0=0:  eps = 0.5*(tanh(q log10(rho/rho_c)) + 1)
  Site:          C   = tanh(gamma ln(1 + rho/rho_crit))
  Both are tanh-of-log-density on [0,1).  They differ ONLY in how the
  rho -> 0 limit is regulated: RG shifts and halves, the site adds 1
  inside the log.  Fit one to the other and report the residual.

  RG(rho_c=1e-21, q=0.75)  ->  best C_rho: rho_crit=1.027e-22, gamma=0.2445
      rms residual = 0.0142   max |residual| = 0.0299   over 7 decades in rho
  RG(rho_c=1e-24, q=0.75)  ->  best C_rho: rho_crit=1.114e-25, gamma=0.2524
      rms residual = 0.0141   max |residual| = 0.0324   over 7 decades in rho
  RG(rho_c=1e-21, q=2.00)  ->  best C_rho: rho_crit=1.428e-20, gamma=7.4588
      rms residual = 0.0171   max |residual| = 0.0464   over 7 decades in rho

                                                          flagged OPEN (their 2.2.1)

  CAUTION -- these must be compared THROUGH the regulator mapping in (2),
  not by reading the raw symbols side by side.  Done properly:

    rho_crit: the site's fitted 3.0e-25 kg/m^3 maps to RG rho_c ~ 2.7e-24
      kg/m^3 = 2.7e-27 g/cm^3, which IS inside RG's quoted galaxy range
      (1e-24 to 1e-27 g/cm^3).  Agreement is real.
    steepness: RG's galaxy q = 0.75 maps to gamma = 0.245, NOT to 0.75.
      The site's fitted gamma = 0.489-0.498 is therefore ~2x STEEPER than
      RG's disc-galaxy value, sitting between RG's galaxies (q=0.75) and
      its clusters (q=2).  It does not 'bracket q=3/4 from below' -- that
      reading compares a natural-log slope to a base-10 one.
    floor: Omega_m = 0.315 vs RG's disc eps0 = 0.20-0.25, a ~30% offset;
      the site DERIVES its floor from cosmology, RG FITS its floor.  That
      is a real difference and it is the one place the site's version is
      more constrained than the prior art.

  Two of three parameters agree once mapped; the third is 2x off.  None is
  cited.  The site's floor being derived rather than fitted is the single
  defensible novelty in the comparison, and it is a novelty about
  PARAMETER ECONOMY, not about the equation.

(4) THE TEST PASS 4 CALLS UNREGISTERED IS THIS LITERATURE'S STANDARD METHOD
================================================================================
  Pass 4: 'Vertical disk structure ... Gaia DR3 vertical-Jeans measurements
  exist now.  This is a Tier-1, existing-data, zero-cost test.' and 'the
  catalog contains none of them'.

  Cesare, Diaferio, Matsakos & Dominguez Romero (2020), A&A 637, A70:
    'the rotation curves AND the radial profiles of the stellar velocity
     dispersion PERPENDICULAR to the galactic disks of 30 galaxies from the
     DiskMass Survey ... to determine the gravitational permittivity'

  So the vertical channel is not an unexploited gap -- it is how the
  competing implementation of the SAME equation fixed its free function,
  six years ago, on 30 galaxies.  That does not make the test worthless.
  It makes it CHEAPER and SHARPER than Pass 4 realised: there is a
  published, fitted, three-parameter competitor with the identical field
  equation to run head-to-head against, instead of a blank-sheet forecast.
