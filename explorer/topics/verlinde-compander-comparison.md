# Topic: Verlinde Emergent Gravity vs. C(ρ) Compander — Is There a Reduction?

**Seeded**: 2026-05-24
**Priority**: HIGH
**Origin**: Visitor feedback 2026-05-24, Pass 4 researcher

## The Question

Verlinde's emergent gravity (2016) derives MOND-like galaxy dynamics from entropy gradients in the Hubble volume. Synchronism's C(ρ) compander produces MOND-equivalent dynamics when γ is free (SPARC RAR: free-γ = 0.49 ≡ MOND, ΔBIC=+7). Both frameworks share a₀ = cH₀/(2π).

**Question**: Is C(ρ) a sub-case, extension, or independent reparametrization of Verlinde's framework in the low-acceleration regime?

## Why This Matters

If C(ρ) reduces to Verlinde's framework in the galaxy regime, that's a third reparametrization in the same class as the MOND reparametrization — but a more precise one, because Verlinde has a field-theoretic foundation (contested but specified). This would settle the landscape position of C(ρ) relative to the contemporary literature.

If C(ρ) does NOT reduce to Verlinde, there must be a regime where they make different predictions. That's a new discriminating test.

## Research Approach

1. Read Verlinde (2016), arXiv:1611.02269, specifically the derivation of the MOND-like force law from apparent dark matter entropy: F_D = M_D a₀/2 in the deep-MOND limit.

2. Compute what C(ρ) predicts for the same input as Verlinde's ρ_DE (dark energy density). In Verlinde's framework, the elastic dark energy responds to baryonic mass via ρ_DE ∝ M_baryon/r³ beyond a transition radius. In C(ρ), ρ is the local density. Are these the same variable?

3. Check: can the C(ρ) compander (with free γ = 0.49 at SPARC precision) be derived as the effective force law from Verlinde's entropy argument?

4. If yes: add a "Verlinde reduction" entry to /honest-assessment and /what-synchronism-is-not. The compander is a reparametrization of entropic gravity, not just MOND.

5. If no: identify the regime where they differ (cluster scale? High-acceleration regime? Gravitational lensing vs. dynamics?).

## Key Reference

Verlinde, E.P. (2016). "Emergent Gravity and the Dark Universe." arXiv:1611.02269. Tested vs. KiDS weak gravitational lensing (Brouwer et al. 2017, A&A 604, A33) — consistent at ~1σ.
