# Closing Artifact (Challenger)

## HELD (survives)
- **Ratio constraint:** stable memory requires E_s/k_BT ≳ 40 (Arrhenius/Landauer), with κ = ln(ν₀·t_ret) derived from retention time. This is correct stat mech and information theory.
- **Two-tier structure:** labile weak bonds plus stable strong bonds is a sensible design requirement. It gives a ceiling set by E_s and a floor set by E_w.
- **Qualitative point:** temperature relative to bond energies governs which patterns persist. This is true but not new.
- **Defender's honesty:** the silicon-convergence argument and the "0.00001%" statistic were withdrawn. The T ≡ ⟨V²⟩ weakness was acknowledged.

## NARROWED (must be scoped)
- "~300 K window" holds only for **liquid-water, H-bond/covalent chemistry** (~240-373 K). Other solvents and chemistries (ammonia, hydrocarbons, vdW) have other windows, which the §4.7 "universal law" forbids.
- "Transcends substrate" holds only in the information sense (a ratio constraint), not as an absolute temperature.
- The floor (E_w/k_BT ≲ 10) is an assumed lability threshold. t_ret, E_w, and E_s are inputs read off known chemistry.
- The scope criterion (passive radiative balance) admits any planet. A cold-world biosphere would therefore be in scope and would falsify "~300 K," so the scope rule and the law cannot both be kept.
- Temperature's role is methodological: any Intent-dynamics model must reproduce Boltzmann-weighted barrier crossing.

## FAILED (unsupported / overreach)
- **"~300 K universal law of organized complexity":** step 4 (window ≈ 300 K) does not follow from steps 1-3 without conditioning on water chemistry. The parsimonious alternative (solvent range + bond-energy scale + orbital distance) explains the same data and allows other windows.
- **"AI needs ~300 K":** the 253-423 K range is a packaging spec. Cryo-CMOS and superconducting logic work at 4 K, and Landauer cost falls with cooling.
- **T ≡ ⟨V²⟩:** it omits mass, and the classical configurational partition function is mass-independent. This is refuted by existing physics at the definition level.
- **Original falsifiers #1-#3:**
  - #1 is unobservable on accessible timescales.
  - #2 can be dodged case by case.
  - #3 is a textbook Kramers/Ising result with no Synchronism-specific content.
- **Foundational/new-law status of §4.7:** it makes no differential prediction relative to Arrhenius + Boltzmann + Landauer.
- **§5.20:** it contains no testable content and offers no support for the law.
- **"0.00001% of cosmic range":** scale-dependent and not evidence.
- **N = 1 circularity:** E_b was inferred from Earth chemistry and then "predicts" Earth's temperature.

## WOULD SETTLE (decisive evidence/test)
- An Intent-dynamics simulation that yields the two-tier band and κ = ln(ν₀·t_ret) **without Arrhenius/Boltzmann inserted**, with a quantitative deviation from Kramers.
- A pre-registered, non-Boltzmann prediction (e.g., a classical mass-dependent threshold) that is then measured.
- A pre-registered E_w/E_s for a new chemistry, with the predicted window, tested against an actual passive-balance autonomous system. An example would be a Titan-type chemistry predicted to operate at ~90 K, or a cold-world biosphere.
- An answer to what observation would falsify the narrowed claim rather than merely rescale E_w. Without one, the claim is unfalsifiable.

**HEADLINE: NARROWED**

(The universal ~300 K law and the foundational framing are Failed. What survives is the Narrowed claim that the temperature window scales with bond energies and solvent, which is standard stat mech and not specific to Synchronism.)
