   the same tidal filling: the external field in units of a₀. Then "a partition rule" is an external field effect
   written as a membership test, and the corner's DR4 number (2.7) is not a prediction of any rule that passes.

   dimensionless numbers a rule could use (g_int/a₀, g_ext/a₀, tidal filling) plus collisionality, beside the boost

   Hyades from Draco and from a 10 kAU binary. (c) The open clusters turn out not to be in the low-acceleration
   regime once the profile is integrated instead of estimated at r_h.
