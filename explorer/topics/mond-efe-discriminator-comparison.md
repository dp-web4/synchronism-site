# Topic: MOND+EFE Numerical Comparison for TEST-01/02/05

## Question

Do TEST-01, TEST-02, and TEST-05 actually discriminate Synchronism from MOND+EFE, or are the predictions degenerate?

## Context

Visitor Pass 4 (researcher, 2026-05-13) identified a critical gap: the three remaining "active discriminating tests" (TEST-01 SPARC environment, TEST-02 wide binary density, TEST-05 RAR environment partition) are all environment-dependent predictions — and MOND's External Field Effect (Bekenstein & Milgrom 1984; AQUAL/QUMOND) has predicted environment-dependent gravity since 1984. The site claims these tests "directly distinguish from MOND" but does not compute the MOND+EFE prediction for any of the three observables. A research proposal was filed to the Synchronism repo on 2026-05-13.

## Why It Matters

If all three remaining active tests are MOND+EFE degenerate, the framework has zero discriminating Tier-1 predictions after TEST-04a (sign-reversed) and TEST-04 (withdrawn). That is an existential finding for the research program's Tier-1 claims — and should be stated explicitly on the site rather than implied away.

## Specific Work

1. For TEST-02 (wide binaries): Look up the QUMOND/AQUAL EFE prediction for ξ(a_ext/a₀) — the anomaly amplitude as a function of external gravitational field. Using ρ_ext → a_ext = a_ext(ρ_ext) via Poisson, what is the predicted slope? What does Synchronism predict via C(ρ_ext)? At what ρ_ext value (if any) do the two predictions diverge by more than current Gaia DR3 uncertainty?

2. For TEST-05 (RAR environment): AQUAL/QUMOND predict σ_int(RAR) correlates with external field via EFE. What is the predicted coefficient? What does Synchronism predict? Same or different?

3. Establish the three diagnostic branches (A: degenerate, B: quantitatively distinct, C: different partitioning variable) and determine which branch applies to each test.
