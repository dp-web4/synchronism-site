## The illicit transition

The whitepaper moves from an **epistemic, observer-side filtering claim** about a classical signal to an **ontological claim** about quantum systems. The step is unlicensed because the CRT analogy carries a hidden property that quantum systems are experimentally known to lack.

**Hidden property: a joint distribution over all sampling choices.**

- A CRT has one underlying raster. Every viewing mode (picture, bands, dot) is a function of that single trajectory. All the outcomes coexist in one joint probability space, and you could compute them all from the same hidden state.
- That property is what "same process, different perception" and "reality is always definite" actually assert.
- Bell and Kochen-Specker results show that for incompatible settings, no such joint distribution is consistent with the data. You conceded this in Round 3 and withdrew the Bell resolution.
- The analogy's one load-bearing feature is therefore the one thing the experiments rule out for local, non-contextual models.

**A second break: "unpredictable" has different sources.**

- The CRT dot is unpredictable only because the witness lacks the clock phase. Read the clock and the uncertainty vanishes.
- Quantum randomness has no accessible clock. Hidden-variable completions that exist (Bohmian mechanics) are nonlocal and have equilibrium-forbidden access to the variables.
- §5.4's "no information travels, they were synchronized from the start" is just the local-hidden-variable story, which is excluded.

## The model borrows what it claims to replace

- §5.2 uses Ψ = Σαᵢψᵢ, decoherence, and environmental coupling.
- §5.5 and §5.4 invoke the Zeno effect, QND measurement, weak measurement, error correction, and GHZ states.
- Each of these is defined inside the Hilbert-space formalism.
- Synchronism offers no independent derivation of any of them. It re-labels results of standard QM and then declares the mystery dissolved.

That is re-description, not replacement. The relabeling also changes nothing in the predictions, so it can't be tested against QM.

## The missing piece that blocks (B)

The model needs a **dynamics** that does three things:

1. It produces Born-rule statistics for incompatible measurement contexts.
2. It gives S > 2 and states which Bell assumption it drops: locality, measurement independence, or realism.
3. It has an explicit coupling term showing how an M2 measurement changes the pattern.

Without that, "pattern autonomy" and "non-participatory universe" are assertions, not results.

## Alternative interpretation covering the same phenomena

Everything §5.1 legitimately shows is covered by two established ideas:

- **Time-averaging and aliasing**, which is signal processing, covers the M1 sampling intuition.
- **Decoherence plus a standard interpretation** (Bohmian, many-worlds, or QBism) covers what the model borrows.

The Synchronism-specific content adds nothing to either.
