## Pass 1: Casual Science Enthusiast

### Summary
- **Pages visited**: 12 (landing, Start Here, Learning Paths, Tools index, First Encounter, The Core Idea, Honest Assessment, Glossary, Coherence Explorer, Equation Anatomy, Galaxy Curve Plotter, Consciousness Threshold Demo)
- **Understanding achieved**: partial
- **Would return?**: maybe
- **Overall impression**: An unusually honest website about a physics idea that didn't work out — but it told me it was wrong about five times before it told me what the idea actually was.

### Journey

Clicked in from Reddit. Headline: "One Equation, Every Scale." Cool — that's a Veritasium-title. Then the very next thing on the page is the equation itself, with `tanh` and `ln` in it. I don't know what either of those is. There's a parameter list underneath (tanh = "S-shaped saturation function", ln = "compression function") which is nice, but I still don't know what a saturation function saturates. I skimmed past.

Then the landing page scoreboard: **0 predictions confirmed, 6 refutations, 0 of 6 "validated" claims survived audit**, and the four coverage areas are labelled "reparametrization", "Failed", "reparametrization", "Speculative—untestable". My honest first reaction, about 40 seconds in: *wait, so is this a theory, or a post-mortem of a theory? Why did someone link this?* I didn't know what "reparametrization" meant. I also noticed "3,308 AI-to-AI adversarial collaboration sessions" and did not know what to do with that — was this made by AI? Is that the story here?

Clicked **Start Here** ("Beginner Path — Step 1 of 6"). The crowd-vs-marching-band picture is great, I got it immediately: density turns a crowd into a band. But the same page, still Step 1, has "γ = 2/√Ncorr", an "audited-negative badge", "circularity: Ncorr is back-solved", "R²=0.14", and "Abrikosov-Gor'kov pair-breaking from 1960". I skipped all of that. And "MOND" shows up several times ("shared with MOND") with no hint what it is — I assumed it was another theory but had to go to the glossary later to confirm.

**Learning Paths**: The Beginner track is 6 steps / ~30 min, which is the right size. But the first thing on the page is a boxed disclaimer, dated 2026-08-05, about how "the two groupings are not equivalent" and which 10 pages the topic tracks omit. That's a note to the people who run the site, not to me. Also says none of the paths include the interactive tools, which seemed odd since the tools are the most beginner-friendly thing here.

**First Encounter** (Step 2) is the best page I saw. Marching band again, "neutron star is 10¹⁴× denser than lead", and MRH explained as "a single atom doesn't feel the Andromeda galaxy." That's exactly my level. Small glitch: the progress indicator reads "Section 1 of 7ρ (presence)" — the ρ ran into the "7". Only one section loaded per page, so the 10-minute intro is a lot of clicking — fine.

**The Core Idea** (Step 3) drops back into deep water: "Hill coefficient", "ρcrit ≈ A × Vflat²", "no critical point ⇒ no universality ⇒ no Landau machinery", "converges to non-cooperative behavior". Beginner path, step 3. I bounced.

**Tools** index: seven tools, each tagged with a category like "Model Explainer", "Artifact Lesson", "Reparametrization". Those tags meant nothing to me. Two tools are described as teaching *refuted* formulas on purpose ("intentional failure demonstration") — I genuinely wasn't sure why I'd want to play with a slider that computes a wrong formula. The Consciousness demo is flagged right on the index as "the site's largest credibility liability", which — okay, I clicked it, obviously.

**Coherence Explorer**: sliders move, curve moves, worked fine. The equation-anatomy page later told me tanh just means "S-curve" and ln means "grows slowly", and once I knew that the slider made sense: the sharpness slider makes the S steeper or flatter, the other slider slides it left-right. But the default sharpness is 0.49 here, while Start Here and the glossary talk about γ = 2 for single electrons and tiny γ for crystals — so which number is *the* number? And the density slider says "1.00" — one what? One neutron star? Also the tool's own caption says the sharpness rule "runs backwards against real physics." So the recommended entry-point tool tells me it's backwards.

**Equation Anatomy**: best explanation of the equation on the site — dimmer switch, S-shape, slow-growth. Then a big red "Audited-Negative: Sign Inverted for All Collective Systems" box. I understood "the formula is backwards" but not why, or whether the rest still counts.

**Galaxy Curve Plotter**: picked NGC 3198. Five lines. I actually understood the story: gray dashed = what gravity from visible stuff predicts, blue dots = what we see, and they don't match (that's the dark matter puzzle — I've seen this in videos!). Then the purple line — the theory's actual prediction — sits right on the gray line because "the disk never gets dense enough for the coherence boost to turn on." And the amber line is labelled "drawn to fit, not computed from the theory." That's a strikingly honest chart. But the tools index said the plotter compares "four models"; the plotter shows five curves.

**Consciousness Threshold Demo**: "Eight approaches converge on C ≈ 0.50" — then the next paragraph explains 0.50 is just the middle of 0-to-1, so of course they land there. Then a list of dated corrections (07-07, 07-08) about a removed claim and a mis-cited session. Felt like reading a changelog, not a demo. "PCI* = 0.31" — no idea.

**Honest Assessment**: the title literally says "of a Failed Framework." Respect. Mostly over my head (3.3σ, 17.95σ, ΔBIC=+184 — the glossary's σ entry "2σ interesting, 3σ evidence, 5σ discovery" rescued me a bit). One sentence near the end was the clearest thing on the entire site: **"The honest contribution is the audit itself, not the physics."** That should be the second sentence on the landing page — it would have answered my 40-second "why am I here?" question.

**Glossary**: σ definition great, "Reparametrization = known physics in new notation" great, MOND defined at last. But there is **no entry for "dark matter"** — the one term a Reddit visitor is guaranteed to look up.

## Pass 3: Graduate Physics Student

### Summary
- **Pages visited**: 17 (landing, Core Idea, Coherence Function, γ Parameter, Parameter Derivations, Galaxy Rotation, MOND Unification, Dark Matter, CDM Discrimination, Dark Energy & DESI, Tier 1: Existing Data, Key Claims, Honest Assessment, For Researchers, Tools index, Chemistry Correlation Explorer, Galaxy Curve Plotter)
- **Understanding achieved**: solid
- **Would return?**: maybe — as a case study in self-audit, not as physics
- **Overall impression**: The equation is a one-parameter Hill function that becomes MOND's simple μ at γ = ½, the site knows this and says so, and every "Validated" badge has already been demoted by the site's own audit — the physics is dead on arrival but the ledger is more honest than most published papers; what's left to criticise is the ledger's internal consistency.

### Journey

My colleague said "interesting AI project," so I skipped the beginner track and went straight to the equation.

**C(ρ) = tanh(γ ln(ρ/ρ_crit + 1)).** First thing I did was check the identity the Core Idea page claims: with x = ρ/ρ_crit, tanh(γ ln(1+x)) = [(1+x)^{2γ} − 1]/[(1+x)^{2γ} + 1]. That's just tanh(u) = (e^{2u}−1)/(e^{2u}+1) with e^{2u} = (1+x)^{2γ}. Correct, trivially. At γ = ½ it's x/(x+2) = (x/2)/(1+x/2) = μ_simple(x/2). Also correct, identically for all x. So the "coherence function" is a shifted Hill function of index 2γ, and MOND's simple interpolating function is a member of the family. I verified dC/dx = γ(1−C²)/(1+x): slope γ at the origin, monotone decreasing, concave everywhere, no inflection. The Coherence Function page states all of this — and draws the right conclusion: **no critical point, no order parameter, "compander not phase transition."** The Ising m = tanh(βJzm) analogy is explicitly disowned on the same page (C is evaluated directly, not self-consistently). That's the correct physics. I'd have written it the same way in a referee report; they wrote it about themselves.

**γ = 2/√N_corr.** The γ Parameter page says it before I could: fluctuation 1/√N is a width, γ sits in a sharpness slot, so the most collective systems (BCS, N_corr → ∞) get the *flattest* curve and C → 0. The formula predicts superconductors are the least "coherent" objects in the universe. Then "no protocol for independently measuring N_corr in any system — every published value is asserted or back-fit from γ." So γ is a free parameter with a story attached. The "2 from phase-space dimensionality (3 position + 3 momentum)" is not an argument — 6 DOF gives √6, not 2, under any reading I can construct.

**Parameter Derivations** is the page I came for. Verdict on the page itself: "Zero parameters in this chain have independent first-principles derivations." I checked the numbers: a₀ = cH₀/2π = 1.042×10⁻¹⁰ m/s² at H₀ = 67.4 ✓; Σ₀ = cH₀/(4π²G) = 119 M☉/pc² ✓ and it is literally a₀/(2πG), so a₀ and Σ₀ are one number, as the page says ✓; back-solving H₀ from Milgrom's a₀ = 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ gives 77.6 km/s/Mpc ✓ — above both CMB and ladder, which the site notes. cH₀ ≈ 6.5×10⁻¹⁰ vs a₀ ≈ 1.2×10⁻¹⁰: ratio 5.4, and 2π = 6.28 is one of a dozen O(1) factors within 15%. The "2π from spherical horizon geometry" is post-hoc. Badge says "Reparametrization — Dimensional Analysis," which is honest. A ≈ 0.029 vs the stated formula giving 4.6×10⁻⁵ (600×) with three incompatible V-exponents (+0.5 stated, +2 in code, −2 required) — badge "Audited-Negative — Chain-of-Custody Failure." Also honest. φ "fitted-then-named." Honest.

So the question shifts from "are the derivations right?" (there are none, and the site says so) to "is the ledger of *failures* right?" That's where I found real problems.

**Galaxy Curve Plotter** — I read the formulas rather than the pictures. Violet curve: ρ(r) = Σ(r)/2h with h = 0.3 kpc, ρ_crit = 0.029·V_flat². For NGC 3198 (V ≈ 150 km/s) that's ρ_crit ≈ 650 M☉/pc³ if the units are M☉/pc³ and km/s (the page never states them). The solar neighbourhood is ~0.1 M☉/pc³; a disk at 100 M☉/pc² over 600 pc is ~0.17 M☉/pc³. So x ~ 3×10⁻⁴ and C ≈ 2γx ≈ 10⁻³ — matches the page's "max C ≈ 0.001." The knee sits at globular-cluster-core density. This is the whole galaxy failure in one line, and the plotter shows it correctly. The MOND curve uses simple-ν, ν = ½ + √(¼ + 1/y), a₀ = 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ ✓. The Newtonian curve uses spherical enclosed mass for a disk — the page admits ~15–20% and that it "modestly exaggerates" the DM gap; for a tool whose whole point is the gap, that's a bigger caveat than its footnote position suggests. ### Journey

**The field equation.** MOND Unification gives ∇²Φ = 4πGρ/C (Appendix D) and, "equivalently," ∇·[C∇Φ] = 4πGρ, i.e. a gravitational permittivity that depends on local density, with a floor C ≥ Ω_m = 0.315 (the "bounded boost," B_max = 1/Ω_m = 3.17). This is not close to refracted gravity — it *is* refracted gravity: ∇·[ε(ρ)∇Φ] = 4πGρ with ε → ε₀ in the low-density limit and ε → 1 in the high-density limit, fitted to disk galaxies in Cesare, Diaferio, Matsakos & Angus (2020, A&A 637, A70), to ellipticals in Cesare et al. (2022), with a covariant version published (~2023). The site's ε₀ is 0.315; RG fits land ε₀ in roughly the 0.25–0.7 range. I searched For Researchers, Galaxy Rotation, MOND Unification, Dark Matter, and Honest Assessment for "refracted," "permittivity," "Matsakos," "Diaferio," "Cesare" — nothing. The Pass 3 reviewer flagged this as a possibility; I'm stating it as fact. Consequences cascade:

- "Citable negative result #1 — local-density no-go" claims density-keyed gravity modifications fail via three routes and that there is "no prior quantified instance" of volumetric ρ failing as organizing variable. RG has published *successful* SPARC-class fits with a density-keyed ε. Either those fits are the counterexample and the no-go is wrong as stated, or the no-go is specific to *this* compander + *this* floor, in which case it is a parameter-fit result, not a class result. The page cites Burrage–Copeland–Millington 2017 as a counterexample and withdrew an unqualified "no prior art" line on 2026-07-27; it now needs to do the same for RG, which is a far closer match (same equation, same state variable).
- The **one transferable result** on this site is the TEST-10 tail: an outer f_DM of 0.927 in SPARC needs B ≥ 13.7, and *any* universal permittivity floor ε₀ caps B at 1/ε₀. For RG's published floors that is B_max ≈ 1.4–4, f_DM,max ≈ 0.3–0.75. That is a real constraint on universal-ε₀ refracted gravity — and the site cannot claim it because it has not identified what it is constraining.
- The dark-energy sector's "Completion A forces Einstein–de Sitter; Completion B: 0 of 192 γ values reach DESI's quadrant" has a published competitor completion (covariant RG). A reader will ask why the site's two ad-hoc completions and not the one in the literature.

**Does anything differ from MOND + ΛCDM?** Yes, two things — and neither is on the registry:

1. **EFE = 0.** MOND Unification states it as a "structural prediction," and Galaxy Rotation says "the fork [quadrature vs. division] is what currently blocks registering EFE = 0." That blocker is a category error. Only one of the three "force laws" is a field theory — the permittivity equation. That equation is *linear in Φ for a given ρ*, so external fields superpose and there is no external-field effect in the MOND (AQUAL/QUMOND, μ(|∇Φ|/a₀)) sense. EFE = 0 is not fork-dependent; it is forced by the form. And it is testable now: Crater II (σ_obs ≈ 2.7 ± 0.3 km/s; MOND-with-EFE ≈ 2.1, isolated-MOND ≈ 4), Antlia II, and the dSph population as a function of Galactocentric distance. This is the single sharpest MOND-vs-framework discriminator available at $0 and the site's "zero active discriminating tests" page does not mention it. (It is, again, shared with RG — but "shared with RG, distinct from MOND" is a *different* and more interesting ledger position than "shared with MOND".)
2. **a₀(z) = cH(z)/2π.** Listed on MOND Unification as a unique prediction, "disfavored 2σ–6σ against Ciocan et al. 2026," and absent from Tier 1, Test Roadmap and Top Decisive Tests. It has no TEST-ID. On its face the sign is wrong: H(z=1)/H₀ ≈ 1.8 pushes z ~ 1 disks *deeper* into the low-acceleration regime (more boost), whereas Genzel et al. 2017/2020 and later work see *more* baryon-dominated, declining curves at high z. That is the kind of clean structural refutation the ledger should headline — and it is also prior art: a₀ ∝ cH(z) appears in Milgrom's own discussions of the a₀–cH₀ coincidence and in several "MOND from cosmology" proposals, so the row is *shared-with-prior-art* and *refuted*, not "unique."

**The Solar System is not a 17.95σ test — it is a division-by-C test.** TEST-25 uses tanh(γ ln(1+x)) as a QUMOND interpolating function in *acceleration* (x = g/a₀), which is the C(a) object, and correctly finds the Desmond–Hees–Famaey 2024 RAR–Cassini tension (they get 8.7σ; the site gets ~2× that with fixed γ and profiled a₀). Fine as far as it goes — but it is a test of MOND with a particular IF, and the site says so. The framework's *own* object is C(ρ), and on the division branch g = g_bar/C(ρ_local): at 1 AU, ρ_local is interplanetary, C sits on the floor, and the prediction is G_eff = 3.17 G for every planet. Kepler's third law kills that at a level no σ describes. On the quadrature branch the prediction is Newton exactly. So "which branch" is decided by the Solar System in one line — division is dead, quadrature is Newton-plus-nothing — and the site instead reports a 17.95σ result on a third object. Same class as the Pass 3 point about flat rotation curves: the structural kill is cheaper and stronger than the statistic reported.

**Dark energy.** Substituting G_eff = G/C(ρ̄) into Friedmann gives ρ_DE ≡ ρ_m(1−C)/C, with C₀ = Ω_m forced so that ρ_DE,0 = ρ_Λ. w runs monotonically from −2γ (past) to −1 (future); sign(w₀+1) = sign(wₐ); γ = ½ is exactly ΛCDM; the galaxy fit gives 0.489.
