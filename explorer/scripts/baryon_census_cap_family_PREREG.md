# PREREG — Is the baryon census a convention-free kill of the cap family B ≤ Ω_m/Ω_b = 6.39?

Written 2026-11-14 before the group/cluster fetch returned. The McGaugh+2010 method had already been read (galaxy leg:
V_c → V_500 with f_V = 1.1 from NFW; M_500 = 204552·V_500³ at H0 = 72). I hold memory values for group gas fractions
(Sun+2009 ~0.08–0.10 at r500), so P3/P4 are not blind to the literature, only to today's quotes.

Definition. Spherical: B(r) = g_obs/g_bar = M_tot(<r)/M_b(<r) = 1/f_b(<r). The cap fails wherever f_b(<r) < Ω_b/Ω_m
= 0.1565 (Planck 2018: Ω_b h² 0.02237, Ω_c h² 0.1200 → Ω_m/Ω_b = 6.36; site uses 6.39).

P1. Galaxy leg of McGaugh+2010 needs V_c held flat out to R_500 (≈ 6–10 × the last measured radius). (Read; not blind.)
P2. Positive control: a mock disc built under a 6.39-capped MOND ν (no halo), with its V_c read at R_last, gives a
    census f_d < 0.5 from McGaugh's formula. The model's own f_b(<r) ≥ 1/6.39 at every r. So the galaxy leg reports a
    deficit in a universe where the cap holds, and it cannot test the cap.
P3. Hydrostatic groups (T ≈ 1–2 keV, M500 ~ 1e13.5–1e14): f_b(<r500) (gas + stars) ≈ 0.08–0.11 → B ≈ 9–12.
P4. Massive clusters (X-COP, M500 ~ 5e14–1e15): B(<r500) ≈ 6.5–8, not a 3σ exceedance on its own.
P5. The group exceedance clears 3σ after (i) stellar mass ×2, (ii) a 20% per-system mass error, and (iii) hydrostatic
    bias (the bias increases B, so no allowance is taken). It is NOT robust to an undetected baryon component of
    ≈ 0.4–0.9 × detected. That is the same escape MOND uses in clusters.
Kill reading: a class kills the cap only if its B exceeds 6.39 at ≥ 3σ, with masses measured inside detected emission.
It must also survive (i)–(iii), with the undetected-baryon allowance printed as the remaining condition.
