  Validated ("quantitative match") from Strongly Supported ("consistent but not uniquely
  predicted"). The /prediction-tracker assigns 8 items to Validated; 7 of them are

  Reparametrization by the taxonomy's own wording. The Pass 4 perennial "0 unique confirmed
  vs 8 Validated" contradiction is an assignment bug, not a definitional one. Rename is
  secondary; reclassification is the fix. Proposed: add "Confirmed" badge for the
  currently-empty (and honestly-so) "novel + confirmed" category.

  notice" prompt contradicts the math. Per-tool audit revealed: MOND Comparator is

  system); Galaxy Plotter's γ=2 assumption is not disclosed. Proposed "Three observations"
  pattern (behavior / anchor / caveat) per tool, and tiered guidance (card / tooltip /
  expandable advanced notes) for multi-persona readability.

   deliberate label redefinition.)

4. Does any interactive tool on the site honestly show a *failure* of the framework? The
   Galaxy Plotter shows success (Newtonian vs observed gap closed). The γ Calculator shows
   apparent success (six presets land in sensible regimes). None shows a failure case —

   framework prediction vs reality and let the user see the 53%-off regime directly would
   be more honest than any prose disclaimer.

## So What?

Both findings are concrete, auditable, and directly address friction that visitors have been
reporting for weeks. Neither is speculative research — they're diagnoses of specific
inconsistencies between two pages on the same site. Both point at the same deeper root: the
site has no documented promotion criterion (methodology page missing; Pass 4 critical for
five logs running). This is advancement, not just documentation: it converts two vague
"something is off" complaints into named, assignable fixes with expected outcomes. If the
maintainer applies the reclassification proposed in the badge finding, the "0 vs 8"
contradiction that has consumed visitor-log space for a month resolves without new research.
The tool-guidance audit similarly redirects implementation effort from "write more prompts"
(low-impact, compounds wrongness) to "audit existing prompts for correctness" (one-commit
fixes that restore internal consistency).

Both findings also name a single structural gap the ecosystem has been walking around:
**the site has no visible promotion rule.** The badge assignment drift, the tool-guidance
drift, and the missing methodology page are three views of the same problem. Future sessions
should consider whether the methodology page is the right lever, or whether a "Promotion
Criterion" sidebar on /research-philosophy (embedded rather than standalone) would be
more resilient.
