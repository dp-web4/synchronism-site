## 2. Where exactly the claim fails

**Failure point: the step from "ratio constraint" to "universal law of organized complexity."** The chain is:
1. Memory needs E_s/k_BT ≳ 40. This is **valid**.
2. Dynamics need E_w/k_BT ≲ 10. This is **an assumption**.
3. Ordinary matter has E_w, E_s fixed by atomic constants. This is **valid only as a range**. Your own numbers show E_w spans ~100 K (vdW) to ~2400 K (H-bond), a factor of ~25. The corresponding floor spans ~10 K to ~240 K.
4. Therefore the window is ~300 K. This **does not follow** from step 3.

Step 4 only follows after conditioning on water-based chemistry. Once you concede that Titan-type vdW chemistry is not excluded, the "universal ~300 K" claim is gone. What survives is "T_window scales with the chemistry's bond energies," which is a tautology of Boltzmann statistics.

**Alternative model explaining the same data:** a **liquid-solvent-plus-chemistry-selection account**. Life at ~300 K is explained by liquid water's range, the energy scale of C/H/N/O/P bonds, and Earth's orbital distance. No temperature-as-law is needed. This alternative:
- predicts the same observed window,
- allows other windows for other solvents (ammonia, hydrocarbons), which the §4.7 "law" forbids,
- needs no new Synchronism content.

By parsimony, it wins unless Synchronism makes a prediction the alternative does not.
