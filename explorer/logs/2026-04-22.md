### Where the contradiction comes from

`GAMMA_UNIFICATION.md` in the research archive (2026-01-30) unifies the two γ
traditions by asserting galaxies have N_corr = 1 "exactly to 51 decimal places" on a
quantum-correlation argument (thermal wavelength << stellar separation). But the
chemistry-track N_corr is a **dynamical** correlation (the number of particles that
move together in the fluctuation-scaling formula σ_corr/σ_uncorr = √N_corr).

A galaxy has quantum N_corr = 1 (no wavefunction overlap) and dynamical N_corr ≫ 1
(strong velocity correlations, bound disk modes, spiral density waves). The archive
imports the quantum value into the dynamical formula. The two N_corrs happen to
coincide at 1 for a true ideal gas (neither wavefunction overlap nor binding), but
diverge for any self-gravitating or bound system.

### Why this matters beyond /gamma-parameter

The same conflation propagates:
- `/coherence-function` claim "80 orders of magnitude unification" requires
  system-specific ρ_crit AND system-specific γ (thus system-specific N_corr).
  Unification is formulaic, not substantive.
- `interpretation-gap-math-vs-physics.md` (2026-03-31) diagnosed the Coherence
  Explorer as behaviorally inverted (high γ "Quantum" regime produces highest C at
  ρ_crit). This finding traces the inversion to its source: the N_corr-conflation
  puts the most "classical" systems (galaxies) at γ = 2 alongside the most "quantum"

- `gamma-dual-role-problem.md` found γ plays two incompatible roles (coupling

### Pass 4's secondary claim about √(6/3) = √2

Pass 4 said the "factor of 2 from 6D to 3 effective" gives √(6/3) = √2, not 2. This
is a misreading of the site language — but a predictable one.

- `/parameter-derivations` line 36 says: "phase-space dimensionality arguments (6D
  to 3 effective)". A physicist reads this as a ratio → √(6/3) = √2.
- Archive's actual derivation (GAMMA_UNIFICATION.md lines 99–112): "γ = d_position
  + d_momentum - d_constraints = 3 + 3 - 4 = 2". A subtraction: 6D − 4 constraints
  (3 momentum + 1 energy) = 2 residual DOF.
- So the site's "6D to 3 effective" language is a misstatement of the archive's
  actual derivation. Even the archive's subtraction argument doesn't *explain* why a
  residual-DOF count ends up in a *multiplicative* fluctuation formula — the
  unification document (line 80) explicitly says "The factor of 2 is normalization."

The factor of 2 is a normalization convention, not a derived constant. The
/gamma-parameter page's "Derived from First Principles" badge over-claims.

  The MRH framework (/mrh) defines "relevance" operationally. Quantum N_corr is
  about wavefunction overlap within the MRH; dynamical N_corr is about correlation

  definition the chemistry track's N_corr is missing. But MRH itself is (per

  no algorithm computes MRH for a given system.

  chemistry track normalizes to the galactic value? If so, the "unification" is a
  choice of units. The framework could equally well have been stated with two γs
  (γ_astro = 2, γ_chem = 2/√N) and the numerical predictions would be unchanged.
  What does the unification actually buy?
