**Verdict:** I found no published paper with this exact construction. I believe it is probably not published in this form, but my search was only two queries.

**What the construction is.** It is a generic chain-rule bound. LLR limits Ġ/G. The Sun's velocity through the local density field, v⊙·∇ln ρ̄_ℓ, converts that into a limit on d ln f/d ln ρ. Any model where G depends on a smoothed ambient density reduces to this. That includes chameleon, symmetron and other environment-dependent scalar-tensor theories, with f supplied by the model. It is not a new model.

**Closest published work I found:**
- Lagos & Zhu, "Gravitational couplings in Chameleon models", JCAP 06 (2020) 061, arXiv:2003.01038. They keep the time dependence of the galactic environment and compute the induced evolution of G_N and G_gw. They find it strongly suppressed in the weak-field, non-relativistic regime. This is the nearest match. I did not see an explicit Sun-trajectory or density-gradient calculation in the snippet, so that is uncertain.
- Khoury & Weltman, "Chameleon Cosmology", astro-ph/0309411 (2004, Phys. Rev. D 69, 044026). The volume and page come from memory, not from the search. It is the background for density-dependent scalar mass and screening.
- Uzan, "Constancy of the Constants of Nature" (gr-qc/0110118) and its update (arXiv:1111.0092). These are reviews of Ġ/G bounds and of how they are model dependent.
- Hofmann, Müller & Biskupek, A&A 522, L5 (2010). This is an LLR bound of Ġ/G = (−0.7 ± 3.8)×10⁻¹³ yr⁻¹. I did not check the citation details beyond the abstract page.
- gr-qc/0610035, "Can (dG/dt)/G Bound the Local Cosmological Dynamics?". It argues that Ġ/G limits can't simply be reinterpreted as constraints on local cosmological dynamics. That caveat is relevant here.

**Caveats on the claim itself:**
- The claim that the bound is "independent of the form of f" holds only if G responds quasi-statically to the smoothed density at the Sun's position. Screening (chameleon or symmetron) breaks this. The Earth–Moon system and the Sun may be screened, and the effective response then involves the local potential, not ρ̄_ℓ. Lagos & Zhu's suppression result points the same way.
- The numbers need checking. Take v⊙ ≈ 20 km/s ≈ 20 pc/Myr and a gradient scale of about ℓ. Then v⊙·∇ln ρ ≈ 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁶ yr⁻¹ for ℓ = 1–10 pc. A bound of 10⁻¹³ yr⁻¹ on Ġ/G then gives d ln f/d ln ρ ≲ 10⁻⁸ to 10⁻⁷, if the gradient is of order unity per ℓ. The quoted upper end of 10⁻⁵ looks generous. Real local-density gradients are also uncertain.

**Conclusion:** It is not published as a stated result, as far as I could find. It looks like a straightforward application of known Ġ/G bounds to environment-dependent G models. I'd treat novelty as low to moderate, and I'd check Lagos & Zhu in full before claiming it as new.
