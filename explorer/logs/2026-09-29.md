## So what?
This advances discovery rather than documenting state, in two ways. First, it closes the maintainer's open "→ dp"
question with the method the site itself says is the one to cite: the pin is disfavoured at 1.9–2.3σ, not refuted,
and no nuisance treatment changes that. Second, and more transferable, it shows that nuisance marginalization is not
an N_eff correction. The site (and, by analogy, the literature it defers to) has been treating "marginalized" as a
synonym for "the correlations are handled"; the tables say the galaxy-level width stays 3–7× the pooled-point width
after marginalization. The pin was the occasion; the error-bar result is the finding.
