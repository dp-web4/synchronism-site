**Assessment: the construction is numerology, and its headline number is wrong.**

**Arithmetic check.** With H₀ = 70 km/s/Mpc = 2.27×10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹, cH₀ = 6.80×10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻². Ω_m^φ = 0.3^1.618 = 0.1425. The product is a₀ ≈ 9.7×10⁻¹¹ m s⁻², not 1.2×10⁻¹⁰. That is about 20% low. Matching 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ would need an exponent of about 1.44, not 1.618. The formula therefore fails even by its own stated inputs. The simpler coincidence cH₀/2π ≈ 1.08×10⁻¹⁰ lands closer to the observed value.

**Strengths**
- It correctly notes that a₀ ~ cH₀ to within an order of magnitude, a coincidence that is real and unexplained.
- The formula is compact and makes a definite, falsifiable prediction.
- It ties a₀ to cosmological parameters, so a₀ would vary with H₀ and Ω_m. That is a testable claim, for example through redshift evolution of the baryonic Tully-Fisher relation.

**Weaknesses**
1. **No derivation.** Nothing explains why the golden ratio should appear as an exponent on a matter fraction. No action, symmetry, or dynamical mechanism is given. Ω_m^φ is a fit with a choice of exponent, which counts as a free parameter.
2. **Look-elsewhere effect.** Combinations of cH₀, Ω's, 2π, e, φ, and small rational exponents are numerous. Hitting one number within 10–20% is expected from such a search, and here it does not even hit.
3. **Time-dependent inputs.** Ω_m and H₀ are epoch-dependent. A fundamental constant that is derived from them implies that a₀ evolves. The text does not say whether that is intended, and it is in tension with the lack of evidence for evolution at the level the formula implies.
4. **Observational tension.** The H₀ tension (67–73) and the Ω_m uncertainty (0.30–0.32) shift the prediction by about 10–20%. The empirical a₀ has a statistical error of about 2% (±0.02) and a systematic uncertainty of about 20% from mass-to-light ratios. The prediction must be compared with these uncertainties explicitly.
5. **No MOND completion.** Nothing is said about the interpolating function, the relativistic extension, lensing, the CMB, or clusters, where MOND is already strained.
6. **Dimensional and conceptual gap.** Raising a dimensionless number to an irrational power is not a physical principle unless it comes from a scaling argument.
