### The Scaffolding Hypothesis

C(ρ) is not a theory — it's scaffolding. It provided a systematic way to explore density-dependent physics across scales and generated ~11 novel predictions. But the predictions don't depend on the specific equation. They survive independently, like a building survives when the scaffolding is removed.

This is actually good news: the failures (interpretation gap, regime label inversion, γ_max refutation) are problems with the scaffolding, not the building. The successes (possibly-confirmed quantum predictions, A2ACW methodology) stand without the equation.

The site's central framing — "one equation for everything" — is an artifact of the research process, not a feature of reality. A more honest and more interesting framing: "What if a wrong equation asked the right questions?"
