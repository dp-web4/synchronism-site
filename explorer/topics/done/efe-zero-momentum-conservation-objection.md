# Topic: Does EFE=0 survive momentum conservation? (Milgrom 1983's own objection)

## Question
The framework's one live non-MOND structural claim — EFE = 0, because C depends only on local ρ and
a uniform external field doesn't change ρ — asserts a pointwise algebraic multiplier on gravity with
no field equation behind it (`/mond-unification`, `/honest-assessment` both state there is no action,
no Lagrangian, no covariant formulation anywhere in the galaxy sector). A 2026-08-03 visitor persona
(leading-edge researcher pass) raised an objection that doesn't appear anywhere on the site: Milgrom
(1983) himself noted that a purely algebraic modification of gravity (multiply g by a scalar function
of local quantities, with no underlying nonlinear field equation) generically violates momentum
conservation / Newton's third law unless it is derived from an action — which is exactly why MOND
was completed a year later by AQUAL (Bekenstein & Milgrom 1984) and again by QUMOND (Milgrom 2010).

## Context
This would be a consistency failure, not a data failure — checkable with no telescope time, and
potentially closing the galaxy sector's one surviving non-inherited claim (EFE=0) on internal
grounds rather than waiting on the Chae et al. 2020/2021 vs. Freundlich/Paranjape-Sheth dispute that
TEST-05 already adjudicates empirically. `/for-researchers` already makes the parallel point that
Synchronism has "no analogous completion" to AQUAL/QUMOND (no momentum conservation, no lensing
prediction) — but that page frames it as an *incompleteness*, not as a specific *violation* the
1983 paper already flagged for exactly this class of algebraic modification.

## Why It Matters
If the objection holds, "EFE=0 predicts something MOND doesn't" (the site's one surviving
discriminator) could be sharpened to "EFE=0, if you could even write down momentum-conserving
dynamics that produce it, which this class of theory generically cannot" — a stronger and more
specific statement than the current framing, and one that costs no telescope time.

## Suggested Starting Points
- Milgrom 1983 (ApJ 270, 365 or 270, 371 — the original MOND papers) for the specific
  momentum-conservation argument against algebraic (non-Lagrangian) modifications.
- Bekenstein & Milgrom 1984 (ApJ 286, 7) — AQUAL, the completion that restores momentum conservation
  via a nonlinear Poisson equation derived from an action.
- `/mond-unification`, `/for-researchers` (artifact 1) for the current site framing of "no field
  equation" as an incompleteness rather than a specific conservation-law violation.
- Check whether the objection is fatal (no possible completion preserves EFE=0 exactly) or just
  another "owed mechanism" gap similar to the dim-4 LIV custodial-symmetry obligation in
  PREDICTIONS.md Bucket 1 (B7) — i.e., does *any* Lagrangian completion exist that reproduces
  algebraic C(ρ)·g in the weak-field limit while conserving momentum, or is this structurally
  impossible for any local-density coupling?
