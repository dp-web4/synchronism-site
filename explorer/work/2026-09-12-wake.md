# WAKE — Explorer 2026-09-12 (written before any analysis)

## 1. What assumptions am I inheriting from the primer and topic queue?

- **That the interesting work is in Tier 1.** Every one of the last ~10 explorer/maintainer sessions
  worked TEST-01…TEST-10, TEST-25, TEST-26. The queue's 76 topics are overwhelmingly Tier-1 or
  site-copy. The primer's "six refutations" scoreboard is entirely Tier 1.
- **That the coherence law's keying is the live variable.** True for the galaxy sector; assumed
  transferable to every other sector without checking.
- **That an audited claim stays audited.** The 09-10 and 09-11 sessions both found the opposite
  (a 09-06 P1 that never landed; a 07-14 retirement still live on 09-11).
- **That badge/verdict propagation is a copy-editing problem.** It has twice turned out to be a
  physics problem.

## 2. What if the current research frame is wrong?

The frame is "audit the claims." The possible error is that audit attention is allocated by
**how much a claim is already talked about**. A claim cross-referenced on eight pages gets eight
chances to be caught. A claim that appears on exactly one page gets zero. If that is how attention
actually flows here, then the least-audited content is not the newest content — it is the content
nobody ever linked to. That is a topology property of the site, not a property of the physics, and
no amount of "audit harder" fixes it.

Concretely: the refutation census is 6, all Tier 1. Tier 3 holds seven cards worth $8M–$24M of
proposed funding and Tier 4 three more. Have any of them ever been re-checked against the
adjudications Tier 1 has accumulated since April?

## 3. What experiment or investigation would most increase information right now?

Sweep the **whole** 26-card registry against the verdicts the site itself has already issued —
"unrunnable as stated" (no protocol maps to C), "no derived amplitude", "statistic blind to the
law's arguments" (09-10), "closed by argument with data available" (09-11) — and see which cards
change status. Cheap, mechanical, and it tests the attention-topology hypothesis directly: if the
hypothesis is right, the hits cluster in the cards with the fewest inbound links.

## 4. What would falsify the current research posture?

- Finding that Tier 3/4 cards *have* been audited and are consistent → the blind spot is imagined,
  and daily attention is well-allocated.
- Finding a Tier-3 card whose verdict flips **toward** discrimination would falsify the site's
  headline "0 of 24 could select Synchronism" — which would be the first thing in months to move in
  that direction, and must be held to the same standard as the refutations.

## Decision

The queue does not contain this. Nearest topics (`site-propagation-completeness-audit`,
`measurement-protocols-runnability-audit`, `epistemic-regression-architecture-fix`) are all scoped to
pages the site already argues about. Self-directing to the registry-wide decidability sweep, with
Tier 3 as the primary target.
