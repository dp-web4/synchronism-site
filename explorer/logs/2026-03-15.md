## Key Findings

- **`ncorr-operational-definition-audit.md`**: The Session #25 "derivation" is mathematically correct but misframed — it derives γ as a fluctuation scaling factor, then identifies it with the coupling constant in C(ρ) without justification. In standard mean-field theory, coupling scales linearly with coordination number (βJz ∝ z), not as 1/√z. The identification of γ_fluctuation with γ_coupling is the framework's deepest unresolved theoretical issue. The five measurement methods from Session #26 are credible in principle but no genuine blind prediction using independently measured N_corr has been demonstrated for any domain.

- **`entity-criterion-pdg-audit.md`**: The criterion Γ < m catches exactly one particle from the PDG — the f₀(500)/σ meson (Γ/m ≈ 1.22), which has been genuinely controversial for 60 years. All other established particles pass. But the criterion is less novel than claimed: it restates the well-known practical distinction that Breit-Wigner parametrizations break down when Γ ~ m. The "derivation" from the oscillation basis fails because the oscillation basis cannot itself be derived from the framework's own transfer rules (scalar diffusion is dissipative, not oscillatory). The criterion is better described as a "novel ontological interpretation" than a "novel prediction."
