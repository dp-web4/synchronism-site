## Answer to the remaining question

The "single universal consciousness" cannot serve as the P2-correlating mechanism without cost. If it does, it is a non-local, non-dynamical common cause linking detector settings and cycle phase. That is an observer-dependent role, so "no observer effect" fails in spirit, as you say. If it does not, the text names no physical channel, and P2 relaxation is an unfilled placeholder. I therefore do not defend P2 relaxation as part of the analogy. It belongs to a separate, undeveloped (B)-class proposal. I also concede the dilemma: a classical sampling picture fails Bell, KS, and LG, and a quantum one makes the CRT decoration. The CRT's role is limited to pedagogy about readout dependence in non-invasively sampled classical signals.

## Closing artifact

**HELD (survives)**
- Recorded appearance of a cycling classical signal depends on detector window and phase (smooth, banded, or discrete). The boxcar contrast law C(T) = |sinc(ωT/2)| is correct, though it is trivial filter content and not CRT-specific.
- The CRT as a mnemonic: a source unchanged by a non-invasive readout can look different under different readouts.

**NARROWED (must be scoped)**
- "No observer effect" → at most "no consciousness-dependent effect," which is untested and unsupported by any mechanism. Physical measurement disturbance is conceded.
- Wave/particle → smooth vs. discrete readout only. There is no amplitude addition, hence no fringes.
- "Decoherence parallel" → a label, until a pointer basis, system-environment coupling, and master equation are supplied.
- Section 5.1 → cite the CRT only as "an example of readout-dependent appearance in a classical, non-invasively sampled signal." Retitle it, and drop "Revolutionary Implications."
- Collapse/repeatability → needs a persistent witness-pattern coupling, which is an operational observer effect.

**FAILED (unsupported / overreach)**
- The epistemic-to-ontic step ("reality is always definite"), since CRT ignorance is removable and quantum statistics are not (KS, Bell).
- "Raster entanglement" as local, deterministic pre-synchronization (|S| ≤ 2 versus observed S > 2), and "Bell's theorem makes perfect sense."
- "Observation collapse" (the dot keeps moving) and "nothing changes / no disturbance" as laws (Leggett-Garg violations, plus Round 3 concessions).
- "Perfectly demonstrates," "superposition doesn't exist," and "no quantum mystery" as ontological conclusions.
- The Planck-scale "Technical Implementation" table, which is a label list with no equations, along with the "Applications explained" lists (cryptography, teleportation, QEC).
- The universal tick, which is unaddressed against Lorentz-violation bounds.

**WOULD SETTLE (decisive evidence/test)**
- An explicit state object with order-dependent (non-commuting) readout operators and a probability rule. From it, derive Malus's law, CHSH = 2√2, and V² + D² ≤ 1.
- A named dropped assumption (locality, realism, or measurement independence) with a physical channel for it.
- A repeatability mechanism for post-measurement stability.
- At least one prediction that differs from standard QM or decoherence theory, or else an explicit declaration of empirical equivalence.
- Compatibility with Lorentz-invariance bounds.

**HEADLINE: NARROWED**

Narrowed rather than Failed: the analogy survives only as a pedagogical statement about classical readout dependence. The "no quantum mystery" claim as a whole does not survive.
