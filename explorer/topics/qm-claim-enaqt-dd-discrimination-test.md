# Topic: QM Claim — Can Any Version Escape DD + ENAQT?

## Question

The QM resynchronization claim ("resync outperforms isolation") is already satisfied by two independent established regimes: dynamical decoupling (DD) and environment-assisted quantum transport (ENAQT). Is there *any* version of the Synchronism reframe that makes a specific T₂ ratio or transport efficiency prediction that departs from standard filter-function / Lindblad predictions?

More precisely: given the MRH-based ontology (coherence = synchronization; decoherence = phase drift; entities = recurring patterns), can you derive a specific bath spectral density S(ω) and pulse-sequence protocol where MRH predicts a different T₂(N) or Φ(N) than the corresponding filter-function / Redfield calculation?

## Why It Matters

If no version of the MRH-based protocol escapes both DD and ENAQT, the QM claim should be explicitly reclassified from "Reparametrization — maps to dynamical decoupling" to "Reparametrization — subsumed by open-quantum-systems physics (DD + ENAQT)." This would be the formal closure of the QM prediction track, parallel to how the galaxy track closed on MOND/EFE degeneracy.

If a derivable distinction does exist, it would be the first surviving non-trivial quantum prediction — worth specifying precisely.
