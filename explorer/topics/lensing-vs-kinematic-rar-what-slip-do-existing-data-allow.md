# Topic: What gravitational slip do existing lensing and kinematic RAR data allow at low acceleration?

## Question
For the framework's own boost cap (1/Ω_m = 3.17) to pass the reliable KiDS-1000 lensing bins, light would have to respond
to the potential more strongly than matter does: lensing boost = B(1+η)/2 with **η ≥ 1.5 at a ×10 baryon allowance and
η ≥ 3.9 at ×5** (maintainer 2026-11-11, `maintainer/scripts/efe_free_corner_which_acceleration.py` section 3). **Do
published comparisons of lensing-inferred and kinematics-inferred accelerations already bound η in the overlap range
(g_bar ≈ 10⁻¹²–10⁻¹¹ m/s²), and at what level?**

## Context
On 2026-11-10 the site made the lensing exclusion of the cap conditional on a light-deflection rule the framework has not
written. On 2026-11-11 the three readings were worked through: light on the boosted potential (cap exceeded), light on
visible matter only (no lensing excess, excluded at 7–11σ), unseen matter doing the lensing (the dark-matter fork). The
one remaining door is a slip larger than GR's. It is small and probably closed by data that already exist, but nobody
here has read them for this purpose.

## Why It Matters
It decides whether "no reading of light saves the cap" is a complete statement or has a hole, and it replaces the flat
×5 / ×10 allowances with something measured.

## Suggested Starting Points
- Brouwer et al. 2021 (arXiv:2106.11677): where the lensing RAR and the SPARC kinematic RAR overlap, quote their own
  statement of agreement verbatim and turn it into an interval on η. Sec. 5.4's hot-gas test (isothermal halo, M_gas = M⋆
  inside 100 h₇₀⁻¹ kpc) is on disk in the tar from 2026-10-04: use the paper's profile instead of a flat factor (the
  explorer's own open thread from 2026-11-10).
- Mistele et al. 2024 (lensing RAR to larger radii and for early/late types); strong-lensing plus stellar-dynamics slip
  measurements in ellipticals (Bolton et al. 2006; Schwab et al. 2010; Collett et al. 2018) for the high-acceleration end.
  The maintainer has not read any of these for this question; retrieve and quote.
- Boran, Desai, Kahya & Woodard 2018 (arXiv:1710.06168) for the gravitational-wave side: what exactly must be shared
  between light and gravitational waves, and to what precision.
- Pre-register the direction: my expectation is η = 1 within about ±0.5 in the overlap, which closes the door there but
  says nothing below 10⁻¹³ m/s², where only lensing reaches.
