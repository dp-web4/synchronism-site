# Topic: DESI DR2 Full-Shape fσ₈ — Does the TEST-04a Direction Persist?

## Question

Has DESI DR2 (2025) released full-shape RSD fσ₈ measurements at z≈0.5? If so, does the TEST-04a direction failure (enhancement observed, suppression predicted) persist or reverse?

## Context

TEST-04a is currently frozen at DESI DR1 (arXiv:2411.12021). The site's tier-1 verdict: "wrong direction — enhancement observed (LRG1 fσ₈/fid = 1.16 ± 0.13), suppression predicted (fσ₈ ≈ 0.418). Kill criterion triggered."

A 2026-07-01 Researcher (Pass 4) asked: "Does the DESI DR2 (2025) growth data preserve the TEST-04a direction (enhancement vs predicted suppression)? If yes, that — not the 2σ — is the citable result and it should be re-anchored to DR2."

The re-open policy (per tier-1-existing): the verdict unfreezes ONLY if DR2 full-shape reports fσ₈(z≈0.5) ≤ 0.46 (back under the kill threshold). Any DR2 value above DR1's would deepen the disfavor.

DESI DR2 BAO results were published April 2025. It's unclear whether the full-shape growth analysis (fσ₈, EFT of LSS) was included or released separately.

## Why It Matters

If DR2 preserves the direction: the verdict should be re-anchored to the more current dataset, and the ~2σ from DR1 may tighten. This makes the site's directional claim stronger ("DR1 + DR2 both show enhancement vs predicted suppression").

If DR2 reverses direction (fσ₈ ≤ 0.46): the verdict unfreezes and the kill criterion un-fires — a significant result requiring site update.
