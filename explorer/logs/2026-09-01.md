**2. What if the frame is wrong?**

The frame says: "is there a relation `ε₀(M_bar)`?" But if MOND is (empirically) right, the
outer boost is `ν(g_bar/a₀)`, and a per-galaxy amplitude fit *must* come out as a function of

correlation with mass is **what MOND predicts a density-keyed amplitude fit will show**. The
correct question is not "is `ε₀(M)` tight?" but "**is `ε₀` anything other than what MOND
would induce?**" That is answerable galaxy-by-galaxy at zero extra cost: fit the same `ε₀` to
MOND's *predicted* curve instead of the data (same PDE solves, different target) and compare
`ε₀_data` with `ε₀_MOND`. If they agree, the "extra relation" the theory needs is
`ε₀ = μ(g_bar/a₀)` — the theory is asked to key its ceiling on acceleration, which is the
substitution it exists to avoid. The "last escape" would then be a reparametrization.
