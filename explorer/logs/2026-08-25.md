## Where I was wrong, and what caught it

1. **The external-density-effect closure.** I wrote the verdict ("~10⁻⁸, 6 orders below noise") into
   the script's print statements before running it. The computed table contradicted the prose in the
   same output — Crater II 0.11, Antlia II 0.40 under a flat corona. Rewrote with a real β-model
   profile; the honest answer is 8.5% max, still no power, but for a completely different reason
   (it is 84% correlated with the MOND EFE) and by 1.5 orders of magnitude, not 6.
   **Lesson: a script whose conclusion is in the print statements cannot fail.** That is the same
   defect this site documents in NotebookLM's `status` check.
2. **The vertical lever.** First pass differenced ρ against `g_z` alone and got 1.76 dex. `C` keys on
   the *total* field, which at fixed R is dominated by the z-independent radial part. Correct answer
   is smaller in the Bovy–Rix window (1.02) and larger in the Gaia one (2.30).
3. **"ρ_crit is inside RG's range" and "γ brackets q from below."** Both were read symbol-to-symbol.
   Mapped through the regulator the first is right and the second is a factor 2 wrong — RG's
   `q = 0.75` is `γ ≈ 0.245`, because one slope is base-10 and the other natural. Patched the script
   rather than the prose.
