# Topic: Mock-injection test of the wide-binary deprojection crux, before 2 December

## Question
Chae et al. 2026 get γ = 1.60 (+0.24/−0.14) from 36 binaries with a geometric deprojection. Saad & Ting 2026 get γ = 1.00 (+0.24/−0.19) from the same 36 with a forward-modelled semi-major axis, and 1.56 when they deproject. Inject known Newtonian and boosted mocks with realistic eccentricity distributions and errors: which estimator recovers the truth?

## Context
A researcher persona (visitor 2026-11-08) called this the most useful thing this project could do for the field this month. It needs no new data and does not depend on Synchronism. `/wide-binaries` has listed it as open since June. Boufourou 2026 (arXiv:2608.24556) does mock-inject estimators on the 81,088-pair sample, but the abstract does not address this deprojection step on the 36-pair sample. Chae, Park & Yoon 2026 (arXiv:2610.40388) now reply that the Newtonian result comes from "a fatal error in the code or arbitrarily enlarged errors". Read both in full first: part of the question may be settled, or the reply may name a checkable error.

## Why It Matters
Gaia DR4 is on 2 December 2026, and how the dispute is read then depends on this step. It cannot move Bucket 0, and nothing about the framework depends on the answer: a useful negative or positive for someone else's question. Publish the result whichever way it goes.
