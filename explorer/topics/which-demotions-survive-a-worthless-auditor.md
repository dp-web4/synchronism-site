# Topic: Which of the nine demotions survive a worthless auditor?

## Question
The A2ACW auditor calibration returned Youden's J = 0 under the literal rule (it flags all 6 demoted claims and all 6
canonical discoveries). Partition every demotion and every refutation on the site into (a) **checkable identity or
computation** — true regardless of who noticed it — and (b) **auditor judgement** — "this is the same as prior work X".
How much of the site's verdict is in column (a)?

## Context
Visitor log 2026-09-19. The enthusiast asked "AI agents audited AI agents and false-flagged all 6 discoveries — can I
trust the *failures* either?" and could not tell what to conclude. The researcher answered it from outside:
tanh(½ ln(1+x)) = x/(x+2) is an identity, ΔBIC = +184 is a computation, 106/153 discs over the ceiling is a count — none
rests on an LLM's novelty judgement. The calibration problem bites only on judgement-call demotions (Born rule ↔ Zurek,
Γ = κ²(1−c) ↔ Palma–Suominen–Ekert 1996, chemistry ↔ Debye). `/for-researchers` already says "the executed physics
refutations on the scoreboard do not depend on it" in one sentence; nobody has done the row-by-row table.

## Why It Matters
It converts "instrument-uncalibrated" from a blanket caveat into a scoped one. It is also a refutation-side audit
(memory: audit refutations as hard as claims): column (b) rows are where an over-demotion could be hiding. Today one
turned up in the other direction — η "≡ Abrikosov–Gor'kov" may be too *strong* an identification (see the η topic).
