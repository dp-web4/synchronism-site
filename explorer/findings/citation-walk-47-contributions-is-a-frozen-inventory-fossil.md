## Summary

Retraction-survival is **systemic**, and the largest instance is not any single number — it is the
**framing of the contributions list itself.** The site's `/honest-assessment` says (line 723–724):

> "The remaining 44 contributions are not yet swept and remain this site's only unaudited surface;
> the demotion prior for them is correspondingly low."

**Both halves are false, and the archive's own sessions establish this:**

1. **"not yet swept / unaudited"** — The archive's own post-inventory sessions **#631–#691** already
   characterized essentially all of them. The cosmology contributions were scored by the archive's
   own **Session #635 Cosmology Scorecard** ("Novel-unfalsified: 0"); the chemistry contributions were
   **executed against the correct null in Session #669** (Δr = 0 *exactly* — they are the Debye model
   relabeled); the BTFR/α contributions were refuted in **Session #631**. The surface was audited
   Feb–May 2026. The site froze at the **Session #615** snapshot (2026-02-17) and never propagated the
   subsequent audit trail.

2. **"demotion prior … correspondingly low"** — Backwards. Given that the archive already resolved every
   contribution it examined as reparametrization / refuted / null-class, and the on-site 9-of-9 audited
   claims all failed, the correct prior for the remainder is **high**, not low.

3. **Bonus (the count itself is a fossil):** The headline number **"47"** was flagged by the archive's
   own **Session #634** (2026-04-25) as a **57% overcount** against Session #582's canonical, strictly-
   defined **30**; the archive reverted to citing 30 (S618, S627) and S634 recommended the site do the
   same. The site still says 47. This is textbook retraction-survival — a compilation surface carrying a
   number the source program retracted two months later.

**Verdict on the topic question: not isolated. The "44 uncharacterized" claim is itself the retraction-
survival artifact — a Feb-17 inventory presented as "unaudited" when the archive audited and demoted it
across #631–#691.**

## Implications for the Site

The honest ledger — the site's single strongest asset — has a stale sentence at its core that inverts
the truth: it tells a researcher the contributions list is *unaudited with a low demotion prior*, when
the archive audited it and demoted it. A Pass-4 researcher who takes that sentence at face value carries
away the opposite of the archive's verdict — the exact failure mode the 2026-07-04 visitor flagged on
`/cdm-discrimination`, one surface over.

This is the same class of defect as CDM σ_int and TEST-04a (a compilation surface outliving its source's
retraction), but at the **meta level**: the surface that *summarizes* all the contributions is itself the
fossil. Fixing it is higher-leverage than fixing any single contribution, because it corrects the reader's
prior over the entire remaining list.
