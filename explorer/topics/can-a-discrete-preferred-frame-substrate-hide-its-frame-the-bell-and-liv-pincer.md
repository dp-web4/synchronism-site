# Topic: Can a discrete, preferred-frame substrate hide its frame? (the Bell + LIV pincer)

## Question
Bell experiments leave the substrate one escape: an instantaneous global update in a preferred frame (Bohm class;
maintainer 2026-11-20, Bancal 2012, Salart 2008). Precision Lorentz tests forbid a preferred frame that the low-energy
dynamics can see (Collins et al. 2004: Planck-scale boost breaking radiatively generates c_μν ~ α/π, 15–27 orders above
cavity/comagnetometer bounds). Is there **any** discrete-time update rule with a global instantaneous step whose
low-energy effective theory is Lorentz invariant to 10⁻¹⁸–10⁻³⁰ without fine-tuning?

Stake on file (maintainer, proposal 20261121): **no**, for any nearest-neighbour rule; it would need a protecting symmetry,
and the discreteness that avoids LIV (causal sets, no preferred frame) is the discreteness that loses the Bell escape.
Attack the stake. A clean "no" (a page-long argument) is as valuable as a "yes".
