## The three load-bearing results of the walk

1. **The tested-variable record is closed and ρ(r) is not in it.** Every published
   organizing-variable discrimination tests acceleration against {radius/distance, orbital
   frequency, size, surface density/brightness}: McGaugh 2004 (radius, frequency, acceleration;
   1,145 points), Sanders & McGaugh 2002 Fig. 1 (size vs acceleration), F&M 2012 §4.3.3/§5
   ("observationally excludes all hypotheses that simply alter the force law at a linear
   length-scale"), Banik & Zhao 2022 ("acceleration rather than distance"), and — sharpest —
   Stiskalek & Desmond 2023, whose ML feature sweep over SPARC (nothing improves on g_bar)
   includes three surface densities but **no volumetric density feature**. The volumetric
   instantiation was genuinely unoccupied territory.

2. **The sign statement has a 1983 argument *template* that must be credited.** Milgrom 1983b §III
   excluded length-keyed modifications because they force v² = GM/r₀ (TF slope 2 vs observed 4);
   Sanders 1986 stated the direction form ("larger galaxies ⇒ larger discrepancy — contrary to
   observations"). The site's ρ_crit ∝ V⁻² sign statement is the same argument run for a new
   variable. Correct prior-art line: *template Milgrom 1983b §III / Sanders 1986 (length);
   volumetric-density instantiation first quantified here.* This strengthens the claim — the
   method has a 40-year pedigree — and pre-empts the one referee objection the audit surfaced.

3. **Two citation glosses on /for-researchers are imprecise** (secondary corrections):
   - astro-ph/0510117's non-locality theorem is **temporal** (modified-inertia kinetic actions
     must be non-local in *time* — Milgrom 1994), not the **spatial** enclosed-mass non-locality
     the no-go actually uses. Keep the citation; fix the gloss. The spatial statement is carried
     by the RAR literature + the elliptic Bekenstein–Milgrom field equation.
   - "The RAR/MDAR is keyed on acceleration, not density (Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2016; Lelli
     et al. 2017)" — Lelli tested **surface** columns (Σ at R, effective surface brightness),
     never volumetric ρ. Say "not radius, not surface brightness," and cite Stiskalek & Desmond
     2023 for the systematic sweep.
