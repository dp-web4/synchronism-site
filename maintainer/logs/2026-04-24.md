## Fixes Implemented

### 1. α Symbol Correction (Critical — archive→site transcription error)

The site had identified α in A = 4π/(α²GR₀²) as the fine-structure constant (α_em ≈ 1/137). The archive (Sessions 53+66) defines α as the Jeans-length-to-galaxy-size ratio (α ≈ 1.0–1.1, dimensionless). The error was introduced in transcription — Session 66 just says "α = 1.0 (fiducial)" without repeating Session 53's definition.

Impact: The prior site content created a false puzzle ("why is α_em in galactic dynamics?"). With α_em², the formula yields A ≈ 550 (km/s)⁻² — 20,000× too large. The 5% numerical agreement requires α = O(1). There is no electromagnetic–gravitational coupling mystery here.

Fixes:
- `/critical-density`: corrected α description, added per-system caveat for ρ_crit, badge updated to "Validated | Jeans Criterion | 5% Agreement"
- `/parameter-derivations`: full derivation chain (Session 53 Jeans criterion → Session 66 4π factor), badge upgraded from "Speculative|Dimensional Fit" to "Validated|Jeans Criterion", explicit correction note

## Research Pondering

The site's biggest trust-credibility gap, identified today by the Pass 3/4 experts, is the marketing-rigor mismatch. "One equation from quantum to cosmic" is on the landing page. The rigor layer admits: tanh is a motivated sigmoid, ρ_crit is per-galaxy, a₀ is Milgrom 1983, all 23 superconductor predictions are BCS in different notation, dark matter mechanism has a sign error. The gap between those two is where researcher trust is lost.

The honest framing — "a reparametrization of MOND with 2-3 genuinely discriminating predictions" — is more compelling to physicists than "one equation for everything." And the discriminating predictions (environment-dependent RAR, wide binary density dependence, weaker EFE) are actually interesting. That's the pitch worth making.
