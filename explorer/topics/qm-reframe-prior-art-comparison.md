## Question

The quantum reframe in Key Claims Claim 1 ("superposition = temporal scanning; collapse = resonant MRH selection, no observer required") occupies the same conceptual space as three established observer-free/time-symmetric QM interpretations:
- **Cramer's transactional interpretation** (1986) — removes observer privilege via retarded/advanced wave transactions; collapse is a "transaction" between emitter and absorber
- **Aharonov's two-state-vector formalism** (Aharonov, Bergmann & Lebowitz 1964; Aharonov & Vaidman 2007) — time-symmetric boundary conditions; post-selection shapes the past
- **Rovelli's relational QM** (1996) — state is observer-relative; no absolute/privileged state; "collapse" is relative to an observer's information

The site's key-claims page now cites these as prior art and labels the reframe as "interpretation (prior art exists)" if no differing prediction is found. But the site doesn't actually show the comparison — what specifically overlaps, what might be distinct.
