# Critical assessment

**Verdict:** As presented, the claim that dark energy emerges with no cosmological constant is not established. The construction is a reparametrization of the Friedmann equation, not a derived result.

## Main problems

1. **The H₀ "reproduction" is a tautology.** H₀² = 8πGρ_m0/(3C₀) is the definition of Ω_m = 8πGρ_m0/(3H₀²) with C₀ = Ω_m. Setting C₀ = Ω_m cannot fail, so it is not a test. The same is true of ρ_DE = ρ_m(1−C)/C, which is just H²−H²_m rewritten.

2. **The cosmological constant is hidden, not removed.** At low density, C ≈ γρ/ρ_crit, so ρ_eff = ρ_m/C → ρ_crit/γ, a constant. The model is asymptotically de Sitter, with Λ_eff ∝ ρ_crit/γ. Because C₀ = 0.3 fixes only one combination of (γ, ρ_crit), ρ_crit must still sit near ρ_m0, which is the coincidence problem restated. At high density, 1−C ≈ 2(ρ/ρ_crit)^(−2γ), so the "dark energy" scales as ρ_m^(1−2γ). The expansion history is therefore fixed by γ and has not been compared with SNe, BAO or the CMB.

3. **There is no consistent dynamics.** Putting G_eff into the Friedmann equation by hand, with G depending on the matter density, does not come from an action. Without one, the Bianchi identity and energy-momentum conservation are not guaranteed, and the effective ρ_eff = ρ_m/C need not obey a consistent continuity equation. It is also ambiguous which ρ enters C: the background mean, or the local density.

4. **It faces severe observational conflicts.**
   - G_eff = G/C ≈ 3.3G at the cosmic mean density today. That would strongly enhance structure growth and lensing, and conflict with σ₈, ISW and RSD data.
   - C varies on Hubble timescales, so Ġ/G ~ H₀. Lunar laser ranging and pulsar timing bound this to roughly 10⁻¹³ yr⁻¹, about 10³ times smaller than H₀.
   - If C depends on local density, voids and the cosmic mean would have different G, which has no justification.
   - Using the same G_eff in the Friedmann equation and in perturbations would shift the CMB-inferred Ω_m, so Ω_m = 0.3 is not an independent input.

5. **"Coherence" is not defined.** The tanh(γ ln(1+ρ/ρ_crit)) form is not derived from any microphysics.

## Strengths

The model is simple, has few parameters, and naturally recovers GR at high density, so BBN and early-universe constraints are plausible. It gives a concrete, falsifiable w(z).

## What to do next

- Construct a covariant action, such as a scalar-tensor or density-dependent coupling theory. Derive the field equations, check conservation, and say precisely which density enters C.
- Fit SNe, BAO and CMB distances jointly, with Ω_m free, and compare against ΛCDM and w₀w_a using information criteria.
- Compute linear perturbations (growth, lensing, ISW) and compare with RSD and weak-lensing data.
- Evaluate Ġ/G and the solar-system and screening constraints.
- Justify the functional form of C, or test alternative forms.
- Remove the "no cosmological constant" claim unless the asymptotic constant ρ_crit/γ is shown to be derived rather than put in.
