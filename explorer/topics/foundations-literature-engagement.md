# Topic: Quantum Foundations Literature Engagement

## Question
Can the framework engage the modern quantum foundations literature (PBR 2012, Frauchiger-Renner 2018, weak measurements, Schlosshauer 2007) on the specific claims made in /born-rule and /measurement-without-observers? What do these results actually say about "MRH crossing = collapse"?

## Context
Pass 4 researcher (2026-05-18) flagged this as HIGH severity: the framework's pages on quantum foundations don't cite the canonical modern literature that bears directly on their claims.

Specific gaps:
- **PBR theorem (Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph 2012)**: Constrains ψ-epistemic interpretations (probability-wave-as-knowledge). "MRH crossing = collapse" is plausibly ψ-epistemic. Does PBR apply here?
- **Frauchiger-Renner (2018)**: Constrains observer-objectivity claims. The framework says "measurement without observers" — FR bears on whether this is self-consistent.
- **Weak measurements (Aharonov-Albert-Vaidman, Ringbauer experiments)**: Probe the "boundary crossing" picture directly. These experiments test whether the "crossing" picture produces testable differences from standard decoherence.
- **Schlosshauer (2007)**: *Decoherence and the Quantum-to-Classical Transition* is the standard reference for decoherence as an account of measurement. MRH is claiming to do what decoherence does — the comparison must be made.

## Why It Matters
A page titled "Measurement Without Observers" that doesn't engage PBR, FR, or weak measurements is not engaging the modern foundations literature. This is the single most fixable gap between the site's quantum claims and expert-level scrutiny. Unlike the physics gaps (MRH formal definition, Born rule derivation), this is a citation/framing task — the claims don't need to be stronger, they just need to acknowledge the field they're operating in.

## Scope
- Read PBR (2012), FR (2018), and Aharonov-Albert-Vaidman (1988) with focus on what they say about "crossing" and collapse models.
- For each: does it constrain, support, or not apply to the MRH crossing picture? One paragraph per paper.
- Propose specific additions to /measurement-without-observers and /born-rule.
- Do NOT try to claim these papers support the framework — they likely don't. The goal is honest engagement.
