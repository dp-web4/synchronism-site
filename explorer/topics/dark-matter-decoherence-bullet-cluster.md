# Topic: Dark Matter as Incomplete Decoherence — Bullet Cluster Analysis

## Origin
Seeded by maintainer session 2026-04-24, from Pass 4 researcher finding (visitor/logs/2026-04-24.md).

## The Issue

The site headlines "dark matter without particles" as one of three key claims. The Honest Assessment page correctly flags this as "Dark Matter Viscosity Sign Error — Wrong Direction." But the key-claims page and landing page still present DM-as-decoherence prominently, and use the a₀ ≈ cH₀/(2π) numerical coincidence as supporting evidence.

Pass 4 researcher identified two problems:
1. **Sign error is structural**: incomplete decoherence would make dark matter *more sticky* (viscous) than baryons. The Bullet Cluster (Clowe et al. 2006) showed DM behaves *more collisionlessly* than baryons. The 1E 0657-56 merger is the cleanest test: lensing mass passed through itself while baryonic gas stalled. This falsifies "more sticky" models at any amplitude.
2. **Stolen credit**: the a₀ ≈ cH₀/(2π) coincidence is Milgrom 1983, independently reproduced by McCulloch 2007, Verlinde 2017, Smolin 2017. It is not a Synchronism result. Presenting it as evidence for the DM-as-decoherence mechanism conflates a numerical coincidence (real, interesting) with a mechanism claim (falsified).

## The Question

Is there a version of "dark matter as incomplete decoherence" that:
1. Survives the Bullet Cluster (collisionless behavior required)
2. Is distinguishable from pure MOND
3. Doesn't rely on the viscosity/stickiness mechanism that Bullet Cluster rules out

Or is the DM-as-decoherence claim simply retired?

## What Would Survival Look Like?

The Bullet Cluster rules out *viscous* DM. But could "decoherent" mean something other than viscous? 

Options:
- **Decoherence reduces self-interaction cross-section** (opposite sign — decoherent matter becomes *less* sticky, not more). This would be consistent with Bullet Cluster, but the mechanism would be inverted from the current site description.
- **DM-as-decoherence is a description of MOND dynamics** in high-decoherence limit — not a particle model at all. In this framing, "DM" is an artifact of applying Newtonian gravity to a high-decoherence regime, and the "particles" that show up in lensing are just the gravitational effects of coherent galactic dynamics.
- **Retire the claim entirely** and reclassify the a₀ = cH₀/(2π) relation as a MOND reparametrization (honest, consistent with current Honest Assessment framing).

## Proposed Investigation

1. Read the Bullet Cluster paper (Clowe et al. 2006, ApJ 648:L109) carefully — what exactly does it constrain? Self-interaction cross-section σ/m < 1 cm²/g at 10¹¹–10¹² M☉ scales.
2. Determine whether "incomplete decoherence → reduced self-interaction" is mechanistically coherent. If decoherence decouples a system from its environment, does that make it less or more self-interacting?
3. Check whether any version of the Synchronism DM mechanism is saved by reinterpreting "decoherence" as "collisionless" rather than "viscous."
4. If not, draft a replacement treatment for the key-claims page: retire DM-as-decoherence, attribute a₀ to Milgrom, retain "dark matter as incomplete account" as a framing question rather than a mechanism claim.
