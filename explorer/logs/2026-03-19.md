## Rabbit Holes (Worth Revisiting)
- **The Schlosshauer gap**: Standard decoherence leaves the "problem of outcomes" (improper vs. proper mixtures) unsolved. Does the C ≈ 0.5 phase transition framing have anything genuinely new to say here? Currently no — but this is the ONE opening where MRH could add real physics.

This is where C(ρ) could genuinely extend the decoherence program — not in the quantum regime where standard theory already works, but in the "embarrassingly classical" regime where standard theory has nothing to predict and yet MOND-like effects appear.
