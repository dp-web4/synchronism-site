PART 2 -- the vacuum source floor: an a-priori kill of L1 for EVERY gamma
==============================================================================

C(rho) = tanh(gamma ln(1 + rho/rho_crit)).  As rho -> 0:
  ln(1+x) -> x,  tanh(u) -> u   =>   C -> gamma * rho/rho_crit
  =>  rho_eff = rho/C -> rho_crit/gamma,  a CONSTANT independent of rho.

So L1's source does not vanish in vacuum.  It approaches a uniform floor
that fills all space.  Numerical check of the limit at rho/rho_crit = 1e-8:

   gamma    rho_crit/gamma (DDO154)    rho/C at x=1e-8    ratio
   0.250                   256.2440           256.2440   1.0000
   0.489                   131.0041           131.0041   1.0000
   1.000                    64.0610            64.0610   1.0000
   2.000                    32.0305            32.0305   1.0000
   5.000                    12.8122            12.8122   1.0000

Consequences of a uniform effective source rho_f = rho_crit/gamma:
  M_eff(<r) ~ (4/3) pi r^3 rho_f  ->  diverges
  V^2 = G M_eff/r ~ (4/3) pi G rho_f r^2  ->  V grows LINEARLY with r, forever
  Every isolated galaxy has infinite mass; no rotation curve ever flattens.

Scale of the absurdity -- the implied halo mass inside 100 kpc:
    galaxy  rho_f (Msun/pc^3)    M(<100kpc) Msun     M_bar Msun      ratio
   DDO 154              32.03          1.342e+17      2.293e+08   5.85e+08

For comparison, the mass of the observable universe is ~1e53 kg ~ 5e22 Msun.

This kill is PARAMETER-FREE in gamma and rho_crit: the floor exists for every
gamma > 0 and every rho_crit > 0.  It is also independent of the disk model --
it follows from the small-x expansion of the compander alone.  L1 is therefore
eliminable WITHOUT data, on internal grounds.

PART 3 -- the surviving reading, and what it costs
==============================================================================

L4 (plotter quadrature) has no field equation and appears in no manuscript;
   it is a plotting stand-in, self-labelled as such in the source comment.
L5 (g = g_bar * C) was retracted site-side on 2026-08-04 (wrong direction:
   the site's own f_DM = 1-C identity requires division).
L1 dies on the vacuum floor above.
=> L2 == L3 is the ONLY reading that is (a) sourced by a field equation,
   (b) momentum-conserving, (c) consistent with f_DM = 1-C, and (d) not
   eliminable a priori.

Cost of that survival, stated plainly: L2 = L3 is exactly the reading that
TEST-09 and TEST-10 already refute (boost ceiling B <= 1/Omega_m).  Closing
the coupling fork in favour of L2=L3 does not add a refutation -- it removes
the escape hatch that made the existing two conditional.

L2's own known pathology (already on /mond-unification, 2026-08-04): because
g = g_N/C and C -> 0 in vacuum, the EXTERIOR field of an isolated mass
diverges.  Note this is a DIFFERENT object from L1's floor: L1 diverges in
the SOURCE (rho_eff -> const), L2 diverges in the FIELD (g -> inf at fixed
M).  Both are vacuum pathologies; they are not the same pathology, and
neither implies the other.
