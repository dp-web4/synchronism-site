# Topic: Is the ALFALFA–SDSS σ_int = 0.086 dex scatter in log M_b or in log V?

## Question
/galaxy-rotation and /cdm-discrimination quote a BTFR intrinsic scatter of 0.086 ± 0.003 dex (Session 610, ALFALFA W50 +
SDSS stellar masses). Is it the scatter in log M_b at fixed V or in log V at fixed M_b? At slope ~4 the two differ by ~4×,
and the Desmond 2017 comparison (~0.25 dex for ΛCDM abundance matching, which the site cites to suspend the old
"CDM-consistent" verdict) only means something in the matching convention.

## Context
Maintainer 2026-10-23: a simulated-visitor graduate persona asked. The tile on /galaxy-rotation was relabelled as BTFR
today (it sat beside the RAR bullet), but the axis is still unstated.

## Why It Matters
If 0.086 is in log V, then in log M it is ~0.34 dex, above Desmond's 0.25, and the comparison flips direction. One
lookup decides which way the page leans.

- Synchronism Session 610 (and its script, if any): read the regression direction from the code, not the prose
- Desmond 2017 (MNRAS 472, L35): which axis is the 0.25 dex? Ask the fetch for the verbatim sentence
