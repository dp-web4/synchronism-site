**Short answer:** I found no published model that matches this construction. The "intent" patterns appear to be the program's own. The generic setup is well known, though, and the claim that Lorentz violation is suppressed by E/E_Planck is the part the literature contradicts.

**Known precursors (generic setup, not the "intent" content):**
- Cubic-lattice or quantum-cellular-automaton models of discrete spacetime with a global tick are an established class. Mlodinow and Brun (arXiv:2506.20136) bound the lattice spacing of a quantum cellular automaton using experimental and astrophysical limits. I only saw the search snippet, so the details are uncertain. A matter-interferometry proposal for detecting discrete spacetime is arXiv:1802.03911.
- The program's underlying idea is a preferred frame with a Planck-scale cutoff. The literature on that idea is mainly the Lorentz-violation fine-tuning debate, described below.

**The key problem with the stated result:**
- Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia and Vucetich, "Lorentz invariance and quantum gravity: an additional fine-tuning problem?", Phys. Rev. Lett. 93, 191301 (2004), arXiv:gr-qc/0403053. They argue that a Planck-scale preferred frame, combined with known interactions, generates Lorentz violation at the percent level through radiative corrections. This is about 20 orders of magnitude above naive E/E_Planck estimates. Removing it requires fine-tuned dimension-4 counterterms.
- So "violation suppressed by powers of E/E_Planck, therefore unobservable" is not automatic. It has to be shown, for example by a symmetry that forbids the dimension-4 operators.
- Related papers I saw in the search results:
  - arXiv:1106.1417, "Small Lorentz violations in quantum gravity: do they lead to unacceptably large effects?". I believe this is Belenchia, Gambassi and Liberati, but that attribution is uncertain.
  - The comment arXiv:1106.6346. It notes that a hypercubic lattice keeps a discrete symmetry that forbids some dimension-4 terms. This is a possible way out, but it is not a full proof.
  - hep-ph/0404196, on cosmic-ray limits.

**Observational context (not searched in detail):** Lorentz-violation limits from gamma-ray bursts and cosmic rays already constrain linear-in-E/E_Planck dispersion corrections. I believe these reach or exceed the Planck scale, but I did not verify this. So the grid may already be partly constrained, and "unobservable at accessible energies" is too strong without a derivation.

**Verdict:** The substrate is a special case of generic discrete-spacetime or cellular-automaton models. The Lorentz-emergence claim runs into the Collins et al. fine-tuning result. The program should show which lattice symmetries protect the low-energy limit and that the leading dimension-4 and dimension-5 operators vanish or are suppressed. I did not check whether any "intent"-transfer dynamics exists in the literature.
