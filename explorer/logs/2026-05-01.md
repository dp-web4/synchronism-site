### 2. What if the current research frame is wrong?

The frame says: **Born rule, dual-C, and scale-invariant N_corr are three different problems.**

But all three can be read as symptoms of one underlying property: the framework provides **phenomenological dynamics** (tanh sigmoid, MRH crossing, γ vs ρ) on top of an **unspecified kinematic structure** (what counts; what is the measure; what is the unit of "presence"). The three visitor-flagged gaps may be the same gap viewed from three different windows:

- **Born rule gap** = no measure-theoretic structure on a state space ⇒ probabilities can't be assigned.
- **Dual-C gap** = no single definition of the observable C ⇒ different domains use different parameters.
- **N_corr gap** = no scale-invariant counting recipe ⇒ "correlated degrees of freedom" means whatever lands on the right γ.

If this is right, then writing a Born derivation, a bridge, and an N_corr recipe **separately** misses the structural point. The framework's missing ingredient is not three pieces; it is **one** kinematic layer (state space + measure + counting procedure), and the three operational gaps are its three most visible absences.

### 3. What experiment or investigation would most increase information right now?

Test the "common origin" hypothesis directly. Specifically:
- Trace whether the archive *already* attempted a Born rule derivation (it did — Sessions #73, #250, #291; Open Question OQ006).
- Check whether D and S in the consciousness arc are defined in terms of ρ (they are not — D is information-theoretic diversity, S is in ms).
- Check whether the bridge C(ρ) → C(γ, D, S) is even **dimensionally** possible (it is not, without an additional velocity scale).
- Check whether N_corr in the consciousness arc is ~10¹⁰ (yes — 10 orders of magnitude away from the γ Calculator's BCS preset of 10⁴).

If these checks all confirm the hypothesis, the right finding for the site is not three derivations — it is one **structural observation**.

### 4. What would falsify the current research posture?

If a single principled definition of "operational coherence" — a single counting procedure that gives a state space with a measure — would simultaneously resolve all three gaps, that would falsify the framework's posture that the gaps are independent research questions and confirm the structural reading.

Conversely: if the archive contains a Born rule derivation, a dimensional bridge, AND a scale-invariant N_corr recipe, but the site simply hasn't surfaced them, then the gaps are site-archive drift, not research gaps. (Partial confirmation: the Born rule **does** have archive material the site is silent on. This is at least partially a drift instance.)

### Decision

The topic queue's three HIGH topics are real, but they share a hidden structural unity. I'll work on the Born rule investigation as the **window into the structure**, and surface two findings:

1. **Born rule in the archive** — Session #73 + Session #250 + OQ006. The site says "Synchronism does not currently derive, assume, or flag" — false on all three counts. This is a drift instance.
2. **The three P0 gaps share a common origin** — missing kinematic foundation. The dual-C bridge is dimensionally underdetermined; the N_corr counting is domain-tunable; the Born rule cannot be derived without a measure on a state space. One framework gap, three visible faces.
