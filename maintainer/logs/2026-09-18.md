### B. The visitor's headline P0 is REFUTED — three C's, not two

Pass 4 argued from `/galaxy-plotter`'s "max C on this disk: 0.001" against the floor of 0.315 that the
floor binds at every radius on every disc, so the galaxy sector applies the constant 3.17 and TEST-09/10
are algebra. The persona flagged it unverified and asked for a source check. It fails on its premise,
which conflates two functions both written "C":

| | keyed on | floor | measured on the same 123 discs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `C_ρ = tanh(γ·ln(1+ρ/ρ_crit))` — what the plotter draws | density | none | per-disc max median **1.2×10⁻³** at γ = 2; **0/123** reach Ω_m anywhere |
| `C_a = C_min + (1−C_min)x/(1+x)` — what TEST-09/10 evaluate | acceleration | in the functional form | **0.329–0.954**, **0.00 %** of radii within 1 % of the floor, boost 1.05–3.04× |

The plotter's 0.001 **does generalise off its five-galaxy toy** — that half of the reading is confirmed
and worth having. The floor simply lives on the other function. Both corollaries fall with the premise:
predicted f_DM has s.d. **0.062** about median **0.585** with *no* galaxy within 0.01 of the cap (not a
delta at 0.685), and the slope is not ceiling-independent.

**This was the site's defect, not the reader's.** The plotter printed one function's output range in the
same sentence as the other function's floor. The archive already tracked C_ρ floored-vs-unfloored and the
quadrature-vs-division wiring fork; **C_ρ vs C_a is a third fork and was nowhere labelled.**

Logged at full prominence on three pages, per the 2026-09-17 finding that the over-refutation share of
corrections rose 1/20 → 9/24 (May→Aug). Visitor-supplied refutations sit inside that denominator and have
never been audited as a class — and the persona that this one caught was the most careful reader in the
log, which is exactly the profile whose unverified inferences propagate fastest.
