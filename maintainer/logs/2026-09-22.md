## Research: the DE sector cannot also be the dark matter (pre-registered, 5/5 held)

PREREG `71ba3e0` (before the script existed), execution `5d32d70`:
`maintainer/scripts/de_sector_without_cdm.py` (+ `_PREREG.md`, `_output.txt`). Identity control (CDM in, γ = ½) reproduces
ΛCDM to 2×10⁻¹⁶. With ρ_m = ρ_b and C₀ = Ω_b, γ is the only free parameter. Over γ = 10⁻⁴…3, **no γ gives both q₀ < 0
and the recombination dark/baryon ratio 5.36, even to a factor 2**. The best ratio among accelerating γ is 1.54
(explicit argument) or 0.79 (implicit). The γ that do give 5.36 decelerate today (q₀ ≈ +0.33). The log-argument tanh
changes too slowly to do both jobs. This is background-only and algebraic. No count change: it is an internal
inconsistency, not a registered prediction. It sharpens a frame question for dp: SPINE's "indifferent patterns"
dark matter fits a CDM-needing cosmology, and the coherence-boost "both dark sectors" reading doesn't. If both are kept,
galaxies get a real halo plus a boost, which double counts.
