**Short answer:** The substrate is not new, and the claim of Planck-suppressed Lorentz violation is probably wrong as stated. I answered from memory and did not search, so check the citations.

**The substrate is a known type of model**
- **Cellular-automaton and lattice models of physics.** Examples are Wolfram's *A New Kind of Science* (2002) and 't Hooft's *The Cellular Automaton Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics* (Springer 2016, arXiv:1405.1548). Earlier work includes Zuse and Fredkin's digital physics (uncertain on exact references).
- **Quantum walks and quantum cellular automata.** These have the same structure: local transfer between adjacent cells in global discrete ticks. Examples are Bialynicki-Birula, Phys. Rev. D 49, 6920 (1994), on the Dirac equation on a lattice, and D'Ariano & Perinotti, Phys. Rev. A 90, 062106 (2014), arXiv:1306.1934. Their continuum limit gives the Dirac equation. At high energy the dispersion is deformed, so Lorentz symmetry holds only approximately. Bisio, D'Ariano and Tosini also discuss this deformation (exact citation uncertain).
- **"Intent" patterns.** I know of no published model with this name. It looks like a relabeling of a field or amplitude on the cells.

**The Lorentz-invariance claim conflicts with known results**
- **Free-field level.** A cubic lattice gives rotation-breaking dispersion terms of order (Ea)², for example ω² ≈ k² − (a²/12)Σkᵢ⁴. Suppression by powers of E/E_P holds there.
- **Interacting level.** A preferred time slicing allows dimension-4 boost-violating operators. One example is species-dependent limiting speeds. Radiative corrections generate these with unsuppressed coefficients. This is the fine-tuning problem of Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia & Vucetich, Phys. Rev. Lett. 93, 191301 (2004), arXiv:gr-qc/0403053. The statement "violation is suppressed by E/E_P" therefore needs a protecting symmetry, such as supersymmetry. See also Polchinski, arXiv:1106.6346 (2011), on this naturalness problem (uncertain on details).
- **Dimension-5 operators.** Myers & Pospelov, Phys. Rev. Lett. 91, 211601 (2003), show these give linear E/E_P effects. Fermi observations of GRB 090510 (Abdo et al., Nature 462, 331 (2009)) and polarization limits from the Crab (Maccione et al. 2008, and Götz et al. 2014; citations uncertain) already constrain linear suppression to at or above the Planck scale. A cubic grid with absolute time would need to explain why those terms are absent.

**Consequence for "unobservable":** The grid is not automatically unobservable. Unless the program shows a symmetry that forbids the dimension-3 and dimension-4 operators (and ideally the dimension-5 ones), existing bounds on a preferred frame and on differences in limiting speed would likely rule it out or force fine-tuning. This problem is a main motivation for causal sets, which stay Lorentz invariant through random sprinkling (Bombelli, Lee, Meyer & Sorkin, Phys. Rev. Lett. 59, 521 (1987)), and for the Lorentz-invariant discretizations in Loop Quantum Gravity.

**Verdict:** The setup reduces to a known class, a QCA or quantum walk on a cubic lattice with a preferred time. The emergent-Lorentz-invariance claim is unproven and faces the Collins et al. objection.
