**Short answer:** The construction is not new, and the suppression claim is the part most at odds with the literature.

**1. Substrate: a discrete cubic grid with a global tick**
This is a cellular automaton or quantum-walk/QCA model of physics. Nothing in your description, including "intent" patterns hopping between adjacent cells, goes beyond the following (the "intent" label appears to be new terminology only):
- Zuse, *Rechnender Raum* (1969), and Fredkin's digital-physics program.
- Wolfram, *A New Kind of Science* (2002).
- 't Hooft, *The Cellular Automaton Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics* (Springer 2016; arXiv:1405.1548).
- Bialynicki-Birula, "Weyl, Dirac, and Maxwell equations on a lattice as unitary cellular automata," Phys. Rev. D 49, 6920 (1994).
- Meyer, "From quantum cellular automata to quantum lattice gases," J. Stat. Phys. 85, 551 (1996).
- Arrighi, Nesme and Forets, "The Dirac equation as a quantum walk: higher dimensions, observational convergence," J. Phys. A 47, 465302 (2014).
- D'Ariano and Perinotti, "Derivation of the Dirac equation from principles of information processing," Phys. Rev. A 90, 062106 (2014).
- Bisio, D'Ariano and Tosini, Ann. Phys. 354, 244 (2015).

These works show that Lorentz-covariant continuum dynamics (Dirac, Weyl, Maxwell) emerges from a lattice with discrete absolute time. The D'Ariano–Perinotti "Dirac cellular automaton" also yields Planck-scale-modified dispersion relations.

**2. Emergent Lorentz invariance**
This idea goes back to Chadha and Nielsen, "Lorentz invariance as a low energy phenomenon," Nucl. Phys. B217, 125 (1983). The background is reviewed in Mattingly, Living Rev. Relativ. 8, 5 (2005).

**3. The claim that violations are suppressed by powers of E/E_Planck**
This claim is not safe as stated.
- Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia and Vucetich, "Lorentz invariance and quantum gravity: an additional fine-tuning problem?" Phys. Rev. Lett. 93, 191301 (2004), show that a Planck-scale cutoff with a preferred frame generically induces unsuppressed, O(1) dimension-4 Lorentz-violating operators through loops. Examples are differing limiting speeds for different species. These are already excluded to roughly 10⁻¹⁵ to 10⁻²³ or better.
- Follow-up: Belenchia, Gambassi and Liberati, "Lorentz violation naturalness revisited," JHEP 06 (2016) 049 (citation details uncertain).
- Cubic symmetry forbids some dimension-4 rotation-violating terms, but not time-versus-space (boost) violation, which is the relevant one for a global-tick grid.

**4. "Unobservable at accessible energies"**
Even the E/E_P-suppressed (dimension-5) terms are tightly constrained:
- Fermi GRB 090510: Abdo et al., Nature 462, 331 (2009).
- Crab Nebula synchrotron emission: Maccione et al. (2008; details uncertain).
- Review: Liberati, Class. Quantum Grav. 30, 133001 (2013).

So the grid is empirically constrained, not simply unobservable.

**Verdict:** The substrate is a known QCA/lattice-gas model. The emergent-Lorentz claim is known and unproven in general. The "automatic E/E_P suppression" claim contradicts the Collins et al. fine-tuning result unless the program supplies a symmetry or mechanism that protects against it. I am not aware of such a mechanism in your description.
