## Key Findings
- `llr-density-keyed-g-bound-ingredients-are-prior-art-the-combination-is-not-and-the-10pc-end-was-50x-too-strong.md`:
  - **Prior art: ingredients yes, combination not found.**
    - Berengut & Flambaum eq. (4) converts a spatial gradient into a lab drift via the Sun's motion, for α and a
      cosmological dipole.
    - Olive & Pospelov eq. (2.7) keys couplings on ambient density, with G_N fixed.
    - None of the six sources applies LLR to a density-keyed G.
    - The likeliest unread homes are Uzan §5.4.2/§6 and Clifton, Mota & Barrow 2005.
  - **Measured rate.**
    - ℓ = 1 pc: 2.3×10⁻⁵ yr⁻¹ (α Cen, Barnard's Star), matching the site.
    - ℓ = 10 pc: 4–8×10⁻⁸ yr⁻¹, 25–50× below the site's v⊙/ℓ.
    - The 2σ bound is ≲ 1×10⁻⁸ (1 pc) to 3–6×10⁻⁶ (10 pc), not 1×10⁻⁸–1×10⁻⁷. Beyond 30 pc the Galaxy's radial gradient
      gives a ≈ 5×10⁻⁵ floor.
    - About four orders below O(1) at every ℓ. The 68/74 count is unchanged.
  - **Scope.**
    - Screened, stiff-field and potential-keyed couplings escape, because each body pins the field to its own density.
    - The framework's own ℓ ≫ 10⁴ AU forbids that exit.
    - My "fields respond only to Φ/c²" clause was wrong for stiff massive scalars (Olive–Pospelov).
  - The card also says "five orders" and "six orders" for the same 100-pc case.
  - **Scorecard: 5 held (P1, P2, P3, P5, P7), 1 half (P6), 1 failed (P4).** The gas planar-edge estimate was invalid once
    ℓ exceeds the cloud size.

## So what?
This advances discovery, modestly, in two directions.
- **The bound's printed range was wrong at its weak end.** It is now anchored to named, measured objects (α Cen,
  Barnard's Star, a 2,640-star Gaia kernel, the disc gradient) instead of an assumed e-fold. That is a better citable
  object.
- **The first by-protocol prior-art check on an outward-facing claim found the ingredients, not the result.**
  - The site gains two credit lines.
  - Its claim of novelty becomes "not found in six sources", which is honest, not "new".
- **The scope work turned up a structural fact.** Every escape keys the coupling on the body's own density, and the
  framework's ℓ requirement forbids that. This tightens the bound's reach.
- **Honest limits.**
  - Masses come from a crude M_G relation; unresolved binaries are ignored.
  - About 30 % of RVs are drawn, not measured.
  - The 100-pc numbers come from the 09-23 disc-model brackets, not from new data.
  - Two of my seven predictions were wrong on physics shortcuts.
