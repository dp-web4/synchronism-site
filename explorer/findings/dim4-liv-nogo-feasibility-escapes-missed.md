## Research Notes

### What the literature actually says

**The general theorem (CPSU 2004) is textbook.** Collins, Pérez, Sudarsky, Gambini, Pullin, *PRL* 93, 191301 (2004) / gr-qc/0403053: microscopic (Planck-scale) Lorentz violation percolates through radiative corrections into **dimension ≤ 4** LV operators suppressed only by Standard-Model couplings (~α/π), not by the Planck ratio — in gross conflict with low-energy bounds unless fine-tuned. This is reviewed as a standard hazard in every modern LV-phenomenology survey (Mattingly, *Living Rev. Rel.* 2005; Liberati 2013; Collins et al., hep-th/0603002). The follow-up debate is mature: Polchinski-era exchange (Collins et al. vs. arXiv:1106.1417 "Small Lorentz violations… do they lead to unacceptably large effects?"), Crichigno–Vucetich quantum-correction analyses. **A referee would immediately recognize the dim-4 c_μν argument as CPSU 2004 applied to one more substrate.**

**The escape landscape is fully mapped — and the site missed most of it.** The site's implicit enumeration is {boost invariance [killed by absolute time], strong-dynamics/non-perturbative UV [killed by SM perturbative couplings]} → "doubly-obstructed." The literature has at least three more, two of them perturbative and boost-breaking-compatible:

**The decisive one is Pospelov–Shang.** Hořava–Lifshitz gravity is the *canonical* preferred-foliation theory — it has a dynamical preferred frame / khronon, i.e. it is in the **same ontological class as Synchronism's absolute time.** Pospelov & Shang demonstrated that the radiative percolation of LV into the SM is *protected by a scale hierarchy without restoring boost invariance and without SUSY*. (They even handle the awkward case — instantaneously-propagating non-Lifshitz modes causing residual quadratic divergences — by adding higher-Lifshitz-dimension terms.) So the site's claim that the survivor "must be non-perturbative" is false: the canonical theory in Synchronism's own class survives *perturbatively* via a scale separation.

### What this does — and does not — do to Synchronism

It does **not** rescue the minimal framework. Synchronism, as stated, implements *none* of the three escapes:
- **SUSY:** absent; adding it is a large unmotivated extension, and low-energy SUSY breaking only protects down to the breaking scale (reintroduces a weaker tuning ~ (m_SUSY/M)²).
- **Scale-hierarchy:** Synchronism's tree-level mechanism is *single-substrate universality* — everything couples at one substrate scale. A Λ_HL ≪ M_pl hierarchy with M_pl-suppressed matter coupling is exactly what single-substrate universality denies. Whether the substrate can be given anisotropic Lifshitz scaling is **an open, answerable model-building question**, not a closed door.
- **Random sprinkling:** requires giving up the regular even-k lattice / preferred frame — i.e. giving up absolute time. Genuinely unavailable to Synchronism (this is the real "ends the theory" escape).

So the corrected verdict is a **conditional** refutation plus an **open custodial-mechanism question**, which is materially weaker than the site's current closed framing:

> The *minimal* framework (no added custodial structure) predicts c_μν ~ α/π, refuted by 16–28 OOM. This is the standard LV-naturalness problem (CPSU 2004). It is **not** "doubly-obstructed": the standard custodial escapes (SUSY — Groot Nibbelink–Pospelov 2005; anisotropic scale-hierarchy — Pospelov–Shang 2012, demonstrated for the canonical absolute-time-class theory) are perturbative and boost-breaking-compatible, contradicting the site's "only escape is non-perturbative" claim. The framework adopts none of them, so the problem is **unresolved — a naturalness problem with known, unadopted solutions, not a closed falsification.**

**A naturalness problem is not a refutation.** Calling the dim-4 face "the framework's most decisive falsification" is a category error: the field solves this class of problem routinely. The 16–28 OOM number is the *size of the tuning* in the minimal model, not a measured contradiction. The defensible sentence keeps "minimal framework refuted at natural value" and drops "doubly-obstructed / most decisive falsification / only-non-perturbative-escape."
