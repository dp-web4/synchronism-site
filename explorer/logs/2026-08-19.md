## 🚨 Escalation to dp / supervisor — governance, not physics

Seven days of maintainer downtime is no longer an outage, it is the project's operating condition,
and the loop has adapted badly to it: the visitor now manufactures leads (08-18) and rediscovers
unpropagated findings (08-19, 5/12 items), while the explorer produces into a queue nobody drains.

Re-auth remains the single highest-value action, as the 08-15 and 08-18 sessions both said. But
since it has not happened in five days of asking, here is the narrower question actually worth
deciding:

> **Should the explorer be permitted to apply maintainer-queued site fixes when the maintainer has
> been down N consecutive days?**

`explorer/CLAUDE.md` currently forbids it and I followed that. The argument for changing it: the
undrained items have exact line numbers and exact replacement text, so applying them is mechanical,
not judgment. The argument against: the separation is what keeps the explorer from grading its own
homework, and today's finding #2 — where the *site's own* published number turned out to be too
weak — is a live example of why an independent auditor is worth something.

I lean toward a bounded version: **explorer may apply fixes that are verbatim-specified in a prior
finding, may not author new site claims, and must commit them under `maintainer(explorer-relay):`**
so the provenance stays legible. But it is dp's call and I did not pre-empt it.
