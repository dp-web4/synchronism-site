## Key Findings

- **`tanh-as-response-not-saddle-point.md`** — C(ρ) = tanh(γ·log(1+ρ/ρ_crit)) is a **field-response curve**, not a Landau saddle-point of a closed-system order parameter. It is the saddle of F[C; ρ] = aC² + bC⁴ - h_eff(ρ)·C with h_eff = T·γ·log(1+ρ/ρ_crit) — i.e., a Curie-law response, not a Weiss ferromagnet. Three consequences: (1) no phase transition in C(ρ); (2) no critical exponents (β, ν, η undefined); (3) the "+1" in the log is forced by the boundary condition `vacuum has no coherence`, not arbitrary. The site's `/core-idea` parenthetical citing the *self-consistent* Ising equation (m = tanh(βJzm)) invokes the wrong analogy — the right analogy is m = tanh(βh) for a paramagnet in applied field. Recommended actions for `/core-idea`, `/coherence-function`, `/phase-boundary-visualizer`, `/parameter-derivations`, plus a back-annotation to Synchronism repo asking which equation the framework actually claims (the explicit response or the self-consistent order parameter — they predict different physics).

- **`mrh-zurek-discriminator-search.md`** — There is **no operational discriminator** between MRH-crossing and Zurek einselection. Going through einselection's four definite predictions (pointer basis, decoherence timescale, off-diagonal evolution shape, Quantum Darwinism redundancy), Synchronism is *silent* in three of four and *inheriting the standard prediction* in the fourth. MRH-crossing has strictly **less** operational content than einselection, not different content. Recommendation: re-badge `/measurement-without-observers` as Reparametrization | Standard Decoherence in MRH Vocabulary, cite Zurek/Joos-Zeh/Caldeira-Leggett/Schlosshauer, surface the *consciousness-threshold connection* (C ≈ 0.5) as the genuinely novel content rather than bundling it with measurement-theory framing.
