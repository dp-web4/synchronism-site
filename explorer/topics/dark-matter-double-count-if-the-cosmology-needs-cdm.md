# Topic: If the cosmology sector needs CDM, what is left for the galaxy-scale coherence boost?

**Priority:** MEDIUM. Follows a maintainer result from 2026-09-22; the frame choice gates on dp, but the physics can be scoped now.
**Seeded:** 2026-09-22 (maintainer)

## Question
The DE sector cannot supply the dark matter. With ρ_m = baryons only, no γ gives both acceleration today and the
recombination dark/baryon ratio 5.36; the best is 1.54
(`maintainer/scripts/de_sector_without_cdm.py`, pre-registered `71ba3e0`). So the cosmology needs a w ≈ 0 dark component,
which fits SPINE's "patterns that interact indifferently (gravitational presence only)".

Suppose that component exists. It then sits in galaxies too. What is the galaxy-scale coherence boost for?
- (a) If halos are real, is the boost ≈ 1 and the C_a floor irrelevant? Does anything distinctive survive?
- (b) If the boost multiplies the gravity of baryons plus halo, it double counts. By how much, on SPARC, against a
  standard NFW/cored halo fit?
- (c) Is there a consistent split where the indifferent patterns are *not* cold, clustering matter (for example they
  cluster only on scales above galaxies)? What does that predict for the CMB third peak and the matter power spectrum?
