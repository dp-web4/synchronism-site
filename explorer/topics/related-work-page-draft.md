# Topic: Draft a /related-work Page Situating Synchronism in the Live Landscape

**Seeded:** 2026-05-03 (maintainer)  
**Priority:** HIGH  
**Context:** Pass 4 researcher named the absence of engagement with contemporary alternatives as the single feature most marking the site as outside the active research conversation

## Question

Can we produce a draft `/related-work` page that places Synchronism in the active landscape of modified gravity, decoherence theory, and consciousness research — for each alternative stating: what's shared, what's distinct, and whether the two could be empirically discriminated?

## The Gap

The site makes claims overlapping these active research programs without engaging any of them:

**Modified gravity:**
- Verlinde 2017 emergent gravity — derives MOND-like behavior from elastic response of dark energy; also tested against SPARC; no free parameter like a₀ (it emerges)
- Moffat MOG (scalar-tensor-vector gravity) — developed action principle, tested against SPARC/bullet cluster
- TeVeS/BIMOND — now ruled out by GW170817, but informative as a comparison
- Khoury-Berezhiani superfluid dark matter — modified gravity at low acceleration from dark matter in superfluid phase

**Decoherence / quantum foundations:**
- Zurek's einselection / pointer states — rigorous program for classical emergence from quantum
- Schlosshauer's decoherence textbook (2007) — canonical treatment; Synchronism's Γ = γ²(1-c) claims need positioning against this
- Friston's Markov blankets / free energy principle — the obvious formal interlocutor for MRH

**Consciousness:**
- IIT (Tononi) — Φ-based; no specific threshold; different measurement structure
- Global workspace theory (Baars/Dehaene) — different ontology, some empirical overlap

## Why It Matters

A single `/related-work` page doing 5–7 brief comparisons (shared / distinct / discriminating test) would do more for the site's academic credibility than anything else. It signals engagement with the field, not isolation from it.

## Suggested Format

For each alternative:
```
### [Name] (Author Year)
- **What's shared**: [1-2 sentences]
- **What's distinct**: [1-2 sentences]
- **Discriminating test**: [specific observable difference, if any]
- **Synchronism's current posture**: [silent / consistent / contradicts]
```
