## Key Findings
- `rg-as-published-has-a-smoothing-length-where-its-galaxy-fits-apply-llr-excludes-it-the-surviving-window-is-sub-parsec.md`
  - **Pointwise RG is disclaimed by M&D 2016 §2.2.** The answer under it is still a ≳ 10⁵ kill: ε at the Moon moves by
    7×10⁻⁶ to 6×10⁻² over the normal solar-wind range.
  - **For D ≳ 1 pc, ε is uniform over the solar system.** So the site's algebraic "pc-smoothed G·f(ρ_s)" *is* RG, and LLR
    excludes all 9 calibration × reading cells at D = 0.5–10 pc, by 30× to 10⁷×.
  - **D ≥ 30 pc.**
    - Only DMS joint (Q·ln) survives LLR (0.25–6× the bound, depending on ρ_s,⊙ 0.084 vs 0.043).
    - On K_z(1.1 kpc), DMS joint is within 1.4σ, DMS mean is 3.5–4.3σ low, and E0 is 3.3–5σ high.
    - DMS joint is already dSph-excluded.
  - **The local tests pass only at D ≈ 0.03–0.3 pc** (Banik WB reading) or 10 AU–0.025 pc (Chae reading). There ε follows
    ISM phase structure, and the smooth-density galaxy fits do not apply. Verdict: **untested**.
  - **New wide-binary signature for small D.** An EFE-free step boost of 3/(1+2ε_ISM): 1.05–1.38 for the DMS calibrations
    and 1.7–2.5 for E0.
  - **M&D's "local experiments would be in full agreement" is refuted at their calibrations.** LLR reads the slope, not the
    level.
  - **Scorecard 3 held / 1 partial / 2 failed.** P3 failed: DMS joint was excluded at D = 0.5 pc and at ≥ 30 pc at the
    registered ρ_s.
