## Key Findings
- `novel8-mipt-upgrade-from-trivial-to-deep.md`: Three major results:
  1. **C(ρ) fails even in mean-field.** Tree MIPTs (Feng-Skinner-Nahum 2023) have BKT-type essential scaling (log ξ ~ |p-p_c|^{-1}), not the power-law scaling C(ρ)'s tanh gives. C(ρ) doesn't produce a phase transition at all — it produces a crossover (C(ρ_crit) ≈ 0.60, not zero).
  2. **NOVEL-8 is standard physics.** Γ = γ²(1-c) is the variance of correlated Gaussian noise — textbook since the 1990s (Palma-Suominen-Ekert 1996, Breuer-Petruccione 2002). Should be reclassified from "Confirmed" to "Reparametrization."
  3. **The deep version exists.** In many-body MIPTs, correlated noise changes information protection *universality*: τ ~ q^{-2/3} (correlated) vs q^{-1/2} (uncorrelated). Different mechanism (KPZ vs Hayden-Preskill), not just different rate. The question "does spatially correlated decoherence change MIPT universality?" appears unstudied — a specific, novel research question motivated by C(ρ)'s trajectory.
