B4  could the framework be AMBIENT-keyed instead (making TEST-20 live)?
    Ambient density is constant to ~1e-6 across a galaxy, so C would be a
    per-galaxy constant and g_eff = g_N/C is a pure rescaling of G.
    Best per-galaxy CONSTANT boost vs MOND on the same curves:
      constant-boost (ambient keying)  chi2/N = 99.82   (per-galaxy median 8.23)
      MOND simple mu                   chi2/N = 51.84   (per-galaxy median 10.54)
      aggregate ratio 1.93x worse, but the PER-GALAXY
      MEDIAN goes the other way -- the aggregate is carried by the best-measured
      discs.  chi2 alone does NOT settle this; the shape test below does.

    Parameter-free version (no fitting at all): under ambient keying C is a
    per-galaxy constant, so g_obs = g_bar / C and the RAR is a line of slope
    EXACTLY 1 in log-log, for every galaxy, every gamma, every knee.
      observed RAR log-slope, g_bar < 0.1 a0 (N=1101): 0.330 +/- 0.017   -> 40.3 sigma from 1
      observed RAR log-slope, g_bar < 0.03 a0 (N= 258): 0.161 +/- 0.025   -> 32.9 sigma from 1
      Ambient-only keying is excluded by the RAR slope alone, with no free
      parameter.  The density-keyed branch is therefore forced to be
      baryon-local, which is what makes B1's lever as small as it is.
