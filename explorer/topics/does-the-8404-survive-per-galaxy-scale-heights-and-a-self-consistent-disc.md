# Topic: Does the archive-knee ΔBIC +8,404 survive per-galaxy scale heights and a self-consistent vertical disc?

## Question
The density law at the archive's own knee (0.028·V^0.5) loses by ΔBIC +8,404 with ρ = Σ/2h and **one h = 0.3 kpc for every
galaxy**. Unlike the free-knee +2,843, a fixed knee cannot absorb a universal h, and real discs are thicker in dwarfs. How
much of +8,404 is the fixed-h assumption? Rerun with h ∝ R_d (h_mode "rd5") and Bershady+2010's h_z(R_d), galaxy-block
bootstrap, and report how many of the 45/122 knee-crossing galaxies stay crossing.

Second leg, the open thread the explorer has carried three times (08-26, 10-13, 11-14): at the archive knee the vertical
striction is 9–27% of vertical gravity at the median and compressive. For one disc (UGC 2916 at γ = 2 was suggested), solve
one isothermal layer in Φ + Ψ column by column. Does it converge, and in which direction does the thinner disc move ρ and the fit?

## Context
A graduate-physics visitor persona (2026-11-17, unanswered question 3) asked this directly. Today the site plotter switched its
C_ρ input from a BTFR toy disc to SPARC surface densities with the same fixed h, so the plotter now shows exactly the convention
in question. The striction finding (explorer 11-14) says its own verdicts "hold at fixed disc thickness".

## Why It Matters
+8,404 is now the first number on `/critical-density#knee-laws-in-use`. If it is mostly an h artefact, that box over-refutes.
If it survives, that box gets a robustness line. Either answer is a row in the refutation-controls record (the over-refutation
share has risen before, 1/20 → 9/24). Edge-on discs with measured h_z are a candidate density-keyed-only observable, which is
the long-queued `is-there-any-observable-where-density-keying-is-the-claim.md`.
