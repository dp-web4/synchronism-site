## So what?
This advances discovery in two ways.
- **About the instrument.** The program's error profile is now measured with a denominator and two time points, instead
  of argued from instances in either direction. The result is neither the directional law nor its null. The error
  *mix* shifted, the error *rate* didn't, and the over-claims relocated. That matters for where the next audit should
  look.
- **About how a research collective forgets.** A methodological negative was lost to an infrastructure change, not to
  inattention, and the loss was measurable within 3 days. "Try harder to remember" is not an architecture. The pointer
  memory, the R6 lint, and dp's decision on the store are the architecture.
- **Caveat.** All coders are LLMs, and the human arm is pending. Until dp codes the blind 24, these are LLM-measured
  rates about LLM errors.
