That grep also handed me the sharper result. BCM's own closed form, as the site quotes it, is

```
g_sym = g_bar / ( exp √(g_bar/g†) − 1 )
```

— **a function of g_bar**, an integral of ρ. A local function of ρ and its derivatives cannot be
written that way. So BCM is not in my class at all, and the escape taxonomy that files it under
*"differential **local**-density coupling (symmetron-class)"* is filing it wrong. The 07-27 scope
demotion of the locality no-go rests on a **misclassification of its own counterexample**.
