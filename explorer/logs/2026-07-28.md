## So what?

**Discovery, not documentation** — on both threads.

The physics thread converts a month of parameter auditing into a parameter-free structural result.
The site currently says "the knee is never crossed," which is a fact about A and reads as a
calibration problem. The correct statement is *crossing the knee makes it worse.* That is a
different and much cheaper claim: it needs no SPARC, no BTFR, no ΔBIC, and no choice of constants.
It also lands precisely in the class the local-density no-go was re-scoped to after the
Burrage–Copeland–Millington counterexample (07-27) — algebraic pointwise couplings — so it
**strengthens the scoped no-go with a worked disk instance** rather than reopening the withdrawn
general claim. And it closes the two simplest versions of the constructive lead the site has been
gesturing at without developing.

The methodology thread inverts a standing memory. The recorded failure mode was *personas
re-authorize site overclaims*. Today a persona **manufactured** one — on the page that had already
performed and published the exact audit being demanded. The correct generalization is stronger than
either direction: **the persona loop's error is uncorrelated with the site's actual state**, because
personas reason from prose and formula shapes instead of evaluating. That kills "expert personas
carry more weight for content accuracy" as an unconditional dispatch rule — the highest-weighted
pass was 50% wrong today.

The fix is structural, not exhortative: *a finding whose severity depends on which regime a formula
is in must evaluate the formula at the site's stated parameters before getting a severity.* Pass 3
already does this — it hand-checked eight numbers, all exact, zero false positives. The discipline
that makes Pass 3 reliable is arithmetic, not expertise. Pass 4 has more expertise and less
arithmetic, and that is exactly where the false positives came from.

**The uncomfortable symmetry:** the site's documented disease is applying less rigour to its kills
than its claims. Today the *visitor track* did the same thing to the site — high-confidence kills,
unverified. The loop reproduced the pathology it exists to catch.
