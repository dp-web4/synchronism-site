# Topic: compute w_eff(z) from C(ρ̄(z)) — the site's "no coupling to w(z)" may itself be an over-claim

## Question

`/honest-assessment` ("What Remains Open") states:

> **Evolving dark energy (DESI DR2 w₀wₐ):** … A framework claiming density→coherence→growth machinery
> should register whether it has any coupling to w(z). **It does not: C(ρ) contains no dark-energy
> sector and no mechanism that modifies the expansion history** — which is itself a mark against
> cosmological scope. *(Added 2026-07-22 after an expert visitor flagged the silence.)*

Visitor Pass 4 (2026-08-10) contests the bolded clause on structural grounds:

> This is a framework whose entire content is a monotone function of density — which, in a homogeneous
> universe, is a monotone function of (1+z)³. There is an obvious, cheap, one-evening derivation
> available: propagate C(ρ̄(z)) into an effective w(z) and see whether it lands anywhere near the DR2
> (w₀, wₐ) contour.

The argument is hard to dismiss. If C multiplies the gravitational source (as it does in every force
law the site entertains) and ρ̄(z) ∝ (1+z)³, then C(ρ̄(z)) is *automatically* a function of redshift.
"No mechanism that modifies the expansion history" is then not an observation about the framework — it
is an untested assertion about a quantity nobody computed. **This program's most-repeated failure mode
is declaring things closed in the pessimistic direction without running them.** This has that shape.

## Why this is the highest-priority item in today's log despite not being a defect

Everything else in the 2026-08-10 visitor log is archaeology — audits of results already obtained. This
is the only forward-looking item, and it is the only identified route to something the program has
never had: **a prospective test, against a live instrument, with a real timeline.** DESI DR2's evolving
dark energy preference (2.8–4.2σ depending on SNe compilation) is the dominant live anomaly in
cosmology, and DR2 growth data lands ~Spring 2027. Every other registration on the books is either
executed, closed, underpowered, or self-eliminating; `/top-5-tests` was emptied of active
discriminating tests today.

## What to do

1. **Compute it.** Propagate C(ρ̄(z)) through to an effective w_eff(z) under each of the force-law
   branches the site keeps live, and overlay the DESI DR2 (w₀, wₐ) contour. State the branch — the
   force-law fork is unresolved and a result quoted without naming its branch is not interpretable.
2. **Expect it to land nowhere, and register that anyway.** A null here is still the first prospective
   registration with an instrument behind it. Register the prediction *before* DR2, with the threshold
   written down and dated — `/falsifiability` reports 0 of 24 tests completed prospectively, and this is
   a free opportunity to make that 1.
3. **Watch the two traps this program keeps falling into:**
   - **Declare the null by permutation, not by convention.** A "consistent with ΛCDM" verdict against a
     structurally wrong null is worthless.
   - **Name which nuisances were marginalised and which were fixed.** The a₀ "8σ" became 0.66σ purely
     because a published systematic was dropped; the ΔBIC +184 became ≈33 once correlated points were
     counted properly. Both were self-inflicted over-refutations. Do not add a third.
4. **Check the sign before claiming anything.** The existing mechanism-class work found the framework's
   growth-sector prediction fails *by sign* (C_galactic vs C_cosmic may be inverted). If w_eff(z) comes
   out with a sign that only works because of an orientation choice, that is the finding, not the w(z)
   number.
