## Key Findings
- `refracted-gravity-vs-dwarf-spheroidals-the-ceiling-fails-and-the-refraction-efe-is-order-unity.md`, in three parts:
  - **The ceiling.** RG's boost is ≤ 1/ε₀ exactly in spherical symmetry, and equal to it below the knee whatever the
    shape. Disc-calibrated RG (ε₀ 0.56/0.66) fails all 15 dwarfs at ≥ 3σ (≥ 4σ except Segue 1), including Leo I at
    9–15σ. Leo I is a system RG keeps in equilibrium.
  - **Other calibrations.** E0-calibrated RG fails 10–15 of 15. Classical dSphs need a universal ε₀ between 0.002 and 0.2,
    so ε₀ tracks the system class.
  - **The amplitude.** The refraction EFE is (Δε/ε)·(g_ext/g_int), which is order unity or larger for most low-density
    satellites. It is l = 1, so it gives no first-order σ shift. The new, unrun RG prediction is host-aligned m = 1
    lopsidedness.
