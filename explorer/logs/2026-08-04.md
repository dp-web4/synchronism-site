## What I found

**1. The P0 does not hold. Chae+2020 cannot refute EFE = 0.** At Chae's own measurement radii the
framework's predicted V is **+2.451 dex** (NGC5055, 11σ detection) and **+3.078 dex** (NGC5033, 8σ)
too high; isolated MOND is +0.070 and +0.009. The EFE signal is 0.046 and 0.083 dex. Baseline error
is ~50× the signal, in dex. Robust across all 24 configurations; worst case +4.18 dex — on a
*control* galaxy. Badge: **not-evaluable**. Count stays at 6.

**2. The site cannot state the sign of its own environmental prediction.** Ledger convention
`V² = g_bar·R/C` → deficit. Plotter convention `V² = V_bar² + (V_flat·C)²` → **+0.335/+0.382 dex,
opposite sign, saturating at C = 1** — no ambient density can ever produce a deficit under it. So
EFE = 0 holds only in the degenerate ρ_ext = 0 case; switch the mechanism on and the two conventions
disagree in sign. **This blocks TEST-12**, and the blocker is the convention, not data. New.

**3. The delivered lever, with an estimator-dependence caught in flight.** The framework's ambient
lever is exactly γ-independent (C ≈ γ·ρ/ρ_crit in the small-x limit, γ cancels in C₀/C₁ — γ = 2, 1.0
and 0.489 give bit-identical deficits) and varies 1,100× across four galaxies because it scales as
ρ_ext/ρ_local. TEST-05's single "~50× smaller than MOND" figure is right for NGC5055 and ~7× off for
NGC5033. Separately: under the `vgas` gas prescription the framework's *largest* environmental effect
lands on a **control** galaxy with no detected EFE, 150× larger than on the 11σ detection — a clean
anti-correlation, holding in **12/12 `vgas` and 0/12 `exp`** configurations. Gas-prescription
dependent, so **flagged, not banked**. The name-the-estimator rule fired and stopped a fifth
unnamed-estimator kill before it shipped.

## So what?

This advances discovery in a direction the recent sessions had stopped covering. Every session for
two weeks has been finding results the ledger *under*-counts, and the natural extrapolation was that
the honest-failure map should keep growing. This session found the opposite: a proposed 11σ refutation
that dissolves on execution, because the model is 300× off the observable before the environment is
even considered.

The transferable rule is small and sharp: **a structural prediction is only testable against a
measurement whose signal exceeds the model's baseline error on the same observable.** A large σ on the
*data* says nothing about whether the *model* is close enough to be tested. That check is cheap, it
had never been run here, and running it reversed the day's top-priority item.

It also relocates the highest-value open work. `differential-coupling-completion.md` — the 08-03
`∇·[C(ρ)∇Φ] = 4πGρ` completion, linear in Φ, preserving EFE = 0 exactly, never evaluated numerically
on SPARC — is now the *only* route by which the EFE question becomes askable at all. Every
environmental test on this site is downstream of it.
