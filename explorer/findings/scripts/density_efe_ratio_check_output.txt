EXTERNAL FIELD vs EXTERNAL DENSITY -- the two ratios side by side
==============================================================================
  g_int  = G Mbar / rh^2         (baryonic internal field at the half-light radius)
  g_ext  = G M_host / D^2
  rho_int= Mbar / (4/3 pi rh^3)  (mean baryonic density inside rh)
  rho_ext= n_ext * 1.4 m_p       (host circumgalactic medium)

  satellite       g_ext/g_int   rho_ext/rho_int    ratio of ratios
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  Crater II            31.130          1.10e-01           2.84e+02
  Fornax                0.070          2.59e-04           2.70e+02
  Sculptor              0.282          1.43e-04           1.98e+03
  Draco                 1.749          5.39e-04           3.24e+03
  Antlia II            32.909          4.02e-01           8.20e+01
  Segue 1             280.552          3.12e-03           9.00e+04
  NGC 1052-DF2          0.378          2.31e-03           1.63e+02
  Omega Cen             0.024          8.69e-08           2.76e+05

==============================================================================
VERDICT
==============================================================================
  g_ext/g_int      : median 1.064   range [0.024, 280.552]
  rho_ext/rho_int  : median 1.43e-03   range [8.7e-08, 4.0e-01]
  suppression      : median 1.13e+03   -> 3.1 orders of magnitude

  MOND's EFE is a real effect because g_ext/g_int ~ O(0.1-1): the external
  field is a sizeable fraction of the internal one, so it changes mu.
  The proposed external DENSITY effect requires the same of rho.  It fails by
  ~3 orders of magnitude, for every satellite, in both hosts.

  Reason, stated structurally: g is sourced NON-LOCALLY (a 1/r^2 tail from a
  10^12 Msun host reaches the satellite undiminished by what lies between);
  rho is the LOCAL value, and the local value at a satellite is the satellite.
  This is the program's own locality asymmetry (2026-08-19), now applied to the
  EFE channel: the same property that makes rho carry <=0.7% of RAR variance
  makes rho_ext carry ~10^-8 of the satellite's own rho.

  disk.  There is no such environment in a satellite orbit.

==============================================================================
WHAT THE FRAMEWORK PREDICTS INSTEAD
==============================================================================
  rho_ext/rho_int ~ 1e-8  =>  Delta ln C ~ (dlnC/dlnrho) * 1e-8.  Even at the
  steepest allowed slope (dlnC/dlnrho <= gamma <= 2), the fractional change in
  the boost between the densest and sparsest satellite environments is < 1e-7.
  Observed satellite velocity dispersions carry ~10-20% errors.

  => The external DENSITY effect is not a discriminator.  It is not weak, or
     hard, or expensive: it is ~6 orders of magnitude below the noise floor of
     the best sample that will ever exist, and no improvement in the data
     changes that.  It is closed A PRIORI, by one division.
