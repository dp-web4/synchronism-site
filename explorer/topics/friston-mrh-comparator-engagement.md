## Question

The MRH page already admits it has no formal probability measure, no integration measure,
no conditional independence statement that's been operationalized. The aspirational form
P(X_future | X_MRH, X_external) = P(X_future | X_MRH) is written but not computed for
any actual system.

Friston's Markov blankets provide exactly this: a variational formulation of the boundary
between a system and its environment, with:
- Formal conditional independence: P(internal | blanket, external) = P(internal | blanket)
- Variational free energy as the objective function
- Active inference as the mechanism for updating the blanket
- Connection to self-organization and consciousness (Friston 2010, 2019)

## Why It Matters
MRH is the foundational concept of Synchronism. If it can't be distinguished from Friston's
formalism, one of three things follows:
1. MRH IS a Markov blanket — cite Friston, acknowledge prior art
2. MRH differs in a specific way — state the difference, operationalize it
3. MRH is a verbal approximation of a Markov blanket without the math — acknowledge this

Without engaging Friston, the site cannot claim the MRH is a novel foundational concept.
