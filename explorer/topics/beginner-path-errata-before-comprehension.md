# Topic: The beginner path teaches errata before it teaches the idea

## Question

Should the beginner path (Why Synchronism → First Encounter → The Core Idea → Two Reframes →
Dark Matter → Honest Assessment → Glossary) be restructured so corrections and refutation caveats
land *after* a reader has understood the base idea, rather than interleaved into the first
teaching pass?

## Context

A 2026-07-28 visitor pass (casual-enthusiast persona, "high" severity) hit this concretely: by
Step 3 of 6 ("The Core Idea"), the page already carries two dated correction boxes, a "this scaling
is refuted" caveat, and a note that 6 of 6 previously-"Validated" claims were demoted — before the
reader has finished learning what C(ρ), γ, or ρ_crit even are. Verbatim: *"I hadn't even finished
learning the idea and I was already reading its errata... It's like being handed a recipe where
half the ingredients are crossed out and there's a note saying 'this used to be wrong for four
months.' I don't know enough to know which parts survived."* The same persona flagged
`/two-reframes` as sold as "the no-equations path" but landing Kochen–Specker, Tsirelson, PR-boxes,
and CHSH numbers with zero definitions — the most jargon-dense page on the site, worn as the
easiest one.

Separately, the same persona's Honest Assessment feedback was unreservedly positive — "the page I'd
send to a friend" — with a specific callout: its "what a non-physicist should take away" section is
the one place the site clearly writes for a first-time reader, and "that voice should be running
the beginner path too."

## Why It Matters

The site's own culture (per CLAUDE.md) is "teach the claim, then surface what would break it" —
claims are stakes in the ground, not hedges. The current beginner path inverts that order in
practice: hedges arrive mid-explanation, before the claim has landed. This isn't a request to
soften the honesty — the honesty is what earns the site trust, per every persona in this pass — it's
a sequencing problem. The scars are, in the enthusiast's words, "actually the most interesting
part, and right now they're wasted on me because I don't yet know what got cut."

## Suggested Starting Points

- `/the-core-idea` (or `/core-idea` — confirm canonical route) — where the correction boxes
  currently interrupt the first explanation of γ and ρ_crit.
- `/two-reframes` — candidate split: keep the CRT/pendulum-clock analogies as the equation-free
  beginner page; move Kochen–Specker/Tsirelson/CHSH content to a separate, linked page for readers
  who want the theorem-level version.
  `/honest-assessment`'s "What a non-physicist should take away" section — the voice the visitor
  wants extended backward into the beginner path.
- Consider: does every Failed-badged page (`/dark-matter` was the visitor's example) need a
  one-line "what's still standing" summary near the top, in plain words, before the caveat list?
