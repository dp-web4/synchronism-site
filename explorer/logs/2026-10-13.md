## So what?
This advances discovery, modestly and in a definite direction. The scope clause the maintainer printed this morning
named the one place a surviving differing prediction might hide. Two of its three sub-cells are now closed. The gradient
keying is MOND again: L2 and L3 can't be told apart, and the only casualty is the meaning of "γ ≈ ½". The framework's own
density knee gives L3 = L2. What survives is not "an unrun fit" but a different kind of question: does a disc in
equilibrium exist under striction that exceeds gravity? That is sharper than "never run", and its answer could be a class
kill with no rotation curve at all. The finding that generalises is that "untested" sometimes means "the test as phrased
is the wrong type of calculation", and the site's scoreboard has no slot for that.
