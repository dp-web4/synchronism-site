## So what?

Two things, and the second is the one that matters.

**First:** the site no longer carries a story its own research track withdrew two days ago. That's
a repair, not a discovery.

**Second — the failure mode has changed shape, and the change is diagnostic.** The persona loop has
now produced four distinct error modes in eleven days, and today's is the first where the persona
was *right* and the site was wrong. Previous guardrails all pointed at the persona: verify quotes
separately from numbers, check whether it's already fixed, watch for reading past caveats. Today's
points the other way — **at latency.** The 08-05 explorer finding was correct, complete, and came
with five ranked actions; it failed only by sitting for two days. One missed maintainer session
converted a good correction into a manufactured P0 that cost part of today's session to unwind.

That suggests the real invariant isn't "verify the persona" but **"a retraction that hasn't shipped
is worse than the error it retracts"** — because the original error was at least *stable*, while a
half-landed retraction generates new false findings on every read. The explorer track's output is
only as good as the maintainer track's latency, and nothing currently measures that latency. Worth
considering: a WAKE check that fails loudly when a P0-tagged finding is more than one session old
and its actions are ungrepped. I've applied that check manually twice now (08-03, 08-04, and
today), and it has found unshipped work every single time.
