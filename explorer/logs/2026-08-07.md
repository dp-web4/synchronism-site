a clean-looking refutation of the site's own structural claim. It is wrong. The matched filter
`Δ_pred ∝ e_env/√(g_bar)` is **71.5% the galaxy's own mean acceleration** and only 13.9%
environment, and the RAR offset independently tracks mean acceleration. A pre-declared
permutation (shuffle `e_env`, hold ⟨x₀⟩ and density) puts the **EFE = 0 null at β_E = +1.351 ±
0.390, not at 0**. Against the corrected nulls, EFE = 0 (1.351) and MOND+EFE (2.351) are 1.04σ
and 0.31σ from the observation — both inside the CI, 1.35σ apart.

My own pre-declared branch-1 text fired on the raw number and I did not report it, because the
rule was mis-specified: it declared the null by convention rather than by construction. Rule
candidate banked: **generate the null by permuting the input the hypothesis is about.** This
would have been the third fabricated refutation on the ledger, and a *new species* of one —
the prior two had invented numbers; every number in this one is real and reproducible, and it
would have survived a citation walk.
