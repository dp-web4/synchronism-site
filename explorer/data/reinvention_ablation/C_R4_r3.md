**Short answer:** The construction is not new, and its central claim, that Planck-suppressed Lorentz violation makes the grid unobservable, is contested in the literature.

**Substrate: known**
- A discrete cubic lattice with global ticks and local update rules is a cellular automaton or "digital physics" model. Precedents are Zuse, *Rechnender Raum* (1969), and Fredkin and Wolfram (e.g., *A New Kind of Science*, 2002).
- 't Hooft's cellular automaton interpretation of quantum mechanics (book, Springer 2016, arXiv:1405.1548) is the closest modern version.
- Local transfer of a conserved quantity between adjacent cells is a quantum lattice gas or quantum walk. Relevant papers are:
  - Bialynicki-Birula, PRD 49, 6920 (1994).
  - Meyer, J. Stat. Phys. 85, 551 (1996) (uncertain on page).
  - D'Ariano and Perinotti, PRA 90, 062106 (2014).
  - Bisio, D'Ariano and Tosini, Ann. Phys. 354, 244 (2015).
- The quantum cellular automaton papers derive Dirac and Weyl dynamics with emergent Lorentz symmetry at low momentum. They also find deformed dispersion and covariance at the Planck scale.
- Emergent Lorentz invariance in a preferred-frame system goes back to Chadha and Nielsen, Nucl. Phys. B217, 125 (1983).

**"Violation suppressed by (E/E_Pl)^n, hence unobservable": challenged**
- Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia and Vucetich, PRL 93, 191301 (2004), argue that a fixed preferred frame with a Planck-scale cutoff generically produces unsuppressed O(1) Lorentz-violating dimension-4 operators through radiative corrections. This is a fine-tuning problem. A construction that merely asserts E/E_Pl suppression needs a symmetry or mechanism to justify it, and the program should address this paper directly.
- Myers and Pospelov, PRL 90, 211601 (2003), show that dimension-5 operators give linear E/E_Pl effects. Fermi observations of GRB 090510 (Abdo et al., Nature 462, 331 (2009)) constrain linear photon dispersion to roughly above the Planck scale (uncertain on the exact bound).
- Birefringence limits from the Crab polarization and from gamma-ray bursts are stronger still.
- Gambini and Pullin, PRD 59, 124021 (1999), is a related discrete-spacetime paper on Lorentz violation from loop quantum gravity-motivated lattices (uncertain on details).
- The Standard-Model Extension of Colladay and Kostelecký (PRD 58, 116002 (1998)) is the standard framework for bounding the resulting operators. Lattice artifacts are the cubic-anisotropy terms, and the cubic symmetry only guarantees rotational invariance at leading order.

**Alternatives that avoid a preferred frame:** causal sets (Bombelli, Lee, Meyer and Sorkin, PRL 59, 521 (1987); Dowker, Henson and Sorkin 2004, Mod. Phys. Lett. A19, 1829). These keep Lorentz invariance exactly, in contrast to a rigid grid with absolute time.

**Assessment:** I know of no published work with exactly this "intent pattern" formulation. This may be a relabeling of a quantum cellular automaton or lattice gas with an absolute time slicing. The "unobservable" conclusion is not established and is in tension with the Collins et al. result and with the Fermi bounds. I cannot rule out unpublished or very recent work (post-2025) that I don't know of.
