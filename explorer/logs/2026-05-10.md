  targets is forced by monotonicity, not by Synchronism's specific functional
  form. Numerical verification: Synchronism's C(ρ, γ=2) gives r=0.93–0.98 on

  (Tc-shell-oscillations, melting-point-period-jumps). This is consistent with
  C(ρ) being a monotonic compander as diagnosed in the companion finding.

## Rabbit Holes (Worth Revisiting)

1. **Susceptibility reframe of γ = 2/√N_corr.** If C(ρ) is a static susceptibility
   χ(q→0), then γ may have a Kubo derivation via fluctuation-dissipation. The
   factor of 1/√N_corr falls out of variance-of-correlated-units; the factor of
   2 is the open question. This is the most productive thread for next session
   — and sharpens the existing `ncorr-from-hamiltonian-protocol` topic.

2. **Hill-coefficient origin of the +1 regulator.** The "+1" in ln(ρ/ρ_crit + 1)
   resembles the unbound state in a Hill/Langmuir partition function. Is the
   regulator a relic of a binding-equilibrium mass-action law that was never made
   explicit? If yes, the framework gains Hill kinetics' predictive structure.
   If no, the +1 is purely cosmetic and should be ablation-tested (does the SPARC
   fit degrade substantially without it?).

3. **Compander predictions for galaxy rotation.** Companders have well-defined
   knee softness, threshold, ratio, and (for dynamic versions) attack/release.
   Can the SPARC fit be re-described in compander parameter space rather than
   borrowed-MOND parameter space? This would give the framework a clean parameter
   inventory in its own class.

4. **Re-audit of "8-way convergence on C ≈ 0.50".** The convergence is on
   *tanh*'s inflection (which is at 0.5 by symmetry), not on *C(ρ)*'s mid-amplitude
   (which is at ρ ≈ 0.32 ρ_crit for γ=2, where C is also 0.5 — but ρ_crit itself
   is at C ≈ 0.88, not 0.5). The "consciousness threshold at C=0.5" is a pure
   tanh property, not a property of the C(ρ) function the framework actually
   uses. This deepens the existing geometric-not-empirical caveat by another
   layer.

5. **Sort chemistry "successes" vs "failures" by monotonicity.** The
   chemistry-null-model finding predicts: high-r targets are monotonic-in-Z
   (sound velocity, electronegativity, atomic volume); low-r targets are
   non-monotonic (Tc with shell oscillations, melting points with period
   jumps). Confirming this sort directly on the gamma-boundary dataset would
   make the compander diagnosis empirically tight.

## Self-Seeded Questions

1. **If the framework owns the compander reframe, what does the landing page say?**
   "What if one *response function* described saturating coherence from quantum
   to cosmic?" invites the right evaluation criterion (compander class) rather
   than the wrong one (phase transition class). Worth drafting a landing-page
   alternative for maintainer review.

2. **Does any genuine phase-transition framework have predictive structure that
   Synchronism currently borrows by analogy?** E.g., MIPT (measurement-induced
   phase transitions) genuinely *is* a phase transition. The 2026-04-11 finding
   already mapped C(ρ) → MIPT structurally. Question: under the compander reframe,
   does the MIPT mapping survive as conceptual analogy or collapse entirely?
   I think it survives only as analogy — a compander-of-density-coherence isn't
   a phase transition no matter what real phase transitions resemble it.

3. **What is the susceptibility-reframe analog of TEST-04a?** Under the
   phase-transition framing, fσ8 suppression came from "coherence-mediated
   suppression of structure growth" — a mechanism-class failure when the sign
   came out wrong. Under the susceptibility framing, the prediction would have
   been: "the structure-growth response χ(z) is reduced at low z by compander
   knee saturation" — and this either still predicts suppression (failed by
   sign), or admits enhancement under a different parameter regime. Worth
   revisiting whether the sign-failure is a parameter-regime issue rather than
   a mechanism-class issue.

4. **The "+1 ablation test."** Run SPARC fit with C(ρ) = tanh(γ·ln(ρ/ρ_crit))
   (no regulator). At ρ=0 the function diverges, but the data has finite ρ.
   Compare fit quality to C(ρ) = tanh(γ·ln(ρ/ρ_crit + 1)). If they're close,
   the +1 is cosmetic. If +1 substantially helps, the +1 has *some* physical
   content (regulator that approximates a binding-equilibrium baseline) and the
   Hill-kinetics reframe gains support.
