**Short answer:** the construction is not new. Its core coincides with several well-studied frameworks, and its "unobservable" conclusion conflicts with known results. I'm citing from memory, so treat details as uncertain where flagged.

**Prior art for the substrate**
- **Digital physics and cellular automata:** Zuse, *Rechnender Raum* (1969); Fredkin and Toffoli–Margolus on reversible CA; Wolfram, *A New Kind of Science* (2002).
- **Deterministic CA underlying quantum mechanics:** 't Hooft, *The Cellular Automaton Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics* (Springer, 2016; arXiv:1405.1548).
- **Quantum cellular automata (QCA) with emergent Lorentz and Dirac dynamics:**
  - Bialynicki-Birula, Phys. Rev. D 49, 6920 (1994), which gives Weyl, Dirac and Maxwell equations as lattice unitary CAs.
  - D'Ariano & Perinotti, Phys. Rev. A 90, 062106 (2014), "Derivation of the Dirac equation from principles of information processing".
  - Bisio, D'Ariano, Tosini and collaborators (several papers) on emergent Lorentz symmetry and the Planck-scale CA. Exact citations are uncertain.
- **Discrete-time lattice with a preferred frame:** this is the generic setting of Lorentz-violation phenomenology. Related work includes Hořava–Lifshitz gravity (Hořava, PRD 79, 084008, 2009) and Einstein-aether theory (Jacobson & Mattingly, PRD 64, 024028, 2001).

The "intent" patterns hopping between adjacent cells reduce to a walker or amplitude or occupation field on a lattice, which is a QCA or classical CA. I know of no published model that uses "intent" as a concept. If it is not defined operationally, I'd consider that part unpublished, but also not physically meaningful yet.

**The suppression claim is a known problem**
- **Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia, Vucetich**, PRL 93, 191301 (2004), "Lorentz invariance and quantum gravity: an additional fine-tuning problem?" They show that a Planck-scale cutoff or lattice with a preferred frame generically induces unsuppressed O(1) Lorentz violation in dimension-4 operators through radiative corrections. The stated E/E_Planck suppression therefore does not follow automatically. It requires a symmetry or fine-tuning, and the program has to supply one.
- **Dimension-5 operators:** Myers & Pospelov, PRL 91, 211601 (2003) show that these would give linear E/E_Pl effects.
- **Linear-in-E/E_Pl dispersion is already excluded:** Fermi-LAT GRB 090510 (Abdo et al., Nature 462, 331, 2009) and Vasileiou et al., PRD 87, 122001 (2013). Crab Nebula and ultra-high-energy cosmic ray constraints also push dimension-5 bounds above E_Pl, and dimension-6 bounds are strong too. These are the Jacobson–Liberati–Mattingly line of work (Nature 424, 1019, 2003 for synchrotron). Exact numbers are uncertain.
- **Lattice-specific issues:** a cubic lattice gives anisotropic dispersion (rotation breaking at order a²p²) and fermion doubling (Nielsen–Ninomiya). The program must address both.

**Verdict:** the grid with global ticks is a special case of known QCA, CA and preferred-frame lattice models. The claim that Lorentz violation is suppressed, and the grid therefore unobservable, is not established. It is contradicted by the Collins et al. argument unless the program adds a protecting mechanism, and it faces strong observational constraints. I know of no publication of the "intent" formalism itself.
