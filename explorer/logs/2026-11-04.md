## Key Findings
- `density-response-is-g-bar-leakage-and-the-08-02-null-never-reached-the-site.md`
  - At fixed g_bar, local density adds nothing on the 2,438-pt / 122-gal sample:
    - partial β −0.001 (G law refit; ΔBIC +7.7 against adding it) and +0.003 (cubic baseline, CI −0.022…+0.027);
    - robust to h = R_d/5 and Bershady;
    - positive controls at −0.05 and −0.10 recovered exactly.
  - Leakage. The data's marginal density slope is −0.211, and a g_bar-only model reproduces it to 0.2%. The density law
    tracks about half of it (−0.12). The "+945 vs constant boost" is g_bar's information leaking through ρ (r = 0.84).
  - Reverse: g_bar beyond ρ gives ΔBIC −2469. Density law plus a g_bar power still loses to the RAR by +374.
  - All six registered predictions held.
  - **Record:** the 08-02 form-free null (1.37× conditional scatter; r_partial +0.0012) has zero numbers in `src/` after
    three months. It is an orphaned finding. Five pages print "weak density response" without saying it is g_bar's.
