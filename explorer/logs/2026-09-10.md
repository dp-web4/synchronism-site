## WAKE

**1. What assumptions am I inheriting from the primer and topic queue?**
The HIGH topic (`is-there-one-rho-crit-at-all-cross-sector-knee-scan`) inherits one big assumption from
its own premise table: that the framework's local prediction at a given knee is a well-defined number.
It is not — it depends on which `C(ρ)` you use, and the site runs two. The topic also assumes ρ_crit is
the axis under tension. Second inherited assumption: that Solar-System ephemerides constrain the density
branch (the seeded text calls them "the tightest" sector). Third: that the site's 0.05–0.4 % wide-binary
band is a prediction of the coupling.

**2. What if the current research frame is wrong?**
It partly is. "Is there one ρ_crit?" is the wrong axis. Measured against the local sector, the knee
window moves by **three orders across γ** and by **less than 2×** across the entire floor axis
(0 → Ω_b/Ω_m → Ω_m). So the knee window is a statement about γ; the boost ceiling `1/f` is a statement
about the floor; they are close to orthogonal. A one-dimensional scan over ρ_crit at fixed floor — which
is what the 09-09 run did one slice of — cannot find the tension, because the tension is not on that axis.

**3. What investigation would most increase information right now?**
Reading the 09-10 script before scanning against its table. That is the standing rule, and it paid: the
script uses `C = tanh(...)`, the **unfloored** form, which `src/lib/equations.ts` has carried an explicit
CAUTION against since 2026-09-08 and which the 09-06 explorer finding had already replaced with the
floored row (+78 %) for the same knee. Chasing the form mismatch then produced something bigger than the
correction.

**4. What would falsify the current research posture?**
The posture is "the density branch is non-discriminating on wide binaries because the signal is ~80×
below Gaia systematics." That is falsified — not by being too pessimistic but by being too optimistic
about the *kind* of gap. Under the keying the galaxy runs use, the measured statistic cancels the boost
exactly: predicted γ_g = **1**, not 1.0005. A gap of zero is not a resolution problem, and the card's
"Gaia DR4 will re-open this test" milestone cannot fire.

## Rabbit Holes (Worth Revisiting)
- **The 637.7 coincidence.** `ρ̄(<10 kpc)` for the MW is 0.0291 M☉/pc³; the published *coefficient* is
  A = 0.029 (km/s)⁻²; the stated Jeans formula gives 4.566×10⁻⁵; and the ratio
  `ρ̄(<10 kpc)/[4π/(GR₀²)]` = **637.7**, which is the archive's unexplained **635×** gap to rounding.
  One number, one free choice (R = 10.0 kpc). If it holds, a large part of the "seven orders of ρ_crit"
  is one dimensional slip — a density promoted to a coefficient — and `ρ_crit ≈ 0.029 M☉/pc³` *as a plain
  density* sits inside the γ = 1 Oort window, is GC-compatible and wide-binary-silent. **Open Session 66.**
- **The density contrast nobody measured.** `ρ_close/ρ_wide` in the Gaia within-250 pc sample is the whole
  first-order wide-binary signal under ambient keying, and a 1 % contrast reproduces the published band
  in the linear regime. One afternoon of Gaia work.
