**2. What if the current research frame is wrong?**

The frame I most doubted was "the galaxy sector is the sector." Everything in this program's ledger
is SPARC, SPARC-adjacent, or cosmological. But the framework's own knee, at every placement, sits at
densities **no galaxy reaches** — so the entire empirical record has been accumulated in the regime
where C(ρ) is a constant rescaling of G, and the function's actual content has never been sampled.
Globular clusters are the only bound stellar systems whose internal profile sweeps through it. That
made the frame question and the experiment the same thing.

  because of its external field effect and for no other reason** (EFE off scores −0.245, statistically

  host's 220 km/s is dead on a second observable**: it saturates the whole cluster at the coherence
  floor, dividing stellar M/L_V by 3.175 and pushing 96 % of the catalogue below M/L_V = 1.2.
  **Refracted Gravity passes at its own fitted ρ_c = 10⁻²⁴·²⁵ g/cm³ = 0.0083 M☉/pc³**, across its
  1σ range and even with this framework's Ω_m floor substituted in — so the *form* is not what
  fails, the *number* is, by a factor of 13–500.

## Rabbit Holes (Worth Revisiting)

- **The γ ladder.** P611.2 implies γ = 2 for *any* resolved-member system. Open clusters, dwarf
  spheroidals and Gaia stellar streams all have public kinematics and sit near the exclusion
  window's lower edge. If dwarfs demand 0.489, the Markov-blanket reset is ad hoc after all.
- **A galaxy-sector refit pinned at RG's ρ_c = 0.0083 M☉/pc³.** Every SPARC fit in this program's
  record used a knee 10³–10⁵× higher. Cesare+2020's answer for RG is that it works — which would
  make the chosen knee, not the physics, the thing SPARC has been selecting against.
- **Q, the transition width.** RG carries an explicit width (0.47 dex); the compander's width is
  locked to γ. Since fitting γ is out-of-sample *harmful* (2026-08-20), a width parameter that is
  not γ may be where the real freedom lives.
- **NGC 2419 and the outer-halo clusters** (R_GC 20–90 kpc, g_ext ≪ a₀) are where MOND and the
  density law stop being collinear. They have 1–3 dispersion bins — too few. 3–4 nights of
  spectroscopy is the cleanest discriminating measurement this framework could name.
