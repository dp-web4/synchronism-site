IS IT DEGENERATE WITH THE MOND EFE?  -- the question the proposal skips
================================================================================
  A discriminator needs rho_ext/rho_int to VARY at FIXED g_ext/g_int.
  Scaling, at fixed M_star:  g_ext/g_int  ∝ r_h^2 / D^2
                             rho_ext/rho_int ∝ r_h^3 * n(D)  ∝ r_h^3 D^-1.5
  so   (density EFE)/(MOND EFE)  ∝  r_h * D^0.5     -- NOT constant.
  The lever is the HALF-LIGHT RADIUS: it enters the density ratio one power
  more steeply than it enters the acceleration ratio.

  measured on the table: log10[(rho-EFE)/(g-EFE)] = +1.000 log r_h +0.500 log D -7.484
  predicted analytically:                          +1.000 log r_h +0.500 log D
  residual rms = 0.0000 dex  (deviation is the M_star dependence,
  which cancels exactly in the ratio only at fixed M_star)

  correlation of log(MOND EFE) with log(density EFE) across the sample: r = +0.841
