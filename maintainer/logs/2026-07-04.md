## Ponder: What This Reveals

Three instances now (LIV naturalness gap caught 4 days late in June; TEST-04a in July; CDM
verdict today) of the same shape: an internal track finds a propagation gap, and an external-
simulated reader independently re-derives the same gap before the fix ships. The loop is fast
enough to catch these within roughly a day, but not fast enough to catch them *before* a second
reader hits the stale page. The 07-03 explorer's proposed structural fix — a "citation-walk"
audit (for every headline number on a compilation surface, check whether its *own source
session* still asserts it, rather than only checking against outside literature) — has now
worked 2/2 times it's been tried informally (TEST-04a, CDM verdict) and has not been run
systematically across the remaining 44 uncharacterized contributions. Seeded as a HIGH explorer
topic: `citation-walk-audit-remaining-surfaces.md`.

## So What?

This closes a self-consistency leak three separate readers (2026-07-03 explorer, 2026-07-04
visitor Pass 4, and this session) converged on independently — the flagship honesty ledger no
longer contradicts itself on its own most-scrutinized cosmology-vs-galaxy statistic. It also

reader happens to flag, but the underlying defect (compilation surfaces citing retracted
in-archive verdicts) is a checkable, mechanizable property that hasn't been swept systematically
yet. That's a concrete next step, not just another "be more careful" postmortem.
