**Assessment: not correct as stated. The central claim is unsupported and probably false in its naive form.**

**Main problem: the suppression claim**
A lattice with a global absolute time breaks boosts completely, and that breaking is not small at the cutoff. Once interactions are included, loop corrections feed the breaking into the low-energy theory, and the effective field theory has no reason to keep it suppressed. Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia and Vucetich (2004) showed that Planck-scale Lorentz violation generically induces dimension-4 operators, such as species-dependent limiting speeds, with coefficients of order g²/16π², **unsuppressed by E/E_P**. Existing bounds on these coefficients are around 10⁻¹⁵ to 10⁻²⁰ or tighter. So "suppressed by powers of E/E_P" holds only if the program supplies one of the following:
- a symmetry that protects it,
- an explicit calculation, or
- extreme fine-tuning.

Even dimension-5 operators suppressed by a single power of 1/E_P are already constrained to about or beyond the Planck scale by gamma-ray burst timing and polarization. A "Planck-suppressed" grid is therefore not automatically invisible, and linear suppression is probably excluded.

**Other weaknesses**
- **Undefined dynamics.** "Intent patterns" have no stated state space, update rule, action or Hamiltonian, so nothing can be derived or falsified.
- **Anisotropic causal structure.** Nearest-neighbour transfer per tick gives an L¹-type (diamond or cube) causal cone, not a spherical one. Isotropy can emerge for long-wavelength group velocities, as in quantum walks, but the maximal signalling speed stays anisotropic. The program should show that this is harmless.
- **Standard lattice obstructions.** Fermion doubling (Nielsen–Ninomiya), chiral gauge theories, and gauge invariance on a discrete spacetime all need to be addressed.
- **No gravity.** A fixed grid with absolute time has no obvious route to diffeomorphism invariance or to the equivalence principle. A preferred frame, presumably the CMB frame, also needs reconciling with GR.
- **Quantum mechanics.** There is no account of unitarity or the Born rule, and nothing on how superposition and entanglement are realized.
- **Unfalsifiability.** The claim "the grid is unobservable" is a prediction that existing data can already test, and it has not been tested.

**Strengths**
- The substrate is conceptually simple and constructive, and it is related to well-studied cellular-automaton and quantum-walk programs.
- Emergent low-energy isotropy is plausible, since cubic symmetry forbids rotation-violating rank-2 terms at leading order.
- Discreteness gives a natural UV regulator.

**What to do next**
1. Write down explicit dynamics: state space, update rule and symmetries.
2. Derive the dispersion relation, including the O(k²a²) corrections, and the light-cone structure.
3. Add interacting fields and compute the radiatively induced Lorentz-violating coefficients in the Standard-Model Extension framework. Compare them with the published bounds.
4. Identify a protection mechanism, such as a symmetry or an RG-flow argument à la Chadha–Nielsen, or concede fine-tuning.
5. Address fermion doubling, gauge invariance and gravity.
6. State concrete, falsifiable predictions, such as specific dispersion coefficients or preferred-frame effects, rather than asserting unobservability.
