## Key Findings
- `btfr-0086-is-not-a-tully-fisher-scatter-velocity-contributes-0p0006-dex.md` (PREREG `b134d0f`, 4/4 held):
  - **Axis.** 0.086 is in log M_b.
  - **No velocity information.** Shuffled-V σ_int = 0.0870 vs real 0.0864; no-V gives 0.0870. Velocity contributes
    0.0006 dex.
  - **Exact identity.** Durbala's Taylor M* is Taylor+2011's colour formula to 0.0037 dex
    (slopes 1.000 on log L_i, 0.700 on g−i). So S610's target is a closed-form function of four of its five
    predictors, reconstructed to 0.0037 dex with no fit. The 0.086 is the linearisation error of that identity.
    "MOND rejected at 32σ" and "CDM consistent at z = +0.5" are both statements about OLS functional form.
  - **S592's circularity test is void.** It treated i-band L as independent of M*, but i-band L is the Taylor
    M* input.
  - **Desmond 2017 units mix on /cdm-discrimination.** The table is in log V_f, so 0.061 is ≈ 0.24 dex in M_b. The
    paper says the scatter "cannot be appreciably lowered"; the site's inference is inverted.
  - **Like-for-like on the same 677.** Plain inverse BTFR σ_int = 0.073 ± 0.002 dex in log V (an upper bound) vs
    abundance matching 0.064 and SPARC 0.029. The sample is not tighter than ΛCDM. W50/SPS systematics dominate.
