# PRIMARY, Round 1

## 1. Label: **(A) Interpretive analogy only**

I am not defending (B). The CRT passage as written ("nothing changes," "no quantum mystery," "perfect") overreaches. As a replacement theory it fails on known no-go results, which I list below. Defending (A) is the only position I can hold honestly.

## 2. Bounded claim (one sentence)

The CRT analogy illustrates that one unchanged periodic process can yield qualitatively different records (smooth "wave-like" integrated images vs. localized "particle-like" point samples) depending only on the detector's sampling window relative to the process period, so some "wave vs. particle" appearances are attributable to temporal sampling rather than to the system switching modes.

## 3. What it does NOT claim (one sentence)

It does not claim to derive the Born rule, reproduce Bell/CHSH violations, or show that measurement leaves the system undisturbed, so "no quantum mystery" is a heuristic motivation and not an established result.

## 4. Experiment classes it must address to avoid being "just metaphor"

**a) Bell/CHSH tests (loophole-free).** "Raster entanglement" (shared-origin synchronized cycling) is structurally a local hidden-variable model. Such models are bounded by S ≤ 2, and experiments observe S ≈ 2.4–2.8. Unless Synchronism supplies a non-local or measurement-dependent mechanism, §5.4's "Bell's theorem makes perfect sense" is unsupported. Under (A), I concede the CRT picture explains classical-style correlation only, not the violation.

**b) Which-path and quantum-eraser experiments.** §5.3 says path detection is "resampling, not decoherence," while §5.5 says path marking "requires intent pattern modification." These conflict. I resolve the conflict toward §5.5: back-action exists, and "the screen is unchanged" holds only for the narrow sampling-window case, not for interaction-based measurement.

**c) Contextuality (Kochen-Specker/Leggett-Garg).** Definite pre-existing cycle phases must be shown consistent with context-dependent outcomes. No mapping is currently given.

**d) A quantitative discriminator, which is what would lift this above metaphor.** The analogy needs one testable prediction: interference visibility V as an explicit function of detector integration time τ and pattern period T, for example V(τ/T), with a form distinct from standard decoherence/time-resolution models. Without that, a formal mapping from CRT parameters to QM observables does not exist.

## Provisional scope statement

The Planck-scale "scan rate" of 1.855×10⁴³ Hz is not an operational sampling variable for any lab detector. So the §5.1 "technical implementation" is currently labeled, not mapped. I flag this now rather than wait to be pressed.

## Provisional status

**Narrowed.** Surviving content: a sampling-window intuition for wave/particle appearance in time-integrated vs. single-event detection. Not defended: the "no observer effect" universal claim, the Bell resolution, and the "perfect" and "completely dissolved" language.

**Would settle:** a derived V(τ/T) with a checkable deviation from standard QM, plus an explicit statement of how the model evades Bell's bound (or concession that it does not).

I invite the Challenger to press on the formal mapping and on whether (A) is too thin to count as a "claim" at all.
