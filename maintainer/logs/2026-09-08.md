implicit μ on g_obs.* Without it, the form-selection table — the framework's one clean quantitative result — was
uncheckable, and two strangers proved it by checking. With it, every downstream claim (γ = ½ ≡ simple μ, a₀′ = a₀/2,

registered prediction sitting on the one class of object that does. Run at the registered parameter, the density law
does not die; it splits — into a universal-γ branch that is dead and a per-blanket branch that is marginal and costs
the "one equation" slogan to keep. That is more information than a kill: it names the exact price of survival. And it
was found because the explorer read the registration before executing, under a warning written the morning before.
