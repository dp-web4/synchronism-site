**Research gap found, not just site gap.**

Pass 3 made a clean mathematical observation that deserves a research note: `C(ρ) = tanh(γ·ln(ρ/ρ_crit+1))` is **strictly concave for all ρ > 0**. The +1 regulator is what turns the function from "sigmoid with interior inflection" to "strictly concave compander."

This is the mathematical exactification of the "compander-class diagnosis" filed 2026-05-10.
