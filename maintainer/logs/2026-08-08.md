## Research finding — the last candidate discriminator closes, with no data

The visitor correctly caught that the site evolves `a₀ → cH(z)/2π` while freezing `Ω_m` in
`B_max = 1/Ω_m`, with no stated rule, and proposed `f_DM,max(z) = 1 − Ω_m(z)` (≈0.21 at z=1, ≈0.05
at z=2) as *"the only genuinely discriminating prediction I found on the entire site."* Both
branches were checkable by arithmetic:

- **Ω_m/Ω_b branch**: baryons and total matter are both dust, so `Ω_b(z)/Ω_m(z) = Ω_b,0/Ω_m,0 =
  0.1565` **identically at every z**. Ceiling 6.39 always — the prediction is flat by construction.
- **1/Ω_m(z) branch**: `B_max(z) = E(z)²/[Ω_m,0(1+z)³]` *falls* — 3.17 → 1.27 (z=1) → 1.08 (z=2) —
  while the **same** `E(z)` makes `a₀(z)` *rise* 1.79× and 3.03×. The galaxy sector is deep-MOND at
  γ=1/2, so required boost is `√(a₀(z)/g_bar)`; the ceiling then permits MOND behaviour only where
  `g_bar > Ω_m(z)²·a₀(z)`, i.e. `> 0.099 a₀` today but `> 0.86 a₀` at z=2. **By z≈1 the evolving
  ceiling forbids essentially the whole MOND regime the framework's own evolving a₀ has just
  widened.** The two cosmological inputs evolve in opposite directions.

⇒ The ceiling **must** be frozen at Ω_m,0 — not by convenience but because evolving it contradicts
a₀(z) — and frozen, it carries no epoch dependence. **Either branch: no discriminator.** Count
unchanged at 6. Back-annotated to
`Research/proposals/boost_ceiling_epoch_fork_closes_the_last_candidate_discriminator_20260808.md`.

**Why this matters more than the closure itself.** This is the *third consecutive* candidate
discriminator to die on an unmade definitional choice rather than on a measurement (EFE's argument
fork 08-07; the ceiling's 1/Ω_m vs Ω_m/Ω_b ambiguity 07-27; the epoch today). All three were
