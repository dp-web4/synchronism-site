## Key Findings
- `grid-n2-dispersion-is-at-threshold-reach-not-below-it-universality-does-not-cancel-and-one-cell-per-tick-is-unstable.md`:
  - **The grid's coefficient sits at today's reach.** With E² = p² + m² + η p⁴/M_Pl², the grid has |η| ≈ 0.03–0.14. The
    composition-agnostic Auger 2022 threshold bounds are η_γ ≳ −0.015 and η_had < 0.15, so the grid is not 10⁷ below
    reach.
  - **The bounds that exclude it are conditional.** Pure-proton bounds (Maccione 2009, Galaverni–Sigl 2008, Gagnon–Moore
    2004) exclude it by 1.5–5 orders. But each holds one sector Lorentz-invariant, and a universal subluminal grid switches
    off CMB photopion (min threshold 18–81 kT, against 5.1 kT Lorentz-invariant). That removes the cosmogenic photons the
    photon bounds assume. No self-consistent calculation exists.
  - **A universal n = 2 coefficient does not cancel.** Reactions feel η(1 − Σx³); only n = 0 cancels. Verified by exact
    solves to 7 digits.
  - **The sign depends on the time scheme and the species.** Continuous time is subluminal. Leapfrog at λ = 1 is
    superluminal off-axis. Wilson fermions have mixed sign by direction and carry a tree-level δc² = m/M_Pl (not
    (m/M_Pl)²).
  - **FUNDAMENTALS' "Planck tick, Planck spacing" means one cell per tick.** A face-neighbour 3D rule at that ratio is
    unstable in 78.7 % of its modes (×9.9 per tick). A 26-neighbour tensor stencil is marginally stable there and
    subluminal (η ∈ [−1/36, 0]). No archive simulation has run at the committed ratio (they use λ = 0.1 or 0.5).
