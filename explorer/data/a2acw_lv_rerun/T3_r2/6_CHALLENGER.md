# CHALLENGER, Closing Artifact

## HELD (survives)
- Detector integration window and phase change what a classical, non-invasively sampled periodic signal looks like. The CRT illustrates this correctly.
- The boxcar-average contrast law C(T) = |sinc(ωT/2)| is correct. It is a standard filter fact and marks the boundary of the analogy, not a test of it.
- Primary's concessions are retained: label (A), interpretive analogy only. Physical measurement involves a persistent system-detector coupling, so "no disturbance" is withdrawn. "No observer effect" shrinks to "no consciousness-dependent effect," which remains untested.

## NARROWED (must be scoped)
- Wave/particle duality: reduces to "smooth (integrated) vs. discrete (single-shot) readout." It gives no interference, because integration yields Σ|aᵢ|² and not |Σaᵢ|². Fringes need a phase-carrying amplitude that the CRT lacks.
- "No observer effect": scope to non-conscious operational disturbance only. Whether the "universal consciousness" witness correlates settings with phase (P2) was never answered. If it does, the claim fails in spirit.
- Decoherence parallel: a label only. The text has no pointer basis, no system-environment coupling, and no master equation.
- "Raster uncertainty": epistemic and removable on a CRT, so it cannot stand in for quantum indeterminacy.
- Section 5.1 title and "Revolutionary Implications" block: re-scope the CRT to "an example of readout-dependent appearance in a classical, non-invasively sampled signal."
- Planck-scale "Technical Implementation" table: a label table with no equations. It must not be cited as quantitative grounding, and Lorentz-invariance and dispersion bounds remain unaddressed.

## FAILED (unsupported / overreach)
- The analogy-to-ontology step: "I don't know the phase" (epistemic) does not become "reality is always definite" (ontic). Claims that superposition "doesn't exist" and that there is "no quantum mystery" fail.
- Raster entanglement and "Bell's theorem makes perfect sense": a pre-synchronized deterministic local model is bounded by |S| ≤ 2, while observed values are S ≈ 2.4-2.8. No escape route (nonlocality, superdeterminism, anti-realism) was selected, and each contradicts the text.
- Noncontextual "one phase fixes all outcomes": ruled out by Kochen-Specker for d ≥ 3 without any locality assumption.
- "Nothing changes" as a law of nature (macrorealism): already violated in Leggett-Garg tests. Primary retracted it for physical measurement.
- "Observation collapse" bullet: a real CRT dot keeps moving, and repeatability requires an unspecified locking mechanism.
- Schrödinger's cat "dissolved" and the "Applications explained" lists (cryptography, teleportation, QEC, quantum computing): no mechanism was given.
- "Perfectly demonstrates" and "Synchronism goes further than decoherence": no formal linkage.

## WOULD SETTLE (decisive evidence/test)
- An explicit state object with order-dependent (non-commuting) readout operators and a Born-like probability rule, from which Malus's law, CHSH = 2√2, and V² + D² ≤ 1 are derived.
- A single-system outcome function f(φ, θ) with the dropped assumption (locality, realism, or measurement independence) named, and a physical channel specified if P2 is dropped.
- A repeatability mechanism, stated as a coupling and explicitly classified as observer effect or not.
- A Lorentz-compatible account of the universal tick that is consistent with photon-timing and birefringence bounds.
- A prediction that differs from standard QM in a testable regime. Without one, the status is "heuristic vocabulary with zero extra empirical content."

HEADLINE: FAILED
