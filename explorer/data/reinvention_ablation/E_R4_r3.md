**Verdict:** The central claim, that Lorentz violation is suppressed by powers of E/E_Planck and the grid is therefore unobservable, is not established and is probably wrong as stated.

**Main problem: the suppression argument**
- A free-field lattice dispersion relation (ω ∼ sin(ka)/a) does give corrections of order (ka)², so the tree-level estimate looks reassuring. The program appears to rest on this.
- In an interacting theory this does not survive. Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia and Vucetich (2004) showed that radiative corrections from a Planck-scale cutoff that breaks Lorentz symmetry generically feed into dimension-4 operators at O(1), not O(E/E_P). Examples are species-dependent limiting speeds and a preferred time direction coupled to the matter fields.
- Absolute global time is an explicit preferred frame. Nothing in the construction protects against these terms.
- Observed bounds on dimension-4 Lorentz violation are around 10⁻¹⁵ to 10⁻²⁷, depending on the sector. Without a symmetry or an RG fixed-point mechanism, the program faces a severe fine-tuning problem of 15 to 20+ orders of magnitude.
- "Emergent Lorentz invariance" in condensed-matter analogues relies on exactly such a mechanism (an IR fixed point, supersymmetry, or tuning). The program needs to show which one it uses.

**Observability**
- Even Planck-suppressed operators are testable. Linear (dimension-5) corrections are already constrained beyond E_P by GRB time-of-flight and polarization, and by Crab and UHECR data. A cubic lattice with a preferred frame also predicts specific rotation-violating dimension-6 terms.
- So "unobservable at accessible energies" is an empirical claim that is already under pressure, not a safe retreat.

**Other gaps**
- "Intent patterns" are never defined. There is no action, Hamiltonian, or update rule, so no dispersion relation, interaction vertex, or RG flow can be computed.
- Lattice fermions face Nielsen–Ninomiya doubling, and chiral matter is hard to realize.
- There is no route to diffeomorphism invariance or GR from a fixed cubic grid with absolute time, and no account of quantum mechanics (unitarity, entanglement, Bell correlations).
- Boosted observers would see sub-Planckian cells, so the statement of what is "Planck-length" is frame-dependent and needs care.

**Strengths**
- The program commits to a concrete discrete substrate and a definite preferred frame, which makes it falsifiable in principle.
- It is honest that Lorentz invariance is emergent rather than fundamental.

**What to do next**
1. Write down a precise microscopic dynamics, with an explicit action or update rule and symmetries.
2. Compute the one-loop renormalization of the dimension-4 Lorentz-violating coefficients for the stated dynamics. Either show they flow to zero or are protected, or admit the tuning.
3. Derive the dimension-5 and dimension-6 coefficients and compare them with GRB, Crab, UHECR, and clock-comparison bounds, replacing "unobservable" with quantitative predictions.
4. Show how fermions, gauge fields, and gravity arise, and how the cubic anisotropy is suppressed.
5. Address the tuning problem directly, for example with a Chadha–Nielsen-type argument, and test it numerically in a toy lattice model.

Until step 2 is done, the Lorentz-emergence claim is an assertion rather than a result.
