## Claim under test
RG: ∇·[ε(ρ)∇φ] = 4πGρ, ε = ε₀ + (1−ε₀)·½[1+tanh(arg)], so ε ∈ [ε₀, 1].
(a) Where ε is uniform (system + ambient both well below the knee), φ = φ_N/ε₀ EXACTLY, any geometry.
(b) In spherical symmetry, Gauss gives g(r) = g_N(r)/ε(r) ≤ g_N/ε₀ exactly.
So 1/ε₀ is the ceiling on the dynamical boost of any spherical or fully-below-knee system, and
(c) in that regime an external field cannot change internal dynamics (uniform medium → no refraction, linear → no MOND-like EFE).
