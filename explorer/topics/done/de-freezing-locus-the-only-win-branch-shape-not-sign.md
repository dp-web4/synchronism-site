# Topic: Is the DE sector's freezing locus distinguishable from generic freezing dark energy at DR3 precision?

## Question
The substituted dark-energy background runs w from −2γ (past) to −1 (future), so in the (w₀, w_a) plane it traces a
**one-parameter curve** (γ). Generic freezing quintessence (and MP-Cardassian with free q, n) fills a two-dimensional region.
If DESI DR3 moved toward freezing (w₀ > −1, w_a > 0), would a fit *on the γ locus* be preferred over ΛCDM and over
w₀w_aCDM by a pre-statable margin? Or does the locus sit so close to generic freezing models (e.g. Linder's calibrated
freezing band, or a Cardassian member) that a detection would not select it?

## Context
A visitor researcher persona (2026-10-08, Pass 4) argued that "tie at ΛCDM / kill on thawing or crossing" leaves out the one
branch where the framework's specific *shape*, not just its sign, could matter. They called a freezing detection on the
locus "weak positive evidence". The nesting analysis (2026-09-27) says DE is a superset of ΛCDM whose one win branch
(freezing) is Cardassian prior art. This topic asks whether "Cardassian prior art" applies to the *exact locus*. In the
low-density limit the framework departs from MP-Cardassian (ρ_DE → ρ_crit/γ, a Λ-like future; /dark-energy, 2026-09-14).

## Why It Matters
It is the last candidate in the registry for a branch that could move Bucket 0, or the last one to close. Either answer
should be registered **before DR3**. DR2 currently prefers thawing, so the honest prior on a freezing reversal is low.
The registration costs nothing, and without it any later freezing result would be read post-hoc.
