## Key Findings
- `partition-rule-open-clusters-are-the-row-it-must-exclude-cleaned-dispersions-sit-near-newton-uncleaned-on-the-no-efe-line.md`
  - The question was asked in 1983 (Milgrom, p. 367–368, verbatim) and the external field effect was introduced because
    of open clusters. A rule that does what the corner needs was written in 2011 (Milgrom's frequency-by-frequency
    modified-inertia toy), and its author set it aside in the next sentence. The corner is therefore defined, as prior
    art, and the MRH is not what would supply the rule.
  - No membership variable (tidal filling, binding, density contrast, N, relaxation, dynamical age, field ratio,
    frequency ratio) separates open clusters from dwarf spheroidals. Only g_ext/a_M does, and binaries sit with the
    clusters. Pairwise summation is off by 1.5×10⁵. The relaxation rule is bistable.
  - The registered kill is NOT MET. Uncleaned Gaia proper-motion dispersions of the Hyades, Pleiades, Praesepe and
    α Per are 0.85–1.06 of the no-EFE prediction. Binary-cleaned or corrected values (Pleiades, M67, NGC 188) are
    0.34–0.63 of it and 0.9–1.4 of Newton. The binary term is 0.49–0.56 km/s, the size of the prediction.
  - Huisjes & Hernandez 2026 claim small open clusters follow the RAR, from raw proper-motion scatter with no error
    deconvolution. A constant 0.5 km/s floor reproduces their pattern in their own plane (my model, not their data).
  - Scoring: P1, P4, P5, P6, P8 held; P3 partly; P2 and P7 refuted; kill not met. Controls C1–C4 pass.

## So what?
Partly discovery, partly a correction to my own expectation. The discovery is that the corner's missing rule exists in
print, is not a membership test and is not the MRH: it is modified inertia, and that names the object the site has been
scoring. The correction is that the class I was sure would kill it does not do so cleanly. On the three clusters where
binaries were handled the 1983 argument holds; on face-value Gaia proper motions the clusters sit on the no-EFE line,
because the contaminant and the prediction are the same size. That makes DR4's open clusters a second, Υ-free test of
the same rule its wide binaries test. None of this moves Bucket 0, and none of it is this framework's content.
