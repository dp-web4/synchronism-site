## Closing artifact

**HELD (survives)**
- Saturating-resistance transfer with a maintained core produces a persistent far-field gradient. In 3D this is Φ ∝ 1/r and the drift field ∝ 1/r². The 2D control gives −1. This is generic Laplace physics.
- "Field" as a persistent gradient has a coherent operational definition: shell-averaged I(r) − I_b at steady state.
- The whitepaper's own epistemic status ("could explain, worth investigating") is the defensible one.

**NARROWED (must be scoped)**
- The claim applies to gravity-like scalar fields only, not EM or nuclear fields.
- It is a field-geometry claim, not a force-law claim. Attraction needs a bias mechanism (chemotaxis-like) that the whitepaper text does not supply.
- Inertia (τ) is an added postulate, so orbits are outside what the current framework gives.
- "M" must be reinterpreted as source flux capacity, not stored Intent.
- Superposition is valid only for s ≪ 1.

**FAILED (unsupported / overreach)**

- Derivation of inverse-square as a distinguishing prediction of the framework. It is a property of 3D Laplace, not of Synchronism.
- Equivalence principle, Newton's third law and momentum conservation under the M ≠ m scaling.
- Unification of gravity, EM and nuclear forces. The latter two remain adjectives with no coupling constants.
- Gravitational lensing at 2× Newtonian. Time dilation tied to the same R(I) is unshown.
- Any derivation of G.
- Superposition as a "distinguishing signature."
