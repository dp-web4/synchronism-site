## Closing artifact

**HELD (survives)**
- Weak claim: known life and conventional computation operate in ~250-400 K, explained by the substrate-independent constraint exp(−E_b/kT) (memory persistence) against Arrhenius update rates.
- The ratio form (kT relative to E_mem and E_act) as a correct textbook scaling. The window is set by dimensionless ratios, not by a number of kelvin.
- Gradients (ΔT) supply the free energy and ambient T selects which patterns persist. These are separate roles.
- The autonomy accounting rule (thermal contact within 10%, <10% of power spent on holding T, any free-energy flux allowed, no pre-ordered states from a different-T subsystem) as a usable form.

**NARROWED (must be scoped)**
- "Universal law" becomes a contingent band set by α, m_e, and the chosen carrier E_b.
- The strong claim applies only to autonomous systems whose information carriers have independently measured E_b in 0.1-1 eV, and it predicts nothing inside 100-600 K. The falsifier is such a system sustained outside that range.
- P1 is reduced to a budget-dependent T*/T_c curve that needs finite-size scaling and a same-metric null model. It is not a standalone prediction.
- The 150-450 K band is a calibration, not a derivation.

**FAILED (unsupported / overreach)**
- "Foundational" status for Temperature: T is an emergent ensemble statistic.
- T ≡ ⟨V²⟩ as written: it lacks mass and k_B, so it has no unit in kelvin, and it cannot be negative, which conflicts with §5.20.
- Silicon-AI convergence as independent evidence: it is a selection effect plus engineering and cooling economics, since Landauer favors colder operation.
- The "0.00001% of cosmic range" figure, and the claim that 300 K is required for consciousness or intelligence.
- P1's novelty: the null was a straw man, and the free repair budget and metric bias can absorb the result.
- The Intent model's commitment: no c, no plateau width, and no parameter mapping exist, so Synchronism adds nothing beyond textbook scaling here.
- The strong claim's practical empirical status: its only live tests are unobservable.

**WOULD SETTLE (decisive evidence/test)**
- A pre-run Intent-model prediction of c = kT*/E_b and the plateau width from its own parameters, which a same-metric Ising/Hopfield null then fails to reproduce under finite-size scaling.
- A pre-registered rule for measuring E_b independently of observed T.
- An autonomous, adaptive, self-repairing system with in-scope carrier E_b sustained below ~100 K or above ~600 K (Titan-class or hot-surface chemistry). That would falsify the strong claim.
- A simulation with independent T and E_b in which the peak does not track E_b. That would falsify even the ratio form of the Synchronism-specific claim.
