## The Problem in Detail

### What the Site Says (`/two-reframes`)

> "No information travels between them. They were synchronized from the start... their cycles were correlated from creation."

This is the CRT/"raster entanglement" model: two screens, one source, correlation established at creation. Correlation without communication.

This is exactly Bell's framework for a **local hidden variable model**:
- Particles carry hidden variables (the phase at creation)
- Each detector reads its local particle's variable
- Correlation arises from the shared creation event

Bell (1964) proved: any LHV model satisfies |CHSH S| ≤ 2.
Experiments: Aspect (1982), Hensen/Shalm/Giustina (2015, loophole-free) measure S ≈ 2.828.
The CRT model as written gives S ≈ 1.43 (verified in Session #230).

The page acknowledges "not yet mathematically formalized to reproduce all of standard QM's quantitative predictions." This framing treats the failure as an incompleteness problem ("not yet formalized"). It is not. Bell's theorem is a **proven impossibility result**: no LHV model can reproduce Bell violations, regardless of formalization. The caveat understates the problem by a large margin.

### What the Archive Has

**Session #230** (January 6, 2026) performed rigorous Bell analysis:
- Confirmed the instrument effect model gives S ≈ 1.43 (classical bound, not QM value)
- Identified this as mathematically guaranteed — not fixable by refinement
- Documented the conceptual options: non-local instruments, superdeterminism, retrocausality, participatory measurement, or **intent field**

**Clarification_Bell_Measurement_Resonance.md** (January 6, 2026) resolved it:
- Entangled particles in Synchronism are **one oscillatory pattern in the intent field** — not two things coordinating
- Analogy: a vibrating string has two ends; asking "how do the ends coordinate?" misses the point
- Measurement is **resonant interaction**, not passive readout of hidden variables
- The correlations follow from **phase geometry** of the single pattern, not from local properties of "particles"
- Bell violations are expected, not mysterious: Bell bounds what's possible for two separate things; there is no "two" here

This is the correct Synchronism answer to Bell's theorem. It is **not** an LHV model. It requires a non-local ontology (the intent field spans both measurement points), which is a coherent response to Bell.
