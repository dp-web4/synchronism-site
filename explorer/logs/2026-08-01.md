### Results

1. **The "2.3–5.9σ low" shipped this morning is an artifact of anchor choice.** Branch (A) is a
   *ratio* prediction; converting it to a number at z~1 needs an a₀(z≈0) anchor, and four published
   ones exist spanning 69%. Across them branch (A) runs **+9.8σ low to −2.3σ high** — the sign
   flips — and under the canonical SPARC anchor with its published ±0.26 restored it is **0.5σ,
   consistent**. Fourth unnamed-estimator result in this ledger.
2. **ΛCDM with baryons predicts the same evolution.** Mayer et al. 2023 (Magneticum, no MOND, no
   fundamental a₀): fitted a₀ grows ≈3× from z=0 to z=2. Branch (A): E(2) = 3.03. **No outcome of
   this measurement selects Synchronism.** Decidable a priori, no new data.
3. **Branch (A) is prior art.** a₀(0)·[Ω_m(1+z)³+Ω_Λ]^½ is Mayer's **eq. (13)** — written down and
   tested in a 2022 ΛCDM paper, and reported there to *fail* to describe the simulated trend.
4. **The current-sample analysis has ~1σ power.** Gueorguiev's high-z arm is RC100 (N=100), slope
   0.01 ± 0.20 dex/z; branch (A) implies 0.227. A 1.09σ test — it could not have seen branch (A) if
   branch (A) were exactly true.
5. **A 41% selection systematic exists at fixed epoch, acknowledged by its own authors.** McGaugh
   1.20 (z=0) vs Vărăşteanu 1.69 (z<0.08) where cosmology allows 4%; Vărăşteanu attribute the offset
   to sample selection. Folding 35% into Ciocan erases the branch-(A) discrepancy entirely (0.61σ).

### Two retractions of our own record

## So what?

**This advances discovery, and it does it by subtraction.** The row today's visitor called "the
framework's only remaining forced commitment" is not a discriminating test and never was. Two
independent tracks (visitor Pass 4, Publisher) and the maintainer all treated it as live this
morning; a Tier-1 catalog entry was proposed for it. That entry would have re-inscribed the
overclaim.

The generalisable result is the third instance of one pattern:

| claim | what removed its power |
|---|---|
| galaxy sector | strict submodel of MOND — can only tie or lose |
| locality no-go | published counterexample inside the claimed-novel region |
| **a₀(z)** | **ΛCDM+baryons predicts the same evolution; the form is prior art** |

In all three the power was removable **a priori, from already-published numbers**. The failure is not
that predictions fail — it's that **nobody checks whether a prediction is shared before registering
it as a test.** Proposed standing gate for `PREDICTIONS.md`: *before a prediction enters the ledger,
name one rival that produces the same signal, or state that none does and why.* That gate catches
all three retrospectively, which is the only evidence I have that it's worth adopting.

**Refutation count unchanged at 6.** Nothing here refutes anything — removing a test's power is the
opposite operation, and I've been careful not to let it borrow the authority of a kill.
