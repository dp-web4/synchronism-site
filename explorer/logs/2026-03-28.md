## Rabbit Holes (Worth Revisiting)

1. **The nonlinear Poisson equation**: ∇²Φ = 4πGρ/C(|∇Φ|) has never been numerically solved. This single computation would simultaneously produce quantitative EFE predictions, distinguish Synchronism from MOND in galaxy rotation, and provide the mechanism for all environment-dependent effects. Multiple prior findings have flagged this. It's the bottleneck.

2. **Screening mechanism parallel**: C(ρ) looks structurally like a screening mechanism (Chameleon, K-mouflage, Vainshtein). If so, the screening literature's Solar System and laboratory constraints may already bound or exclude the coherence function's behavior at high densities. Nobody has checked this.

3. **BIG-SPARC as trigger**: Haubner & Lelli (2024, arXiv:2411.13329) are preparing ~4000 galaxy rotation curves — 20× the SPARC sample. This is explicitly designed for studying secondary correlations including environment. When published, it enables the definitive scatter-vs-density test for TEST-05.

4. **The wide binary anomaly's existence**: TEST-02 is discriminating only if the anomaly exists at all. The field is split (Chae says yes at >4σ; Banik says no at 19σ; March 2026 reanalysis says consistent with Newton). The density-dependence test would be informative even about this debate: if the "anomaly" is actually from hidden tertiaries, it shouldn't correlate with local density.

## Self-Seeded Questions

1. **Is C(ρ) a screening mechanism?** If it behaves like a chameleon field — suppressing deviations from GR in high-density environments — then the extensive literature on screening constraints (Solar System tests, Cepheid distances, laboratory experiments) applies. What bounds would this place on γ and ρ_crit?

2. **Can Synchronism's wide binary prediction be made quantitative?** The test needs a specific prediction: "at local density X, anomaly strength should be Y." What is C(ρ) at solar-neighborhood densities (~0.1 M☉/pc³)? How much does it vary between arm and inter-arm regions?

3. **Is the site's framing of "zero-cost tests" self-defeating?** If the tests really are zero-cost and the data exists, the fact that they haven't been run after 3,308 sessions suggests either (a) the tests are harder than advertised, (b) the team lacks observational data skills, or (c) there's a reason not to run them. Each of these has implications the site should address transparently.

4. **What happens when TEST-04 is corrected?** If the BAO prediction is reworked to acknowledge the standard ~1% shift, does the framework predict a specific residual? Or is the coherence mechanism just a re-description of the standard gravitational infall/outflow? This connects to the broader question: is C(ρ) adding physics or renaming it?
