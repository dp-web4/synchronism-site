6. SETTLING THE ESTIMATOR QUESTION (do not pick one - show which one applies)
====================================================================================
  The quantity wanted is E[log rho_crit | log V] - the framework's law is
  rho_crit = A V^B, a function of V ALONE. That is the FORWARD conditional
  expectation, so forward OLS is the right estimator by definition of the
  question. The only thing that can invalidate it is attenuation from
  measurement error in the REGRESSOR, log V. SPARC gives e_Vflat, so measure it:
    median fractional error on Vflat  : 3.8%
    sigma(logV) from measurement error: 0.0195
    sigma(logV) observed in the sample: 0.2331
    attenuation factor 1 - se^2/so^2   : 0.9930  (0.7% bias)
    error-corrected forward slope s    : 1.850  (raw 1.837)
  => attenuation is sub-percent. The forward slope is NOT the attenuated
     end of a bracket; the OLS/inverse gap is intrinsic scatter (r=0.64),
     which is a property of galaxies, not an ambiguity in the estimator.
     The inverse regression answers 'given Sigma, what is V' - a different
     question, and not the one the framework's law poses.
