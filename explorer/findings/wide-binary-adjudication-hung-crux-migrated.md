## The Mid-2026 Literature Map

### Pro-anomaly camp (Chae / Hernandez)
- **Chae et al. 2026** (arXiv:2601.21728; Chae, Lee, Hernandez, Orlov, Lim, Turnshek,
  Lee): 36 binaries <150 pc with RV uncertainty <100 m/s, ruwe + CMD + multi-epoch RV +
  speckle-interferometry contamination vetting. Result: Γ=0.102±0.02 →
  **γ_boost = 1.60 (+0.17/−0.14), inconsistent with Newton at 4.9σ** in the range
  g_N ∈ 10⁻¹¹–10⁻⁹ m/s². Four binaries exceed Newtonian escape velocity
  (1 < v_obs/v_escN ≤ 1.2). This is the strongest anomaly claim ever published in this
  literature — and note its central value **exceeds the AQUAL/QUMOND+EFE expectation
  (~1.4×)**, so even the anomaly branch carries an internal amplitude tension vs MOND.
- **Chae 2025** (ApJ, arXiv:2502.09373): the Bayesian 3D-orbit methodology paper behind
  the above; Hernandez (independently, e.g. arXiv:2410.17178) continues to report ~40%
  boosts at g_N ≲ 10⁻¹⁰ m/s².
- **Hernandez, Chae & Aguayo-Ortiz 2024** (MNRAS 533:729): the camp's critique of the
  null analyses — Banik et al. 2024 compared noise-free models to noisy data (biases
  toward lower inferred gravity) and skipped the high-acceleration internal-calibration
  region; claims the "16–19σ Newtonian preference" becomes statistically uncertain under
  proper error propagation. Partially unanswered in print.

### Pro-Newton camp (Banik / Pittordis / Sutherland, now + El-Badry)
- **Banik et al. 2024** (MNRAS 527:4573): Newtonian preferred at ~16σ — the result the
  site already cites.
- **Pittordis, Sutherland & Shepherd 2025** (arXiv:2504.07569): realistic triple-population
  mixture modelling, Gaia DR3 + FLAME masses. **Newton still favoured over MOND, but the
  revised version concedes Δχ² is "somewhat reduced"** — the camp's own confidence
  interval moved toward the middle.
- **Cookson, Banik, El-Badry, Sutherland, Penoyre, Pittordis & Clarke 2026**
  (arXiv:2602.24035): a "quality framework" (sample purity, astrometric filtering,
  contamination mitigation, uncertainty propagation) applied as meta-analysis + new
  high-purity ≤130 pc, 1–30 kAU sample. Claims the velocity distribution is Newtonian
  at all separations, no 20% MOND enhancement, **Newton up to 1500× more likely**, and
  the trend claim: anomaly signals shrink as rigor rises. El-Badry joining matters —
  he wrote the canonical hidden-companion contamination papers.

### Independent third parties — the new and decisive-feeling development
- **Saad & Ting 2026** (arXiv:2603.11015): reanalysis of **Chae et al. 2026's own 36
  systems**. Hierarchical Bayesian model fitting 3D orbital elements with the semi-major
  axis as a free parameter → **γ = 1.12 (+0.27/−0.22), Newton-consistent at ~0.4σ**.
  Same data, Chae's geometric-deprojection assumption swapped back in → γ = 1.56,
  reproducing Chae. **The entire 4.9σ anomaly lives in one modeling choice.** No Chae
  rebuttal in print as of 2026-06-12.
- **Saglia et al. 2025** (A&A 699, aa55115-25; HARPS RVs): 31 of 32 wide binaries
  explainable by bound Newtonian orbits; high-quality RVs essential for purging
  multiples. (Chae 2025b reanalyzed the same 32 and reports a *tentative* anomaly —
  the camps even dispute each other's calibration samples.)
- **Pasquini et al. 2026** (arXiv:2602.04661; VLT-ESPRESSO): 3 of 12 clean systems
  >13 kAU have velocity differences with **no viable bound Newtonian orbit**, hidden
  companions ruled out — but the authors attribute these to tidal/encounter disruption
  and warn "utmost caution must be applied when studying wide binaries as isolated
  systems." Anomalous *velocities* are real across camps; the dispute is what they mean.

## The Two Genuinely New Things (Beyond "Still Disputed")

**1. The crux migrated, and the site's framing is one generation stale.** The site (and
the 2026-06-12 maintainer alert) frames the dispute as Chae-vs-Banik = whose *sample
cuts* are right. As of March 2026 the sharpest version is **Chae-vs-Saad&Ting on the
same 36 systems**: not selection, not contamination — both sides accept the vetted
sample — but the **prior over 3D geometry** (geometric deprojection of projected
separation vs hierarchical fit of semi-major axis). This is a *narrower and more
decidable* question than the 2023-24 fight: it can in principle be settled by
simulation (inject Newtonian + MOND mock binaries, ask which inference recovers truth)
without any new data. That is the specific thing to watch — not "Gaia DR4."

**2. A mirror of the loop's own central lesson.** Saad & Ting's demonstration — same
data, γ flips from 1.56 (5σ "discovery") to 1.12 (null) under a different modeling
assumption — is the wide-binary field's version of what this project keeps finding
internally: **a fitted parameter absorbing a modeling assumption and presenting as a
measurement** (cf. γ=2/√N_corr absorbing N_corr; the A-from-Jeans 0.0294 outliving its
computation; A2ACW's 1.4% survival being a property of the filter, not the corpus).
The same epistemic failure mode the site documents in its own program is currently
operating in the *external* literature its last kill branch depends on. Worth one
sentence on the site: the adjudication TEST-02 waits on is itself a
model-dependence dispute of exactly the kind the honest-assessment catalogs.

## Asymmetric Stakes, Stated Plainly

This channel is **no-win for the framework** (known since 2026-06-05, worth restating

null camp prevails, C(ρ) survives degenerately with Newton and the framework's last

empirical program — the adjudication decides the epitaph, not the survival.

   RV+speckle samples** → record degenerate survival, close TEST-02 with no points.
5. **Gaia DR4** (expanded epoch astrometry) — relevant but secondary to 1–3; the
   modeling crux must be settled or DR4 inherits it.
