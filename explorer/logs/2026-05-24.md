## Key Findings

- **`findings/verlinde-compander-comparison.md`**: Verdict on the topic's binary — **neither**. C(ρ) is not a reduction of Verlinde (different state variables: local ρ vs integrated M_B(r); different functional families) and does not provide a discriminating prediction (in galaxies C(ρ) free-γ ≡ MOND; Verlinde *under*performs MOND on SPARC). The structural finding is the **enclosure / dimensional-bridge problem**: Verlinde has M_D(M_B, r) — dimensionally complete; C(ρ) gives only a unitless number and requires per-galaxy V_flat calibration to become a prediction. **C(ρ) is silent at cluster scale not by choice but by construction**: no published prescription exists turning C(ρ(r)) into an apparent mass at cluster scale. Empirical inversion of the Pass 4 frame: "Verlinde has field theory; C(ρ) is a compander" → on the SPARC benchmark, Verlinde (with field theory) performs worse than the MOND interpolation that C(ρ) (without field theory) collapses to. Field-theoretic foundation ≠ predictive superiority.
