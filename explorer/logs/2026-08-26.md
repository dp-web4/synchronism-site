**Four results, all converged (<2% over resolution 200×220 → 300×340 and box 400 → 1500 kpc).**

1. **`L2 ≡ L3` is false for a disc.** Max L2/L3 = **5.89** in g (139% in v). Mechanism: Gauss's law
   sets the field by the permittivity averaged over the *enclosing sphere*, and a thin disc is
   measure-zero on its own sphere — at r = 8 kpc, ⟨C⟩ = 0.148 while C(midplane) = 0.923.
   Closed form: `max(L2/L3) → 1/C_min = B_max`; measured 3.178 against 1/Ω_m = 3.175.
   **The maximum error of the site's substitution is exactly its boost ceiling.**
2. **The variational extra force** `−∇[C′(ρ)|∇Φ|²/8πG]` — the gravitational copy of the
   Korteweg–Helmholtz electrostriction term — is **0.72× gravity radially** and up to
   **164× K_z vertically**, in a thin shell at the height where ρ crosses ρ_c. Generic across
   exponents (245× at q=1, 5.2× at q=2, 10× for the framework's own tanh form).
3. **Without it, Newton's third law fails by 3.6× the pair's own mutual force** (control C=1 gives
   0.001). The analytic identity `−(1/8πG)∫|∇Φ|²∇C` reproduces it to 0.4%; the striction term
   cancels it to 0.0%. Isolated pair self-accelerates at **0.047 a₀**.
4. **A new a-priori closure.** ρ_crit scan: the omitted vertical force drops below K_z **only when
   the knee sits inside R ≲ 3 kpc — inside the radius where no boost is needed.** So any
   reformulation that makes the knee reachable (the repair the queue is contemplating) switches on
   a vertical force 1–3 dex larger than the vertical gravity. Needs no data.
